# NEXAH Code of Conduct

NEXAH welcomes careful disagreement, interdisciplinary perspectives, and
questions that expose uncertainty. Participation should preserve the same
conditions the project asks of its knowledge artifacts: openness, boundaries,
traceability, humility, and room for correction.

## Expected conduct

- Treat contributors and readers with respect and patience.
- Critique claims, methods, and artifacts rather than people.
- State uncertainty and conflicts of interest when they matter.
- Credit sources and contributions accurately.
- Make space for different levels of experience and different perspectives.
- Respect privacy, consent, and declared project boundaries.
- Correct mistakes without erasing the history needed to understand them.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate
misrepresentation, plagiarism, exposure of private information, and repeated
disruption of good-faith collaboration are not acceptable.

## Scope and response

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, reviews, and other spaces
represented as part of NEXAH. Concerns may be reported privately to the
repository maintainer through the maintainer's GitHub contact channel. Reports
should be handled with discretion, proportionality, and attention to the safety
of affected people.

Maintainers may clarify, edit, hide, reject, or restrict participation when
conduct materially violates these expectations. Project authority does not
extend beyond NEXAH spaces.
