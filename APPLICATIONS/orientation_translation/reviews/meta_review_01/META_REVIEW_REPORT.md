# WHAT THE FIRST THREE ORIENTATION PILOTS REVEALED
## Meta Review 01

## 1. Review question

This review asks how Orientation Translation behaved across the completed Photosynthesis, Cellular Respiration, and Mitosis sequences. It reviews source fixation, representation, local comparison, reader routes, explanation, reflection, neighborhood admission, stopping, rejection, and process-rule records.

It excludes biological comparison involving Pilot 03, new scientific claims, graph merging, Atlas construction, hypothesis formation, canonical vocabulary, method revision, OLS/Kernel/Registry/Architecture changes, Question Operator integration, and Library concepts.

## 2. Corpus

The frozen review baseline contains 89 Markdown files: 14 for the Pilot 01 sequence, 15 for Pilot 02, 15 for Pilot 03, 15 in Growth Study 01, 15 in Comparison Framework 01, and 15 in the completed Photosynthesis–Cellular Respiration Comparison 01. The latter is evidence about comparison behavior only.

All three pilots use one fixed English Wikipedia revision and the same analyst. This makes within-corpus procedural inspection possible but prevents source-type, domain, and analyst-independence claims.

## 3. What repeated

### Across all three pilots

- one source revision was fixed before construction;
- several bounded representations were separated without calling one complete;
- local comparison preserved differences and non-implications;
- multiple non-ranked reader routes were made explicit;
- evidence, editorial construction, unsupported implications, and uncertainty remained distinguishable;
- Reflection generated bounded unanswered questions;
- neighborhood edges, rejected expansions, and stopping decisions were inspectable;
- direct source support dominated admission;
- no reader benefit, scientific validation, or analyst independence was demonstrated.

### Across two pilots

P02 and P03 record exact reverse-edge status for every admitted edge and use asymmetric, question-bounded neighborhoods rather than P01's symmetric rings. P02 and P03 also state more explicitly that explanatory order must not become causal order.

### Apparent template recurrence

Shared filenames, OLS stage order, tables, status headings, and report sections arise from a shared procedure. They demonstrate documentation regularity, not independent empirical recurrence.

### Uncertain recurrence

Indirect support, source-scale effects, the necessity of each artifact, stopping adequacy, and the value of question-led routes remain uncertain.

## 4. What differed

Representation counts, reader-path counts, topology, source breadth, rejection granularity, bridge treatment, reverse auditing, stopping logic, and local modification needs all differed. These asymmetries are method evidence because the procedure preserved them rather than forcing one common graph. Their causes—subject, source, analyst, template maturation, or method—cannot yet be isolated.

## 5. What the artifacts did

Orientation Models, internal Comparison Matrices, Reflections, and Neighborhoods most consistently produced inspectable orientation. Source Records and Traceability preserved evidence boundaries. Reader Mode improved concision but has no measured reader benefit. Explain Mode exposed reasons and limits but risks narrative continuity and audit burden. Evaluation and Pilot Report partly duplicate one another; current evidence is insufficient to remove either.

## 6. What the Process Rules did

Nine rule behaviors were stable across three cases, particularly source fixation, staged observation, plural representation, non-ranked routes, Reflection separation, edge audit, editorial-bridge isolation, rejection recording, and explicit stopping. Exact reverse auditing and avoidance of forced shape are stable in P02/P03 but asymmetric in P01. P02 recorded two local modifications; P03 recorded one. Indirect-edge admission remains tested only once. No declared rule failed decisively.

## 7. What the method currently appears able to do

Across three completed cases, Orientation Translation repeatedly made the internal plurality of a fixed public source visible. It produced declared representations, question-led routes, local comparisons, explanation records, unanswered questions, and finite evidence-bounded neighborhoods while preserving unsupported inferences and stopping decisions.

The current corpus suggests that the procedure can preserve provenance, direction, local support, editorial judgment, and negative decisions under a single-source, single-analyst workflow. Comparison 01 further shows that two completed sequences can be compared without merging them, while returning no edge identity and no reciprocity where evidence is insufficient.

## 8. What the method cannot yet claim

It cannot claim generality beyond these case types; validated reader benefit; universal representational categories; universal Orientation Operators; Atlas readiness; Orientation Hypothesis readiness; domain-independent success; scientific correctness; independence from analyst judgment; stable behavior across source types; or reproducibility by another analyst.

## 9. First provisional characterization

Orientation Translation currently behaves as a source-bounded editorial procedure for separating representations, relations, boundaries, navigation routes, unanswered questions, and unsupported inferences within a single case. Its strongest demonstrated property is inspectability, not correctness, learning gain, or universality.

## 10. Next-stage recommendation

- **Comparison 02:** methodologically valuable because P03 differs from the metabolic pair, but it risks conflating case and method effects before reader value is known.
- **Pilot 04:** would broaden case count but repeat the same analyst/source pattern.
- **Reader testing:** directly tests the largest repeated unknown: whether routes and Explain Mode improve orientation.
- **Source-quality replication:** isolates source effects but does not test the public-facing value of the artifacts.
- **Method revision:** premature; no decisive rule failure is established.
- **Pause/freeze:** preserves evidence but leaves the central reader claim untested.

**Recommendation: reader testing.** Use the three frozen Reader/Baseline/Explain artifacts under a separately approved, predeclared protocol. This tests the method's claimed orientation function without changing the method or adding another case. Comparison 02 remains a later option.

## 11. Disposition

**METHOD REQUIRES LIMITED REVIEW BEFORE FURTHER USE**

The procedure is stable enough to inspect, but its public-facing value and analyst dependence remain untested. “Limited review” here means a separately governed reader-evaluation stage, not revision of the frozen method or artifacts.

**META REVIEW 01 COMPLETE — NO METHOD REVISION AUTHORIZED**
