## Consequential unresolved differences

1. Whether asymmetric neighborhoods result from subject structure or improved resistance to template shape.
2. Whether complete reverse auditing changes admissions or merely improves documentation.
3. Whether source breadth drives the number of representations and hidden questions.
4. Whether controlled local rule modification is reproducible across analysts.
5. Whether the method's heavy record structure produces reader value or only auditability.
