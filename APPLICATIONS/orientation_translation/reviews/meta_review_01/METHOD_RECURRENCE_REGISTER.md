## Required distinctions

The recurring biological presence of processes, inputs, outputs, spatial locations, or phases is not a method finding. Likewise, repeated tables and filenames show procedural standardization, not independent validation. The strongest defensible recurrence is that the procedure repeatedly preserves multiple representations, support boundaries, explicit routes, rejected implications, and a stopping decision within one fixed source case.
