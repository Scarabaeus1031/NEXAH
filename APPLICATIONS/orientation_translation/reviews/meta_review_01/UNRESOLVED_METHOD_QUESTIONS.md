# Unresolved Method Questions

## Source boundary

1. **How does the procedure behave with a primary, specialist, contested, or multi-source evidence body?** Origin: all three pilots used one tertiary Wikipedia revision. Exposed by: P01–P03. Cannot be answered because source type and analyst are constant. Requires: a separately governed source-quality replication.
2. **How sensitive are representations and stopping decisions to source revision drift?** Origin: fixed-revision safeguards. Exposed by: P01–P03. Cannot be answered because no pilot was rerun on another revision. Requires: versioned replication without modifying the originals.
