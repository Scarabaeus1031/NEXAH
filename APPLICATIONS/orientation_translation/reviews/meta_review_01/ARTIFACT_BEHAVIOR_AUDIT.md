# Artifact Behavior Audit

| Artifact class | Intended function | Actual behavior across cases | Risk / uncertainty | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Source Record | freeze identity, provenance, scope, limits | consistently fixes one revision and source boundary | one tertiary source type; source selection remains editorial | retain unchanged for further testing |
| Source Representation | describe source organization before translation | explicit in P02/P03; function partly distributed across P01 records | separate artifact's added value is not established | retain but monitor |
| Baseline | show what the un-oriented article does and does not provide | consistently exposes missing starting position, route separation, and consolidated boundaries | may prefigure later findings through analyst framing | retain but monitor |
| Observation | record source-visible material before representation | consistently supports later traceability and tension records | selection completeness and analyst agreement unknown | retain unchanged for further testing |
| Orientation Model | declare bounded representations and semantic limits | repeatedly produces the clearest account of plural views | representation boundaries are editorial and may be template-sensitive | retain unchanged for further testing |
| internal Comparison Matrix | compare local representations without cross-case claims | repeatedly exposes compatible but non-identical maps and warns against causality | can smooth differences into neat tables | retain but monitor |
| Orientation Map | organize question-led routes | creates inspectable alternatives in every pilot | route order may look scientific or optimal; no reader validation | retain but monitor |
| Reader | present routes without full audit detail | improves immediate legibility relative to full records | actual benefit, comprehension, and route choice unmeasured | revision candidate after further cases |
| Explain | expose reasons, evidence, limits, and editorial additions | makes provenance and prohibited implications visible | dense; can create narrative continuity stronger than source causality | retain but monitor |
| Evaluation | separate demonstrated, plausible, unknown, and prohibited | consistently resists overclaiming | repeated limitations may duplicate reports | apparently redundant but insufficient evidence |
| Traceability | connect products to observations, source regions, and decisions | provides audit path in all pilots | granularity and external reproducibility untested | retain unchanged for further testing |
| Pilot Report | synthesize result, limits, process behavior, disposition | becomes increasingly useful for cross-case audit | later reports are richer and not retrospectively equivalent | retain unchanged for further testing |
| Reflection | inspect what became visible and generate bounded unanswered questions | consistently creates the strongest post-translation methodological insight | may duplicate Evaluation and introduce analyst-led bridge ranking | retain unchanged for further testing |
| Neighborhood | admit a minimal directional local relation structure | preserves support, rejection, stopping, and local asymmetry | counts/topology can be mistaken for knowledge quality; P01 ring pressure | retain but monitor |
| Failure Mode Audit | test whether process or review violated boundaries | explicit in studies/comparison and P03 evaluation; not uniform as standalone pilot artifact | absence in earlier pilot packages prevents direct comparison | structurally unclear |

## Findings

The artifacts that most consistently produced new orientation records were the Orientation Model, internal Comparison Matrix, Reflection, and Neighborhood. Source Record and Traceability did not themselves create orientation, but made later claims inspectable. Reader Mode remains a plausible presentation layer rather than validated evidence of benefit. Evaluation and Pilot Report overlap, yet their different audit/synthesis functions have not been tested enough to merge or remove them.
