## Structural notes

- Artifact counts describe record shape, not quality.
- The increase from three to five reader paths is not a progression claim.
- Neighborhood sizes are products of local questions and stopping decisions, not comparable performance scores.
- Pilot 01's ring construction is structurally different from the asymmetric Pilot 02 and Pilot 03 neighborhoods.
- Pilot 01's missing complete reverse register cannot be inferred from later templates.
- All three cases separate reader routes from scientific pathways and graph edges, although the explicitness of that separation increases in later records.
