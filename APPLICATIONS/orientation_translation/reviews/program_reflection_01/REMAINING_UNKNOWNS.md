# Remaining Unknowns

Only unresolved problems already exposed by completed artifacts are listed.

## Reproducibility

- Can another analyst produce materially compatible classifications, routes, admissions, rejections, and stopping decisions from the same fixed source?
- Which differences would be acceptable editorial variation rather than failure?
- Does the Cartography audit survive a second independent study-to-map execution?

## Reader validation

- Do Reader Mode, Explain Mode, routes, neighborhoods, or maps improve human orientation?
- Do readers preserve evidence boundaries or infer causality from sequence, proximity, and visual continuity?
- Does the Museum Edition clarify entry or merely increase aesthetic engagement?

## Independent review

- Can support classes, identities, role correspondences, and non-edges be reviewed consistently by someone other than the producing analyst?
- How often do negative decisions survive independent review?

## Scaling

- Do neighborhoods remain inspectable at greater depth or with broader sources?
- Can comparison remain bounded across more than two neighborhoods?
- At what point do matrices, audits, and Explain artifacts create excessive review burden?
- No audited Atlas-scale integration has yet been demonstrated.

## Source and domain dependence

- All three pilots use fixed English Wikipedia revisions; behavior across primary literature, datasets, mixed media, or contested sources is unknown.
- The current cases do not isolate subject effects from source structure, template maturation, or analyst choices.
- Scientific fidelity has not been independently validated by domain experts.

## Repository and publication integration

- Which descriptive research artifacts deserve long-term navigation without being mistaken for canonical method?
- Some program artifacts remain untracked or newly integrated; long-term version and release policy is not established by the program.
- The relationship between research publications, Library curation, and public Are.na presentation is only partly exercised.

## Conceptual uncertainty

- The minimal necessary action set is unknown.
- The distinction between Reflection generating questions and merely recording them remains unresolved.
- Exact boundaries among representation, route, neighborhood, orientation space, map, and Atlas remain contextual.
- No evidence establishes a universal Orientation Translation architecture or cognitive mechanism.

These unknowns limit claims. They do not authorize a roadmap or redesign in this review.
