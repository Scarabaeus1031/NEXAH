## Ecology observed

The publications form an ecology because different artifacts own different responsibilities:

- source and observation artifacts preserve input evidence;
- models and matrices organize representation and comparison;
- Reader/Explain artifacts translate for use;
- evaluations and reflections inspect what the translation did;
- neighborhoods publish admitted and rejected local relations;
- comparisons preserve differences between frozen outputs;
- maps translate reviewed spaces visually;
- manifests and audits preserve publication fidelity.

No artifact replaces all others, and the repository has not demonstrated that every case needs every form.
