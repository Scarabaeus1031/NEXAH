# Final Disposition

## Decision

# RECURRING ORIENTATION FUNCTIONS

## Basis

The thirteen recurring editorial operations support distinguishable
artifact-level orientation functions across science, architecture,
documentation, repository navigation, comparison studies, and reader maps.
Most functions survive replacement of visual panels with prose, tables,
matrices, links, or routes.

The strongest recurring functions are:

- distinguishing representation types;
- comparing without collapsing identity;
- locating evidence, scope, scale, and authority;
- checking provenance and non-implications;
- preserving alternatives, asymmetry, and local stopping boundaries.

## Conservative boundary

This disposition does not establish that the operations are:

- universal;
- mandatory;
- cognitive or biological;
- a theory of reasoning;
- an OLS extension;
- canonical NEXAH Operators;
- a formal grammar;
- a complete framework;
- or demonstrably effective for readers.

`CANDIDATE ORIENTATION FRAMEWORK` is not selected because the corpus supplies
no validated necessity model, complete dependency architecture, stable syntax,
independent reproduction, or reader-effect evidence.
