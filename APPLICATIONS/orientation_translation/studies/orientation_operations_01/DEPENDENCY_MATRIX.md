# Dependency Matrix

## Dependency vocabulary

- **Definitional:** the dependent operation cannot be described coherently
  without the other operation in that instance.
- **Enabling:** the other operation makes the function possible in the observed
  artifact, but not necessarily in every artifact.
- **Strengthening:** the operation can occur alone, but the other makes its
  meaning or boundary more inspectable.
- **Independent in evidence:** no dependency is demonstrated by the corpus.

No category establishes a universal hierarchy.

## Per-operation assessment

| Operation | Observed dependencies | Type | Independent from | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| separate representations | none required | independent in evidence | color, icons, arrows, route choice | a single representation needs no separation operation |
| juxtapose bounded views | separated or at least identifiable views | enabling | sequence, traceability | textual comparison can substitute for spatial adjacency |
| sequence with declared relation | identifiable items plus a stated relation | definitional | juxtaposition, alternative routes | order alone is insufficient; relation meaning must be supplied |
| offer alternative routes | plurality of entry questions or possible paths | definitional | visual juxtaposition, color | a route can exist without comparison or return |
| compare by contrast | distinguishable objects and a comparison basis | definitional/enabling | route choice, stopping | physical adjacency is not required |
| qualify non-implications | a candidate claim, route, or relation to bound | definitional | traceability, alternate routes | can be expressed without a visual boundary |
| label evidence or status | an item or claim receiving status | definitional | scale, routes, sequence | traceability strengthens but does not define status |
| mark a boundary | a represented scope, region, claim, or authority | definitional | color, icons, explicit stop | border form is optional; a sentence can mark the boundary |
| declare scale and scope | a representation or relation with applicability | definitional | route plurality, visual layout | only conditionally relevant when scale/scope affects reading |
| preserve traceability | a source, decision, or prior artifact | definitional | juxtaposition, route choice, color | evidence labels strengthen verification but source lineage can exist without them |
| state a stopping condition | a bounded question or route plus local sufficiency | enabling | return/open question | scope and boundary marking usually strengthen the stop |
| preserve alternatives and asymmetry | plural routes, directions, roles, or unequal cases | definitional | color, icons, stopping | a single-case artifact has no asymmetry to preserve |
| return through an open question | a bounded current result and an unresolved continuation | enabling | visual sequence, traceability | can occur without a formal stop but is clearer after one |

## Observed support relations

| From | Supports | Evidence-supported reason | Not implied |
|---|---|---|---|
| separation | juxtaposition; comparison | identifiable objects can remain distinct while related | mandatory first step |
| evidence labeling | qualification; checking | status makes non-implications and limits easier to inspect | validation or truth |
| scope declaration | comparison; stopping | applicability bounds comparisons and local closure | a universal scale hierarchy |
| traceability | evidence checking; qualification | sources and decisions can be revisited | factual correctness |
| alternative preservation | route choice; asymmetry | plurality remains visible | equal value or equal support |
| boundary marking | stopping; transition | endpoints and crossings become locally legible | physical boundary or mechanism |
| stopping | open question/return | local closure can expose what remains unresolved | a mandatory cycle |

## Dependency finding

The corpus supports a sparse network of conditional support, not one ordered
pipeline. Different artifacts activate different subsets. No root operation,
universal prerequisite, or complete execution sequence is demonstrated.
