# Independence Assessment

## Question

Does each operation retain an identifiable orientation function when its visual
or textual realization changes?

## Tests

## Representation substitutions observed

- visual panels ↔ textual sections;
- arrows ↔ numbered routes ↔ prose order;
- color status ↔ explicit status vocabulary;
- spatial juxtaposition ↔ comparison matrix;
- visual border ↔ scope statement or prohibited implication;
- graphical path ↔ repository link sequence;
- icon category ↔ named responsibility or representation type.

These substitutions support independence from a single style. They do not
prove independence from language, editorial intent, or the broader NEXAH
practice.

## Independence finding

The thirteen functions are generally representation-independent at the level
of artifact design. Their meaning survives when the operation is re-expressed
in prose, matrices, routes, diagrams, links, or status tables. The evidence
does not demonstrate cultural, linguistic, cognitive, or authorial
independence.
