# Failure Mode Matrix

## Evidence rule

Failures are drawn from the previous review and its source audits. They are
artifact-level failure pressures, not measured failures inside readers.

| Operation absent or weak | Documented orientation failure pressure | Evidence | What is demonstrated | What is not demonstrated |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| separate representations | identity, role, or layer collapse | C-02, C-09–C-11, C-14 | reviewed artifacts encountered or guarded against collapse | every reader would collapse the items |
| juxtapose bounded views | isolated views or adjacency without declared comparison | C-01–C-05, C-17 | comparison requires more than proximity | visual juxtaposition is necessary for comparison |
| sequence with declared relation | order read as causality, necessity, or physical pathway | C-01, C-06–C-08, C-16 | the ambiguity is explicit and recurrent | a particular arrow causes a particular misreading |
| offer alternative routes | one editorial path appears mandatory or optimal | C-06–C-08, C-19 | artifacts explicitly reject ranking/uniqueness | multiple routes always improve orientation |

## Failure finding

The evidence links each operation to a recognizable failure pressure, but it
does not show a deterministic relationship between absence and reader failure.
The matrices demonstrate governance pressure and editorial risk, not cognition.
