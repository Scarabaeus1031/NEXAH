# Cartographic Form Register

These are historical working labels, not a canonical taxonomy.

| Form | Provisional definition | Recurring features | Examples | Limitations / overlap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sequence Map | A curated progression that locates a reader in an ordered body of material while allowing explicit alternatives or stops. | order, next step, branch, return, friction | Library Reader Journey Review; pilot Orientation Maps | A fixed causal flow does not qualify. Overlaps Reader-Path Map. |
| Field Map | A spatial field in which positions, regions, gradients, or regimes make relations inspectable. | regions, local/global placement, gradients, neighborhoods | Regime Atlas; Navigation Grammar | Scientific field plots alone are not Orientation Cartography. |
| Transition Map | A map centered on thresholds, passages, or changes between states. | boundary, admissible passage, direction, alternative transition | Regime Atlas transition/gate layer; Solar–Lunar–Carbon backbone/open edges | Route must not be read automatically as mechanism. The archived Gate Geometry placeholder is not usable evidence. |
| Comparative Map | A structure for comparing non-identical representations while keeping their scales and support separate. | aligned objects, asymmetry, outliers, non-equivalence | Planetary Chemistry Atlas 01; Photosynthesis–Respiration Comparison | Visual symmetry can launder unequal evidence. |
| Neighborhood Map | A bounded local representation of immediate and secondary relations. | focal representation, rings/depth, edge class, stopping boundary | Photosynthesis Neighborhood; Cellular Respiration Neighborhood | A neighborhood is not an ontology or global graph. |
| Layer Map | A map that distinguishes levels, responsibilities, or scales and their interfaces. | stacked layers, interfaces, authority/scale differences | Living Library; Orientation Laboratory | Can collapse into a system diagram if no reader movement exists. |
| Reader-Path Map | Alternative routes selected by reader purpose or context. | role, entry, next step, bridge, explanation, optional branch | Beginner/Builder/Research journeys; Pilot Orientation Maps | Editorial paths are not source relations. |
| Atlas Plate | A bounded comparative view designed for revisiting and relation across other plates. | named scope, multiple regions/views, legend, reference function | Regime Atlas; Planetary Chemistry Atlas; Solar–Lunar–Carbon maps | Book metadata alone does not prove that every page or Work functions as a map. |
| Architecture Map | Locates components, responsibilities, interfaces, and operational routes. | subsystem regions, interfaces, authority boundaries, entry points | Applications Navigation Map; Living Library | Dense dashboards and simple pipelines remain excluded. |

## Emerging but not recurrent enough

**Open-Territory Map** is strongly expressed by the Solar–Lunar–Carbon pair, where unknowns and non-admitted relations occupy meaningful space. Earlier artifacts contain unknowns and future paths, but the corpus inspected does not yet provide two independently audited examples. It remains an emerging publication device, not an admitted historical form.
