# Limitations

- The inspection is bounded and selective, not a complete visual inventory.
- Repository history supplies dates for tracked artifacts, not necessarily original creation dates.
- Many Library Works live primarily on Are.na; page-level content is not fully mirrored in the repository.
- A Work title containing “Map” or “Atlas” was not treated as proof of function.
- Visual inspection cannot validate underlying scientific claims.
- Several historical visuals mix current, historical, speculative, and promotional language.
- Textual paths and neighborhoods were admitted because cartographic function does not require geographic drawing; this broadens the form boundary and requires care.
- The Solar–Lunar–Carbon Evidence Map and Museum Edition are two publication layers of one reviewed space, not two independent replications.
- Draft-generation history is partial: only available retained generations are recorded.
- Cartographic criteria are provisional and have not been tested by independent reviewers.
- No reader study demonstrates that any artifact improves orientation.
- “Open territory” currently has only one fully audited worked example.
- The review does not establish a canonical vocabulary, a new subsystem, or a formal standard.
