# Method Emergence Assessment

## Historical practice

Repeated artifacts perform several stable operations: bound a corpus, place representations, differentiate scales or regimes, show routes, preserve alternatives, and invite return. This is sufficient to identify a recurring practice.

## What the prototype adds

The Solar–Lunar–Carbon reconstruction explicitly records:

1. a completed bounded source review;
2. a relation matrix and prohibited conclusions;
3. spatial translation into an Evidence Map;
4. relation and boundary audit;
5. detected visual misrepresentation;
6. evidence-based correction;
7. approval of the corrected Evidence Map;
8. derivative public translation;
9. crosswalk between source status and aesthetic expression;
10. checksums and a correction history.

## Why this is not yet an explicit general method

- only one completed review has passed through the full two-layer workflow;
- independent editors have not reproduced it;
- no second domain has tested whether the same audit controls remain sufficient;
- placement remains qualitative and partly editorial;
- public-reader effects have not been evaluated;
- no conformance or acceptance test exists for Orientation Cartography.

## Method disposition

**PROVISIONAL RECONSTRUCTION WORKFLOW**

The workflow may formalize the historical practice, but one worked case cannot establish general reproducibility. It remains non-canonical and outside OLS, Kernel, Registry, and Operator contracts.
