# Historical Development

## Reconstructed sequence

The corpus does not support one clean evolutionary ladder. It supports overlapping strands:

1. **Structural and scientific mapping (April–May 2026).** Application branches, gates, regimes, axes, and multi-scale terrains establish a recurring preference for spatial comparison across representations.
2. **Architecture mapping (May–July 2026).** The same grammar is applied to systems, layers, responsibilities, and interfaces. Navigation becomes a property of repository and system design, not only a visual metaphor.
3. **Library and reader-path mapping (July 2026).** Works are organized into alternative journeys for newcomer, builder, and researcher. Friction, proposal status, and optional bridges become part of the map.
4. **Evidence-bounded neighborhoods and comparison (July 2026).** Orientation Translation introduces explicit support classes, local edge roles, rejections, stopping rules, and asymmetry.
5. **Comparative orientation spaces (July 2026).** Planetary and Solar–Lunar–Carbon studies reconstruct multiple regions without asserting one mechanism.
6. **Audited publication layers (July 2026).** One completed review is translated into an Evidence Map and then into a Museum Edition, with correction and crosswalk records.

## Continuities

- recurring fields, gates, regions, axes, layers, routes, and atlases;
- emphasis on “where am I?” and “what comes next?” rather than final explanation;
- multiple representations held together without requiring identity;
- return paths and comparative re-entry;
- visual translation as a bridge between specialist material and human orientation.

## Discontinuities

- early maps often imply stronger unity than their evidence records justify;
- evidence classes, explicit non-edges, rejection registers, and stop rules appear late;
- reader agency becomes more explicit in Library journeys and Orientation Translation;
- the Museum Edition separates public atmosphere from the research-facing relation layer;
- qualitative relational distance is explicitly distinguished from physical distance only in the audited prototype.

## Abandoned or corrected forms

- simple information-overload-to-map narratives are insufficient as evidence discipline;
- visually balanced regions are rejected when support is asymmetric;
- a physical kilometer scale in the Museum Edition was removed because the map has no measurable geographic metric;
- linear chemistry-to-property progression was rejected by Planetary Chemistry Atlas 01;
- visions remain labelled as visions rather than being used to prove current implementation.

## Direction of change

The strongest historical change is not from “diagram” to “beautiful map.” It is from **spatial explanation** toward **auditable navigation**: source scope, relation status, limits, missing connections, reader movement, and correction history increasingly survive into the artifact.
