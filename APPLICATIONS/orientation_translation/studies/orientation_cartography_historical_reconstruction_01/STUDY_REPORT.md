# Orientation Cartography — Historical Reconstruction 01

## Scope

This bounded review asks whether NEXAH produced navigational visual and editorial structures before the phrase **Orientation Cartography** was introduced. It inspects selected artifacts with an actual locating, relating, differentiating, routing, bounding, scaling, or opening function. It is not a repository-wide image inventory and does not rename the historical corpus.

## Finding

The inspected corpus supports a recurring orientation-cartographic practice. The recurrence is functional rather than stylistic:

- regime and navigation maps locate heterogeneous representations in a larger field;
- Library sequences and reader journeys route different readers through the same body of Works;
- neighborhoods preserve local relations, evidence classes, rejections, and stopping boundaries;
- comparative planetary studies separate objects, scales, mechanisms, and unknowns;
- architecture maps locate subsystems and authority boundaries;
- atlas-like Works are repeatedly described as maps, references, or higher-order navigation structures.

The evidence does **not** support retrospectively calling every NEXAH visual a map. Several inspected artifacts are dashboards, linear pipelines, isolated scientific figures, or future visions. They may be adjacent to cartography but do not qualify by appearance alone.

## Historical question

Yes. NEXAH has repeatedly constructed artifacts that help readers navigate relations, regions, layers, scales, transitions, choices, and limits. Before July 2026 this practice was distributed across Architecture, Research, the Living Library, and application studies. It lacked a shared audit procedure and a stable name.

## Method question

The Solar–Lunar–Carbon work adds something narrower and genuinely new: a documented path from a completed bounded review to a relation-qualified Evidence Map, a visual audit, a correction loop, and a derivative Museum Edition. This is one worked prototype. It supports a **provisional reconstruction workflow**, not yet a demonstrated general method.

## Strongest admissible split

- Historical disposition: **RECURRING ORIENTATION CARTOGRAPHIC PRACTICE**
- Method disposition: **PROVISIONAL RECONSTRUCTION WORKFLOW**

No OLS term, Kernel behavior, Registry entry, Operator, or canonical architecture is changed.

## Inspected corpus boundary

The review sampled:

- archived regime, navigation, gate, and application maps;
- current Orientation Laboratory and Living Library architecture visuals;
- Library reader journeys, sequences, and Atlas records;
- Orientation Translation pilots, neighborhoods, comparisons, and planetary studies;
- the Solar–Lunar–Carbon review and its two approved maps;
- selected vision artifacts only as counterevidence against retrospective canonization.

Detailed admissions, ambiguities, and exclusions are recorded in the companion registers.
