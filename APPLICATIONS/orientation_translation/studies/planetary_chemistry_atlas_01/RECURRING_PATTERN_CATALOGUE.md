# Recurring Pattern Catalogue

A pattern is admitted only when the repeated observation is stated separately
from its interpretation.

| ID | Recurring observation | Worlds | Evidence strength | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RPC-01 | One molecule can dominate atmospheres with very different total mass and outcomes | Venus, Mars (CO₂) | Well established | CO₂ percentage alone does not predict temperature |
| RPC-02 | N₂ can be the background gas for distinct active chemistries | Earth, Titan | Well established | Shared origin, habitability or identical nitrogen cycle |
| RPC-03 | H₂/He dominate accessible giant-planet atmospheres | Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune | Well established at broad level | Identical interiors or cloud structures |
| RPC-04 | Trace condensables can organize visible weather and clouds | H₂O on Earth/Mars/Jupiter; CH₄ on Titan/Uranus/Neptune; NH₃ on giants | Established occurrence; roles vary | A universal condensable-cycle model |
| RPC-05 | Photochemistry creates species not supplied in their observed form | Titan, Venus, Earth, Mars and giant upper atmospheres | Well established as a process family | Same reaction network or rate regime |
| RPC-06 | Atmospheric circulation can be vigorous under very different energy budgets | Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Titan | Well established | Solar heating as the only driver |
| RPC-07 | Surface/exosphere exchange controls sparse atmospheric states | Mercury, Moon | Well established | Equivalence to collisional weather atmospheres |
| RPC-08 | Atmospheric loss is ongoing or historically consequential | Mercury, Earth, Mars, Venus, Moon, Titan | Established in general; histories vary | A single magnetic-shield rule |
| RPC-09 | Magnetic interaction geometry differs strongly between worlds | Earth, Mercury, giants; induced fields at Venus/Mars; crustal fields at Moon/Mars | Well established | Direct prediction of atmospheric retention |
| RPC-10 | Similar bulk category can conceal major dynamical asymmetry | Jupiter/Saturn; Uranus/Neptune; Venus/Earth | Well established | Visual similarity as scientific identity |

## Pattern classes

### Compositional recurrence

A species appears in more than one atmosphere. This is the weakest admitted
pattern and carries no automatic functional claim.

### Process recurrence

A comparable process family—condensation, photolysis, circulation, escape—is
observed under different conditions. Mechanism and parameter regime must still
be checked per world.

### Role recurrence

A species or process occupies a similar explanatory role, such as a condensable
driving a weather cycle. This is an interpretation supported by observations,
not an identity statement.

### Contrast pattern

A shared constituent makes a difference more visible. Venus/Mars and
Earth/Titan are the strongest current examples.

## Rejected patterns

- composition → property as a deterministic chain;
- magnetic field → atmosphere retained as a binary rule;
- CO₂-rich → hot as a percentage-only rule;
- N₂-rich → habitable;
- methane → biological activity;
- water detected → surface ocean;
- H₂/He atmosphere → identical giant-planet structure.
