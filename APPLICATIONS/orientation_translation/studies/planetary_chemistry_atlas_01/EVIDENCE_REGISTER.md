# Evidence Register

## Evidence policy

Institutional mission/fact sources anchor the orientation layer. Approximate
values are labelled as such. A public fact page may summarize a scientific
consensus but is not treated as a measurement dataset.

## Well established

- Dominant atmospheric classes: CO₂ on Venus/Mars, N₂ on Earth/Titan, H₂/He
  on the giant planets.
- Mercury and the Moon have surface-bound, highly tenuous exospheres.
- Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune lack a solid surface at which a terrestrial
  “surface pressure” could be assigned.
- Venus has a dense CO₂ atmosphere, sulfuric-acid clouds and extreme surface
  temperature/pressure.
- Titan has nitrogen-dominated air, methane photochemistry and stable surface
  hydrocarbon liquids.
- Earth and Titan host different condensable weather cycles.
- Atmospheric escape and solar-wind interaction are observed processes.

## Moderately established / model-dependent detail

- Exact minor-species abundances on giant planets and their variation with
  depth, latitude and retrieval model.
- Deep cloud ordering and abundance for H₂O, NH₃ and NH₄SH.
- Relative contribution of internal heat, solar heating and latent processes
  to specific circulation features.
- Full causal histories producing the present Venus/Mars divergence.
- Long-term quantitative weighting of magnetic topology in atmospheric escape.

## Open questions

- Titan methane resupply.
- Venus ultraviolet cloud absorbers and present volcanic-atmospheric coupling.
- Deep composition and thermal evolution of Uranus and Neptune.
- Quantitative evolution of atmospheric inventories across impacts, escape,
  outgassing and surface reactions.
- How representative current snapshots are of long-term planetary states.

## Unknown / not admitted

- A universal composition-to-habitability rule.
- A universal magnetic-shield criterion for retention.
- A common origin implied by a common dominant gas.
- Biological interpretation of methane outside Earth without independent
  evidence.
- Any causal relationship drawn only because two items are adjacent in the
  supplied visual.
