# Evaluation

This evaluation separates what the completed artifacts demonstrate from what they merely make plausible.

| Dimension | Demonstrated | Plausibly enabled | Not demonstrated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bounded growth | One finite neighborhood with 31 nodes, 30 admitted edges, explicit exclusions, and stopping. | A repeatable way to limit local expansion. | Optimal size, general stopping law, or indefinite controlled growth. |
| Preservation of source evidence | Source and later artifacts remain distinguishable; edges cite support and boundaries. | Versioned comparison under future source changes. | Independent verification of every scientific entailment. |
| Unsupported gaps | Hidden questions, editorial bridges, and unsupported expansions remain visible. | Productive navigation through known gaps. | That all relevant gaps were found. |
| Rejection | Proposed relations can be excluded without disappearing from the record. | Negative decisions as durable governance evidence. | Consistent rejection across analysts. |
| Transitivity discipline | The neighborhood explicitly prohibits inherited A–C support. | Safer expansion of local structures. | Adversarial compliance under larger graphs. |
| Cross-scale discipline | Scale transitions can be inspected edge by edge. | More responsible navigation across biological levels. | A validated scale model or absence of scale collapse. |
| Reproducibility | The first sequence is reconstructable from frozen artifacts. | A second topic can test the same control structure. | Independent reproduction. |
| Analyst independence | Decisions and reasons are explicit enough to compare. | Inter-analyst disagreement can be measured. | Agreement or independence from the original editor. |
| Reader benefit | The artifacts offer paths, questions, and explanations. | Better question formation and orientation. | Measured comprehension, transfer, or decision quality. |
| Scalability | The local graph remains inspectable at its current size. | Multiple versioned neighborhoods could be compared. | Performance or governance at Atlas scale. |
| Automation | Structured records could be machine-read. | Assistance with validation and comparison. | Safe automatic admission, inference, or mutation. |

## Internal coherence

The sequence is internally coherent: later artifacts use earlier ones without changing them, support classes remain visible, and the neighborhood respects its stated limits. Internal coherence is not evidence of scientific validity, educational effectiveness, or generality.

## Overall assessment

The pilot demonstrates a disciplined local construction. It plausibly enables a reproducible growth study. It does not yet demonstrate a transferable model, an Atlas, automatic growth, or improved human outcomes.
