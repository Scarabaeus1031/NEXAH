## Register finding

Growth included both **addition** and **non-addition**. GE-12, GE-13, and GE-14 changed the orientation space by making exclusions and limits inspectable. A model that counts only nodes and edges would miss these events.

## Candidate process inference

The sequence suggests that a growth event should record previous state, evidence status, admission decision, risk, and preserved boundary. That is a **candidate growth rule**, not an established requirement: the fields have been reconstructed from one case and have not been independently applied.
