# Stopping Rules

The Photosynthesis neighborhood stopped at 31 nodes and 30 admitted edges. This is an observed endpoint, not evidence of an optimal graph size.

## Observed stopping mechanisms

| ID | Mechanism | Basis | Kind | Reusable? | Fragility |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SR-01 | Stop after one reviewed secondary representation per first-ring neighbor. | N, construction procedure | Editorial and practical | Possibly as a test constraint, not as a domain rule | Imposes symmetry that the subject may not possess. |
| SR-02 | Admit no further crosslinks. | N, minimum connected graph | Editorial and epistemic | Reusable when the aim is inspectability | May hide strongly supported lateral relations. |
| SR-03 | Permit only one explicitly labeled indirect edge. | N, E25 | Epistemic and editorial | Reusable as a conservative pilot limit | The number one is arbitrary. |
| SR-04 | Exclude editorial and unsupported bridges. | R and N | Epistemic | Strongly reusable | Depends on reliable classification. |
| SR-05 | Do not expand beyond the fixed evidence body. | P → R → N frozen sequence | Procedural | Reusable | Source coverage can be narrow or outdated. |
| SR-06 | Stop when every first-ring branch has one inspectable continuation or an explicit rejection. | N | Editorial | Candidate only | Completion can be mistaken for conceptual sufficiency. |
| SR-07 | Stop before Atlas formation. | N, Atlas assessment | Epistemic | Reusable until a comparison basis exists | Does not define the later Atlas threshold. |

## Why 31 nodes and 30 edges?

The count follows the chosen construction shape: a root, a finite first ring, one secondary continuation per admitted branch, and no additional crosslinks. It demonstrates that the procedure can produce a finite, connected, auditable neighborhood. It does not demonstrate that 31/30 is scientifically complete, pedagogically ideal, or transferable.

## Could another analyst stop elsewhere?

Yes. Another analyst could:

- reject a marginal direct neighbor earlier;
- select a different supported secondary representation;
- stop a branch without a continuation;
- admit a strongly supported lateral edge;
- use a narrower or broader source.

Such differences would not automatically invalidate the method. They would need to be visible, justified, and compared. Analyst independence is therefore not demonstrated.

## Reopening a stopped neighborhood

A stopped neighborhood may be reconsidered only when a new, independently fixed evidence body or a new explicit orientation question exists. Reopening should:

1. preserve the prior version;
2. declare the new evidence or question;
3. rerun edge admission and rejection;
4. record all changes as growth events;
5. retain non-transitivity and support labels;
6. end with a new explicit stopping decision.

Silent extension would erase the meaning of the original boundary.

## Assessment

Stopping was partly epistemic, partly editorial, and partly practical. It was not purely arbitrary, but several numeric and symmetric limits were editorial constraints rather than findings. The reusable principle is bounded, inspectable stopping—not the particular count or ring geometry.
