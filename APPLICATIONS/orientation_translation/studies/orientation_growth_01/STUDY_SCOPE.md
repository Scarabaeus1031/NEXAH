# Study Scope

## Research question

**How may an orientation space grow without losing its evidence boundaries?**

The object of study is the process linking one fixed source representation, its orientation translation, a retrospective analysis, and a bounded neighborhood. Photosynthesis science is not the object of study.

## Evidence body

| Code | Frozen artifact | Permitted use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `P` | Photosynthesis pilot, twelve files | Reconstruct source fixation, translation, declarations, paths, boundaries, and evaluation |
| `R` | `WHAT THE FIRST ORIENTATION PILOT REVEALED` | Reconstruct reflection, status separation, evidence gradients, hidden questions, and candidate bridges |
| `N` | `PHOTOSYNTHESIS ORIENTATION NEIGHBORHOOD` | Reconstruct node/edge admission, support classes, exclusions, stopping, and expansion recommendation |

No claim may be treated as scientific evidence merely because it appears in `P`, `R`, or `N`. The frozen source remains a tertiary Wikipedia representation, and this study examines only how the artifacts handled it.

## Status categories

| Category | Admission test | Authority | Example in this study |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source-supported observation | Explicit in `P` or its fixed source record | Local to frozen source/pilot status | The source revision was fixed before translation |
| Process observation | Requires comparison across `P`, `R`, and/or `N` | Local analytical finding | Each later artifact introduced a new kind of object while preserving earlier files |
| Editorial procedure | A human-selected operation, constraint, lens, depth, or format | Method choice only | One secondary neighbor per first-ring node |
| Candidate growth rule | Narrow generalization from the one process | Provisional; requires independent reproduction | Freeze an evidence body before each expansion pass |
| Unsupported interpretation | Not established by frozen artifacts | None | The workflow generalizes to every domain |

Category movement is prohibited without new evidence. In particular, repeated use of an editorial procedure does not make it a candidate rule automatically, and internal coherence does not demonstrate reproducibility.

## In scope

- exact process reconstruction;
- functional-layer analysis;
- growth-event registration;
- admission and stopping rules actually used or inferable;
- structural, evidential, semantic, procedural, and interpretive stability;
- failure modes and alternative interpretations;
- the narrowest candidate model justified by one case;
- a future reproducibility test;
- placement of OH-001 relative to the process.

## Out of scope

- new Photosynthesis claims or sources;
- changes to the pilot, reflection, or neighborhood;
- OLS interpretation beyond what the frozen pilot already used;
- Registry, Kernel, or architecture changes;
- construction of an atlas or ontology;
- automation or automatic graph expansion;
- creation, development, or testing of OH-001;
- claims of reader benefit, scalability, analyst independence, or universality.

## Decision boundary

The study may identify a **candidate** local growth process. It cannot establish a reproducible model from one source, one analyst sequence, and one neighborhood. Reproducibility requires an independently fixed second source and independent reconstruction.
