# Failure Modes

These failure modes test the reconstructed procedure rather than the scientific content of Photosynthesis.

| Failure mode | Trigger | Observable symptom | Consequence | Current safeguard | Safeguard weakness | Required test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Graph explosion | Every mention, link, or plausible association is admitted. | Rapid node growth, many weak edges, no defensible stopping point. | The neighborhood becomes an unbounded knowledge graph. | Relevance review, one secondary continuation, no crosslinks, explicit stopping. | Numeric limits can suppress valid structure and may not transfer. | Repeat on a second topic without prescribing node count. |
| Semantic collapse | Related representations are treated as synonyms. | Nodes merge across process, object, scale, or explanatory role. | Distinctions that support orientation disappear. | Separate nodes, edge reasons, non-transitivity. | Natural-language labels can still hide ambiguity. | Independent semantic audit of a sample of edges. |
| Transitive overreach | A–B and B–C are taken to establish A–C. | Unsupported shortcuts appear as direct relations. | Evidence is inherited across unexamined transitions. | Explicit no-transitivity rule. | No adversarial example has tested compliance. | Seed tempting A–C shortcuts and require independent evidence. |
| Evidence laundering | Editorial or indirect relations are presented as directly sourced. | Support labels become uniformly strong or source citations do not entail the edge. | Curatorial judgment acquires false authority. | Four support classes and explicit unsupported boundaries. | Classification is performed by the same editorial process. | Blind reclassification by an independent reviewer. |
| Boundary drift | Later additions reinterpret what an earlier frozen artifact claimed. | Old claims change without versioned evidence. | Provenance and comparability are lost. | Frozen P, R, and N artifacts; this study is additive. | Future maintenance could bypass the freeze. | Reopen under a controlled new version and compare diffs. |
| Source drift | The public source changes or a replacement source is silently used. | Edge citations no longer match the recorded source. | Reproduction yields different results without explanation. | Fixed source representation and traceability. | No durable source snapshot is guaranteed here. | Archive or hash the source representation used in Pilot 02. |
| Scale collapse | Molecular, cellular, organismal, ecological, and planetary representations are linked as if equivalent. | Edges omit the scale transition or causal level. | Cross-scale navigation becomes misleading. | Edge-specific reasons and unsupported boundaries. | No general scale vocabulary or validation exists. | Audit every cross-scale edge and require the scale change to be stated. |
| Reciprocity assumption | A → B is automatically read as B → A. | Reverse navigation appears without independent support. | Directional meaning and evidence are distorted. | Directional edge records; reciprocity not presumed. | No explicit reciprocal-pair test has been run. | Pilot 02 must evaluate both directions independently. |
| Aesthetic authority | Symmetry, centrality, color, or polished layouts are read as evidence. | Readers infer importance from design rather than provenance. | Editorial form overstates epistemic confidence. | Textual support labels and limits. | The visual artifact can still dominate reading. | Reader test with graph layout varied but evidence held constant. |
| Infinite deferral | Every uncertain relation is postponed and no usable orientation is produced. | Only exclusions accumulate; no bounded path is offered. | The method remains safe but practically empty. | Direct and bounded indirect admissions are permitted. | No threshold for sufficient utility is established. | Compare reader orientation with and without admitted neighborhood paths. |

## Cross-cutting observation

Most safeguards are editorial, not computational. Their strength is inspectability; their weakness is dependence on disciplined human judgment. Automation would amplify errors unless the support classes, source identity, rejection record, and stopping decision remained explicit.
