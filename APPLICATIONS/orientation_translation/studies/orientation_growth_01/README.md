# NEXAH Orientation Growth Study 01
## From Pilot to Neighborhood to Atlas

This study asks whether the observed sequence

`Source Representation → Orientation Translation → Reflection → Neighborhood Construction`

is evidence of a reproducible method for growing an orientation space without losing its evidence boundaries.

It does not assume that an Orientation Growth Model exists. It compares that interpretation with editorial, graph-construction, research, learning-design, and knowledge-architecture interpretations.

## Frozen evidence

The study reads but does not modify:

1. `pilots/photosynthesis_v1/` — the completed Photosynthesis pilot;
2. `reviews/WHAT_THE_FIRST_ORIENTATION_PILOT_REVEALED.md` — the pilot reflection;
3. `neighborhoods/photosynthesis_v1/PHOTOSYNTHESIS_ORIENTATION_NEIGHBORHOOD.md` — the first neighborhood.

No external scientific source is added. OLS, Registry, Kernel, repository architecture, and OH-001 remain outside the study's write scope.

## Status vocabulary

- **Source-supported observation** — directly supported by frozen source or pilot evidence.
- **Process observation** — visible by comparing the three frozen evidence bodies.
- **Editorial procedure** — a deliberate method or constraint introduced by the designers.
- **Candidate growth rule** — a possible general rule inferred from one observed process; not reproduced.
- **Unsupported interpretation** — exceeds the available evidence.
