# Reproducibility Plan

No replication is performed here. This document specifies the minimum test required before the candidate process may be treated as more than a single-case reconstruction.

## Candidate Pilot 02

**Cellular Respiration** is the strongest local candidate because the frozen Photosynthesis work identifies it as a neighboring representation. This does not predefine its source, lenses, nodes, edge count, or neighborhood shape.

## Independence conditions

1. Fix and preserve a new source representation before analysis.
2. Prefer an analyst who did not construct the Photosynthesis neighborhood.
3. Do not prescribe the 31-node, 30-edge, two-ring result.
4. Do not reuse Photosynthesis edges as inherited evidence.
5. Keep Orientation Translation, Reflection, and Neighborhood artifacts distinct.
6. Record admissions, rejections, uncertainties, and stopping decisions.
7. Freeze each stage before the next begins.

## Replication sequence

| Stage | Required action | Comparison target | Prohibited shortcut |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Source | Select, identify, preserve, and bound the Cellular Respiration source. | Source fixity and traceability | Treating the Photosynthesis source as sufficient. |
| 1. Translation | Construct orientation lenses from the new source. | Separation of source statements, process observations, and editorial constructions | Copying the prior lens set by template. |
| 2. Reflection | Identify emergent relations, hidden questions, weak support, and candidate bridges. | Whether reflection adds inspectable questions without changing evidence | Answering hidden questions or promoting bridges. |
| 3. Neighborhood | Admit the smallest justified local structure with edge-level support and rejection. | Evidence classes, direction, non-transitivity, and stopping | Forcing the same rings, counts, or symmetry. |
| 4. Comparison | Compare both frozen sequences. | Common process structure and legitimate variation | Merging the neighborhoods into an Atlas. |

## Expected common structures

These are candidate expectations, not required outcomes:

- fixed input evidence;
- distinct representation/orientation work;
- reflection before expansion;
- edge-level admission and rejection;
- explicit support and unsupported boundaries;
- non-transitivity;
- finite, reasoned stopping.

## Permitted differences

- number and type of lenses;
- immediate and secondary representations;
- node and edge counts;
- depth and asymmetry;
- absence or presence of an indirect edge;
- stopping point;
- whether a useful neighborhood can be formed at all.

## Reciprocity test

For every relation shared by both pilots, evaluate each direction independently. A Photosynthesis → Cellular Respiration transition does not establish Cellular Respiration → Photosynthesis. Record whether the reverse direction is directly supported, indirectly supported, editorial, or unsupported in the new source.

## Failure conditions for the candidate model

Reject or substantially revise the candidate model if:

- the stages cannot be distinguished without copying the first pilot's template;
- an independent analyst cannot apply the support classes consistently;
- boundaries or source identity drift during the process;
- the neighborhood requires inherited or transitive support;
- stopping cannot be justified without an arbitrary target count;
- rejection records are insufficient to prevent graph expansion;
- the procedure produces no orienting value distinguishable from a link list.

## Relation to an Atlas

Two completed neighborhoods would still not automatically form an Atlas. Atlas consideration would require stable identities, an explicit comparison question, independently supported inter-neighborhood transitions, preserved directional and support differences, and human review. Shared vocabulary or reciprocal appearance alone would not suffice.

## OH-001

OH-001 remains outside this application-growth sequence. Its earliest admissible consideration would be after the independent reproduction and a separate cross-neighborhood comparison. Even then, consideration would not imply creation, approval, or architectural adoption.
