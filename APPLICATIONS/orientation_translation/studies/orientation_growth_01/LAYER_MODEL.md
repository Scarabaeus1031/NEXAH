# Layer Model

## Test criterion

A layer is treated as functionally distinct only if it has a distinguishable object, operation, admissible evidence, authority boundary, output, and prohibition. A separate file or chronological stage is not sufficient.

## Candidate-layer assessment

| Candidate layer | Object and operation | Evidence that may enter | May produce | May not produce | Independence / reproducibility | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source Layer | Identifiable external representation; fix identity and status | Selected source plus access/revision metadata | Frozen evidence body | Truth, completeness, correction, or authority | Functionally independent; reproducibility of fixation is testable | **Distinct and demonstrated locally** |
| Representation Layer | Source observations; construct typed, bounded lenses and relations | Frozen source observations and declared context | Orientation model and lens representations | Reality, validation, or complete model | Distinguishable within Stage 1, but not tested independently from the pilot's editorial design | **Distinct function inside translation** |
| Orientation Layer | Representations plus reader position; compare and construct paths/explanations | Representation outputs, declared bases, provenance, uncertainty | Comparison, orientation map, Reader/Explain renditions | Recommendation, optimality, learning, or authority | Functionally distinct; only one realization exists | **Distinct function inside translation** |
| Reflection Layer | Completed orientation products; analyze emergent structure, support gradients, hidden questions, and gaps | Frozen `P` only | Process observations and candidate bridges | New source claims or answers to hidden questions | Produces objects not present in `P`; independent analyst reproduction untested | **Provisionally distinct analytical layer** |
| Neighborhood Layer | Admissible local nodes and edges; classify, admit, reject, and stop | Frozen `P` and `R`, with explicit support | Bounded local adjacency, exclusion register, stopping boundary | Ontology, transitive closure, reciprocity, or atlas | More than a graph view because it makes admission decisions; reproduction untested | **Provisionally distinct admission layer** |
| Atlas Layer | Multiple independently constructed neighborhoods; compare and connect them | Would require at least two fixed sources and independently audited neighborhoods | Cross-neighborhood navigation if criteria are met | Assumed reciprocity or merged meaning | No instance exists | **Not present; future possibility only** |
| Hypothesis Layer | Recurrent cross-neighborhood pattern; formulate a testable orientation hypothesis | Would require reproduced process evidence and explicit comparison | Candidate hypothesis under separate governance | Hypothesis from one local neighborhood or aesthetic recurrence | Not performed | **Outside the present application sequence** |

## Reflection: layer or evaluation?

Reflection is more than the pilot's original evaluation because it changes the analytical object. `P/EVALUATION.md` compares baseline and output; `R` classifies which relations emerged from orientation, constructs an evidence gradient, records hidden questions without answering them, and distinguishes candidate bridges. Those outputs later constrain `N`.

This supports a **provisionally distinct analytical layer** in this case. It does not prove that every orientation application requires a separate reflection layer; the same function could be embedded in another workflow.

## Neighborhood: layer or graph view?

The Mermaid graph is only a view. The layer resides in the edge-level admission decisions, support classes, excluded bridge register, rejected expansion register, and stopping boundary. Removing the diagram would not remove those decisions. This supports a **provisionally distinct admission layer**, not a knowledge-graph claim.

## Atlas status

`N` calls the result an atlas seed because a directly supported neighboring focal topic exists. No second independently constructed neighborhood exists, so there is no cross-neighborhood identity, reciprocity, conflict, or edge audit. The Atlas Layer is therefore absent.

## Hypothesis placement

Hypothesis formation does not belong inside the local application-growth sequence shown by `P → R → N`. A recurring pattern cannot be distinguished from source choice, analyst style, or editorial procedure until an independent second sequence and cross-neighborhood comparison exist.

OH-001 therefore remains deferred and outside this study. The earliest defensible review point is **after a reproducibility test and cross-neighborhood comparison**. Even then, admission is not automatic.
