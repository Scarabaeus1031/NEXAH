## Structural finding

The composition is layered but not semantically uniform. SD-01 through SD-05
remain primarily the comparison architecture of Planetary Chemistry Atlas 01.
SD-06 through SD-10 introduce form, interface, field, and cross-scale transition
questions that the chemistry study neither required nor evidenced. SD-11 is a
governance layer applicable to both.

The image therefore contains a chemical atlas core plus an editorially proposed
comparison environment. It does not yet demonstrate that all included forms are
members of one scientific family.
