# Evidence Boundary Matrix

## Status vocabulary

- **Supported:** the existing Atlas 01 review or its registered sources directly
  supports the bounded comparison.
- **Weakly supported:** part of the relation is supported, but scope, mechanism,
  or cross-scale transfer is not.
- **Editorial:** the whiteboard deliberately places representations together;
  this is evidence of an editorial comparison, not of a natural relation.
- **Unknown:** the present corpus cannot determine the relation.
- **Not admissible:** the proposed implication would exceed or contradict the
  current evidence boundary.

## Evidence finding

The strongest supported chain remains inside Planetary Chemistry Atlas 01:
elements, species, atmospheric states, and separately reviewed process
families. The morphology and transition portions are supported as editorial
questions. Their scientific relations remain case-specific, weak, unknown, or
inadmissible when generalized.
