# Repository Independence Assessment

## Question

Can the comparison represented by the whiteboard exist independently of
Planetary Chemistry Atlas 01?

## Dependency test

| Component | Depends on Atlas 01? | Can be examined independently? | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| element/species inventory | Yes | Not without replacing its evidence base | It is part of the chemistry comparison already reviewed. |
| planetary atmosphere matrix | Yes | Not in the present corpus | Its values and boundaries are owned by Atlas 01 evidence. |
| planetary process comparison | Yes, for current claims | Only with a new case-specific evidence set | Atlas 01 supplies the present scientific support. |
| idealized form inventory | No | Yes, as an editorial or geometric question | The objects are not required by the chemistry study. |
| cross-scale examples | No | Yes, but not as established science | They require biological, geometric and physical evidence absent from Atlas 01. |
| boundary/interface comparison | No | Yes, with declared role and mechanism per case | It asks a different question from atmospheric composition. |
| tidal deformation case | No | Yes, with dedicated physical evidence | It is a coupled dynamics example, not a chemistry dependency. |
| transition archetype | No | Only as an editorial hypothesis | It currently derives from arrangement, not independent recurrence evidence. |
| evidence-status grammar | No | Yes | It is a review discipline shared across studies. |

## Independence finding

The complete whiteboard cannot remain conceptually inside Planetary Chemistry
Atlas 01 without broadening that study from atmospheric composition and
process comparison into geometry, boundary roles, biological examples and
coupled dynamics. That would erase the evidence boundary Atlas 01 established.

At the same time, the non-chemical portion is not yet a self-sufficient Atlas.
It has no independent corpus, comparison protocol, case-level source register,
or demonstrated recurrence. Its independence is therefore **question-level**,
not yet **evidence-level**.

## Repository-bound conclusion

The whiteboard can be reviewed independently as a bounded editorial research
object. It cannot presently stand independently as an evidenced scientific
model. Planetary Chemistry Atlas 01 remains one input case, not the authority
for the cross-scale comparisons.
