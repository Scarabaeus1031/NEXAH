# Comparison Object Inventory

## Object discipline

The same word may denote a material system, a geometric description, a
boundary role, or an editorial symbol. Those uses are not interchangeable.

## Type groups

The inventory separates into at least five object classes:

1. **Geometric descriptors:** sphere, ellipsoid, torus, drop-like and shell-like
   form.
2. **Material or biological systems:** atmosphere, cell, egg, cloud, planet.
3. **Interaction regions:** magnetosphere and particular fields.
4. **Relational roles:** inside, boundary, outside, exchange.
5. **Process descriptions:** deformation, circulation, transition and flow.

These groups can be placed in correspondence, but they cannot be compared by
one unqualified identity test. A form comparison asks a different question from
a mechanism comparison; a boundary-role comparison asks a different question
from either.

## Inventory finding

The candidates do not form one coherent object class. They form a deliberately
heterogeneous inventory whose useful property is the possibility of controlled
comparison. The whiteboard becomes misleading only when visual similarity is
treated as mechanism, identity, function, or evidence.
