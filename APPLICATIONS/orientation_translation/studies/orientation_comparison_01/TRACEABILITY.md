## New framework inference codes

- **CI-01** — Comparison should create an external correspondence record rather than merge inputs.
- **CI-02** — Comparison equivalence is dimension-specific.
- **CI-03** — Scoped identity requires referent, definition, scope, scale, source, and version review.
- **CI-04** — Role and identity must be compared independently.
- **CI-05** — Edge correspondence requires endpoint identity, direction, relation semantics, support, scope, and exclusions.
- **CI-06** — Reciprocity requires independently supported directional edges.
- **CI-07** — Asymmetry is a valid and potentially orienting comparison result.
- **CI-08** — Atlas participation requires governed comparison, not resemblance.
- **CI-09** — `Incomparable`, `unresolved`, and no match are valid outcomes.
