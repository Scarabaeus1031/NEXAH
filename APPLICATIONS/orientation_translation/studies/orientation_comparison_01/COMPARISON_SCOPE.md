# Comparison Scope

## Research question

How can two Orientation Neighborhoods be compared without destroying their independent evidence boundaries?

## Unit of study

The study concerns a future pair of independently constructed, frozen Orientation Neighborhood packages. Each package includes its own source boundary, translation history, support classifications, admitted relations, rejected candidates, stopping decision, and uncertainty record.

No actual pair is compared here.

## In scope

- defining eligible comparison objects;
- defining dimensions that preserve rather than erase differences;
- testing identity claims;
- comparing roles and directional edges;
- treating reciprocity and asymmetry explicitly;
- defining comparison failures;
- defining minimum Atlas participation criteria;
- specifying a reproducible future protocol;
- separating demonstrated design properties, plausible uses, and unknown performance.

## Out of scope

- Pilot 02 or any new Orientation Translation;
- comparison of Photosynthesis with Cellular Respiration or another topic;
- construction of an Atlas or cross-neighborhood edge;
- canonical concept identities or ontology design;
- OH-001;
- OLS, Registry, Kernel, architecture, or operator changes;
- automatic graph merging, similarity inference, or recommendation;
- scientific evaluation of any neighborhood's subject matter.

## Independence condition

Two neighborhoods qualify as independent only when neither inherits the other's nodes, roles, edges, support classes, stopping shape, or conclusions as evidence. A shared procedure is permitted; a copied result is not. Each must preserve its own source identity and decision history.

## Comparison boundary

Comparison produces a separate record about correspondences and differences. It must not rewrite either input. A correspondence is therefore a reviewed claim between immutable references, not a merge operation.

## Status discipline

Framework statements are classified as:

- **Demonstrated design property** — explicit in this framework's structure.
- **Plausible comparison capability** — supported by reasoning but untested on two neighborhoods.
- **Unknown** — requires future empirical use.

These statuses describe the framework, not the scientific truth of future neighborhood content.
