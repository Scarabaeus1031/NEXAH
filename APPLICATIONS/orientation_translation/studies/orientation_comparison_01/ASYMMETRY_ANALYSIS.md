# Asymmetry Analysis

Asymmetry is first-class comparison evidence. A comparison framework that rewards only matches will erase precisely the local differences that Orientation Neighborhoods are intended to preserve.

## Forms of asymmetry

| Form | Example pattern | What it may reveal | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structural | Different depth, branching, density, or stopping shape | Different source coverage or editorial purpose | Better or worse neighborhood |
| Identity | A node exists in only one neighborhood | Scope or source-specific representation | Irrelevance to the other neighborhood |
| Directional | A → B is supported; B → A is not | One-way process, explanation, or navigation | Causality unless explicitly supported |
| Evidential | Corresponding edges have different support classes | Different source strength or entailment | That the stronger record upgrades the weaker |
| Role | Same identity serves different local roles | Context-dependent orientation function | Identity failure |
| Scale | Similar relations operate at different declared scales | A scale transition needing explicit treatment | Cross-scale equivalence |
| Rejection | One neighborhood rejects what the other admits | Different evidence bodies, questions, or thresholds | Automatic contradiction |
| Temporal | Growth events occur in different order | Different discovery or editorial history | Necessary process order |
| Interpretive | Same evidence supports different bounded readings | Legitimate plurality or unresolved ambiguity | That all interpretations are equally supported |

## Why asymmetry may be more informative than symmetry

Symmetry identifies repeatable correspondences. Asymmetry exposes where evidence, purpose, scale, direction, or editorial judgment diverges. Those divergences can locate:

- a boundary between representations;
- a missing source;
- a one-way transition;
- a change of scale;
- a role shift;
- an unresolved question;
- a different stopping rationale;
- a possible future bridge that must remain editorial.

## Asymmetry record

Every material difference should state:

```yaml
dimension:
object_a:
object_b:
difference:
possible_explanations:
evidence:
uncertainty:
orientation_value:
must_not_infer:
```

## Boundary

Asymmetry is not automatically conflict, defect, novelty, or discovery. It becomes informative only when its source and comparison dimension are explicit.
