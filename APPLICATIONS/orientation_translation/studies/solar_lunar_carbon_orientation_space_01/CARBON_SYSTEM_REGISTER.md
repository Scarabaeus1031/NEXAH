# Carbon System Register

This is an inventory of carbon-related systems encountered in the bounded corpus. It is not a complete Carbon Cycle.

| Carbon-related system | Scale | Corpus status | Available relation | Missing evidence / boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Atmospheric carbon dioxide in the Photosynthesis pilot | Molecular to environmental | Supported prerequisite | CO₂ enters the represented carbon-fixation account. | Does not represent all atmospheric sources, sinks, or feedbacks. |
| Calvin cycle | Molecular / pathway | Supported | ATP and NADPH support incorporation of CO₂ in the familiar represented pathway. | Not universal to all photosynthetic organisms or carbon-fixation modes. |
| Biological carbon fixation | Molecular to cellular | Supported | Carbon dioxide is incorporated into organic compounds. | Does not by itself constitute a global carbon budget. |
| Fixed organic carbon and carbohydrates | Molecular to cellular | Supported consequence | The pilot connects fixed carbon with stored chemical forms and metabolism. | Does not supply a full biomass, trophic, or nutrition model. |
| Biomass / biological energy | Organismal to ecological | Broadly supported | Photosynthetic production is related to wider biological significance. | Intermediate food-web and ecosystem mechanisms are incomplete. |
| Cellular respiration and CO₂ | Molecular to limited ecosystem context | Narrow / asymmetric | Respiration is a neighboring representation; one source statement relates plant respiration to ecosystem CO₂. | No reciprocal closed photosynthesis–respiration cycle or complete ecosystem carbon account. |
| Global carbon conversion | Planetary significance claim | Broadly supported | The source locates photosynthesis at global scale. | Reservoirs, fluxes, time scales, and balancing processes are absent. |
| Carbon Cycle | Multi-system | Indirect only | Carbon fixation and global conversion justify it as a neighboring representation. | The cycle itself was not constructed or independently sourced. |
| Planetary atmospheric CO₂ | Planetary | Supported comparison | Venus, Earth, and Mars permit bounded composition and environment comparison. | Same CO₂ presence or dominance does not establish same climate, history, or biology. |
| Methane and other carbon-bearing species | Planetary | Supported by Planetary Chemistry Atlas 01 | CH₄ and CO appear in world-specific atmospheric roles. | Carbon-bearing species do not imply biological origin or one shared cycle. |
| Ocean–atmosphere exchange | Planetary / environmental | Not established in this corpus | Candidate Carbon Cycle component only. | Requires dedicated oceanic reservoir and flux evidence. |
| Sedimentation | Geological | Not established in this corpus | Candidate long-term carbon pathway only. | No sediment transport, burial, or time-scale evidence reviewed. |
| Carbonate formation | Chemical / geological | Not established in this corpus | Candidate mineral carbon pathway only. | No carbonate chemistry or planetary reservoir evidence reviewed. |
| Tidal influence on carbon cycling | Coastal / planetary | Unknown | An open candidate relation generated by juxtaposing the tidal case and carbon questions. | No admitted mechanism, measurement, or source supports it here. |

## Register conclusion

The corpus strongly supports a carbon-transformation region centered on photosynthesis. It only partially exposes a carbon-system region. Ocean exchange, sedimentation, carbonate formation, and tidal carbon effects remain outside the admitted evidence.
