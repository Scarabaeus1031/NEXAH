# Open Questions

These questions arise from the orientation space. They are not answered here.

## Solar energy and biological transformation

1. Which solar-radiation representation is required to move beyond the generic term “light” without overloading the Photosynthesis pilot?
2. Where does the reviewed source stop between photon capture and ecosystem-scale energy flow?
3. Which photosynthetic variants require a different path from the Calvin-cycle-centered representation?
4. How should changing light conditions be compared without turning environmental correlation into biochemical control?

## Water

5. Which water reservoirs and fluxes would be required for a genuine Water Cycle representation?
6. How do biological water use, atmospheric water, ocean water, ice, and planetary condensables differ as comparison objects?
7. Where would a water-availability constraint end and a hydrological mechanism begin?
8. Can Earth’s water cycle and other planetary condensable cycles be compared without implying chemical or functional identity?

## Carbon

9. Which intermediate representations are required between carbon fixation and a complete Carbon Cycle?
10. Where should respiration enter without producing a false reciprocal closure with photosynthesis?
11. Which reservoirs, fluxes, and time scales are missing from the current carbon account?
12. What evidence would be required to admit ocean–atmosphere carbon exchange?
13. What evidence would be required to admit sedimentation and carbonate formation?
14. How should atmospheric CO₂ on Venus and Mars be compared with biological carbon on Earth without collapsing scale or function?

## Lunar influence

15. What dedicated physical evidence is required to audit the `Sun + Moon → terrestrial tides` representation?
16. Which tide variables would be needed before ecological or carbon consequences could be considered?
17. Is any lunar relation to photosynthesis observable after controlling for solar, tidal, seasonal, and environmental variables?
18. What would distinguish direct lunar forcing from a correlated terrestrial cycle?
19. Does the lunar branch belong in the same future evidence program as the carbon branch, or only in the same orientation map?

## Planetary comparison

20. Which planetary environmental variables are comparable at compatible scales and reference levels?
21. How should solar input and internal heat remain visibly separate in atmospheric comparisons?
22. Which carbon-bearing molecules support comparison without implying shared origin or life?
23. At what point does a planetary context become too broad to support a biological transition?

## Orientation method

24. Does declaring weak and absent edges help readers more than omitting them?
25. Can readers recognize that one coherent orientation space may contain several unrelated mechanisms?
26. Does the strong solar–biological path visually overpower the weaker lunar branch and create a false impression of equal evidence?
27. Which stopping boundary most effectively prevents a reader from inferring lunar control of the Calvin cycle?
