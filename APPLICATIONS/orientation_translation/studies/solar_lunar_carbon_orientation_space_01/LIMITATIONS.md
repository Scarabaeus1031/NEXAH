# Limitations

## Corpus limitations

1. The review reuses a bounded set of existing NEXAH studies; it is not a repository-wide resurvey.
2. The Photosynthesis pilot is based on one fixed Wikipedia revision and did not independently validate its cited scientific literature.
3. Planetary Chemistry Atlas 01 has a registered planetary evidence set, but it was designed for atmospheric comparison rather than an Earth-system carbon study.
4. Morphologies of Transition 01 explicitly lacked a dedicated physical evidence set for its tidal case.
5. No dedicated Carbon Cycle, Water Cycle, oceanography, coastal ecology, sedimentation, carbonate, or tidal biology source was reviewed.
6. No new scientific sources were introduced in this review.

## Comparison is not correlation

Placing two representations in one map establishes only that they can be inspected together under declared criteria. It does not establish that their variables covary.

## Correlation is not causality

Even if solar, lunar, biological, hydrological, or carbon variables were observed to vary together, correlation would not establish direction, mediation, sufficiency, or mechanism. This review contains no such statistical analysis.

## Causality is not mechanism

A causal claim would still not identify how the effect is produced. The admitted relations use mechanism language only where the completed source material already supports a bounded process account.

## Editorial organization is not explanation

The orientation map organizes strong, indirect, weak, open, and rejected relations. Its layout is an editorial construction. Visual proximity, arrows, region membership, and reading order are not scientific evidence.

## Scale limitations

- Molecular carbon fixation is not the Carbon Cycle.
- Cellular products are not automatically ecosystem fluxes.
- Atmospheric composition is not planetary climate by itself.
- A planetary tide representation is not a coastal ecosystem model.
- A lunar exosphere is not lunar control of terrestrial biology.

## Direction and reciprocity limitations

The Photosynthesis–Cellular Respiration comparison found asymmetric mentions and no established reciprocal closed cycle. The present review preserves that result. Reverse arrows and apparent cycles must not be inferred.

## Evidence asymmetry

The solar–photosynthesis spine is much better supported than the lunar branch. A shared visual frame could conceal this difference. The map therefore treats asymmetry as evidence and retains weak and absent edges.

## Completeness limitation

The study does not claim to inventory all solar, lunar, carbon, water, biological, or planetary relations. It reconstructs only what the bounded corpus can support or explicitly identify as missing.

## Prohibited interpretations

The review does not validate:

- lunar control of photosynthesis;
- lunar dependence of the Calvin cycle;
- a tidal control of carbon cycling;
- one universal forcing mechanism;
- a universal morphology or transition law;
- earlier NEXAH hypotheses;
- causal conclusions from planetary comparison.
