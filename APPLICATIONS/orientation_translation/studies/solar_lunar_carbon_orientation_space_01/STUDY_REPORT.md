# SOLAR–LUNAR–CARBON ORIENTATION SPACE
## Exploratory Review 01

## Status

This is a bounded, non-canonical orientation review. It reconstructs relations already present in completed NEXAH studies. It is not a scientific theory, causal model, ontology, Atlas, or validation of earlier hypotheses.

## Research question

Can Solar energy, Lunar influence, Water, Carbon, Photosynthesis, the Calvin cycle, and Planetary processes be organized into one coherent orientation space while every relation remains qualified?

## Reconstruction result

A coherent orientation space can be reconstructed, but it is not a single causal chain and its regions are not equally supported.

### Strong biological–energetic region

The completed Photosynthesis material directly supports:

```text
solar energy / light
        ↓
light-dependent reactions
        ↓
ATP + NADPH
        ↓
Calvin cycle / carbon fixation
        ↓
fixed organic carbon
```

Water and carbon dioxide enter this region as explicit prerequisites in oxygenic photosynthesis. Oxygen and carbohydrates appear as represented consequences. These relations are bounded by organismal variation: the Calvin cycle and oxygenic pathway must not be treated as universal forms of all photosynthesis.

### Wider carbon and living-systems region

The pilot directly supports broad statements about biomass, biological energy, atmospheric oxygen, and global carbon conversion. It does not supply a complete carbon-cycle, food-web, ecosystem, ocean, sedimentation, or carbonate model. The transition from carbon fixation to Carbon Cycle is therefore indirect and intentionally incomplete.

Cellular Respiration is a directly named neighboring representation, but the frozen comparison found asymmetric mentions rather than a reciprocal closed cycle. The review may place respiration near the carbon region; it may not use it to close a photosynthesis–respiration loop.

### Planetary region

Planetary Chemistry Atlas 01 supports comparison of atmospheric CO₂, H₂O, condensation, photochemistry, surface–atmosphere exchange, solar forcing, internal energy, atmospheric escape, and world-specific processes. It also demonstrates that the same molecule does not imply the same world or function.

This region supplies environmental and planetary context. It does not turn composition into biological mechanism or establish a universal composition-to-property chain.

### Lunar region

The reviewed planetary evidence directly supports a variable, surface-bound lunar exosphere and processes involving solar-wind supply, impacts, surface migration, and outgassing. These facts do not connect the Moon to the Calvin cycle, photosynthesis, or terrestrial carbon cycling.

Morphologies of Transition 01 records `Sun + Moon → terrestrial tides` only as weakly supported within that corpus: the relation appears in an editorial whiteboard and was not audited against a dedicated physical evidence set. `Tides → coastal ecosystems`, `Tides → carbon cycling`, and any lunar influence on photosynthesis remain unknown in this review.

## What makes the space coherent

The space is coherent at the level of qualified orientation because it lets a reader move among four distinct questions:

1. Where does usable light enter a biological process?
2. Where are water and carbon dioxide transformed in that process?
3. Where do the source-supported relations stop before a full Carbon Cycle or ecosystem model?
4. How do solar and lunar planetary influences differ in evidence, scale, and mechanism?

Coherence comes from inspectable relations and declared stopping boundaries—not from a common mechanism.

## What the space does not establish

- The Moon controls photosynthesis.
- The Calvin cycle depends on lunar cycles.
- Tides regulate the Carbon Cycle.
- Solar and lunar forcing are interchangeable.
- Water links all represented systems through one mechanism.
- Carbon-bearing atmospheres imply photosynthesis or life.
- Similar planetary or biological forms demonstrate a relation.
- The represented regions form a complete Earth-system model.

## Central finding

The evidence supports a recurring multi-system orientation space with a strong solar–photosynthesis–carbon-fixation spine, a partially supported outward route toward carbon and planetary systems, and a deliberately weak lunar branch. The unequal support is a result, not a defect to be repaired editorially.

## Disposition

**RECURRING MULTI-SYSTEM ORIENTATION SPACE**

This disposition recognizes a navigable comparative space. It does not assert a shared causal mechanism, scientific unity, completeness, or canonical status.
