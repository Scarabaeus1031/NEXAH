# Relation Matrix

Status values:

- **Admitted** — usable within the stated boundary.
- **Bounded bridge** — usable only as an indirect or contextual transition.
- **Open** — insufficient evidence in the reviewed corpus.
- **Not admitted** — a prohibited or unsupported implication.

## Matrix finding

The admitted structure is intentionally nonuniform. Strong relations cluster around photosynthetic transformation and registered planetary observations. Wider carbon-system links become indirect; lunar-to-biological links remain open or not admitted.
