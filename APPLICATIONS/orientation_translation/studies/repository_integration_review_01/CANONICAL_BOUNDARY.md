# Canonical Boundary

## Status of the reviewed material

All findings in the four reviewed studies remain non-canonical research evidence. Repository acknowledgement does not change that status.

## Findings that must remain non-canonical

### Method Archaeology 01

- Candidate or provisional descriptions of Orientation Translation actions
- Any inferred method sequence derived from repository artifacts
- Any claim that the method is complete, stable, independently reproducible, or reader-validated
- Any use of **CANDIDATE ACTION INVENTORY SUFFICIENT FOR READER-TEST DESIGN** as authorization to execute a test, reuse the method, revise the method, or establish canonical operations

### Morphologies of Transition 01

- The proposed grouping of morphological, boundary, transition, or mixed comparison spaces
- Any interpretation of the reviewed whiteboard as a new discipline
- Any promotion to an atlas, Concept, Operator, or architecture layer

### Editorial Grammar of Orientation 01

- The recurring editorial operation family
- The admitted candidate operations and their grouping
- Any conversion of recurrence into a formal grammar, mandatory sequence, visual standard, or semantic contract

### Orientation Operations 01

- The recurring orientation functions observed in artifacts
- Strength classifications assigned within the review
- Any claim that the functions are necessary, universal, cognitive, causal, or demonstrated in readers

## Canonical systems that must not change

- OLS release and Orientation Language specification
- Orientation Kernel behavior and contracts
- Canonical Registry and identities
- Operator Registry and operator contracts
- Navigator behavior
- APIs and adapters
- Architecture v1.0 and current subsystem boundaries
- Editorial Operating System authority and workflows
- Living Concepts identities, overlays, and answer contracts
- Library entities, reader policies, and editorial sequences

## Documentation rule

A link, index entry, or descriptive acknowledgement records that a study exists. It does not ratify its vocabulary, findings, classifications, or proposed abstractions.
