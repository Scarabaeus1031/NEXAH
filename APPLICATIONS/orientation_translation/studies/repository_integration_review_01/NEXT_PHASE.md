# Next Phase

## Evidence status

Repository-only evidence has been extensively reviewed through pilots, growth and comparison studies, Meta Review 01, Method Archaeology 01, and the three later bounded reviews. Another repository integration review would repeat the same evidence source rather than test the remaining uncertainty.

## Recommended next evidence source

The next evidence source should be independent human reader observation.

A future, separately authorized study should examine whether readers unfamiliar with NEXAH can use independently selected Orientation Translation artifacts to:

- distinguish source-supported relations from editorial constructions;
- identify evidence, uncertainty, and stopping boundaries;
- recognize when two representations are being compared rather than merged;
- describe where their orientation changed;
- identify a justified next question or next representation.

## Why this source is next

The completed reviews demonstrate recurrence in repository artifacts. They do not demonstrate reader effect, cognitive necessity, or analyst-independent interpretation. Human observation addresses that specific unresolved boundary without requiring canonical promotion.

## Not recommended next

- another repository-only review;
- an OLS or Kernel change;
- a new Operator or Registry identity;
- a formal grammar implementation;
- automatic extraction or inference;
- an Atlas or ontology expansion.

## Scope boundary

This document recommends an evidence source only. It does not design, authorize, or execute a reader study.
