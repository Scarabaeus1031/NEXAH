# Unresolved Action Questions

No question below authorizes method revision.

## Granularity

1. **Are “separate,” “distinguish,” and “classify” distinct actions or different descriptions of one editorial act?** Origin: Models, Matrices, and status registers use all three. Evidence: CA-05–CA-07, CA-16. Unresolved because artifacts preserve outputs, not drafting decisions. Required test: independent analysts annotate the same action history and compare boundaries.

## Necessity

2. **Which candidate actions are necessary for an inspectable result, and which exist because of the current template?** Origin: recurring 12/13-file pilot packages. Evidence: Artifact–Action Matrix and template recurrence in Meta Review. Unresolved because no governed ablation or alternate workflow exists. Required test: prospective comparison of complete traceability with different artifact packaging.

## Order

3. **Which actions require fixed order and which can iterate?** Origin: published OBSERVE→REPRESENT→COMPARE→ORIENT→EXPLAIN order versus revisits across Reflection/Neighborhood. Evidence: Sequence and Dependency Registers. Unresolved because publication order hides drafting loops. Required test: preserve a versioned action log in a future separately authorized case.

## Recurrence

4. **Which repeated actions belong to Orientation Translation rather than one analyst's habits?** Origin: all cases share analyst and prompt lineage. Evidence: 99-file corpus and Meta Review limitations. Unresolved because authorship independence is absent. Required test: blinded independent reconstruction.

## Representation

5. **Is separating representations the central action or one action among several co-dependent controls?** Origin: every pilot organizes around 8/6/7 representations. Evidence: Models, Matrices, Routes, Reflections. Unresolved because source fixation, rejection, evidence status, and stopping may be equally necessary. Required test: Reader Test plus independent process annotation; no method change.

## Synthesis

6. **Does the method intentionally delay synthesis, or does the artifact template merely publish synthesis later?** Origin: observation and representation precede maps/reports. Evidence: all pilot sequences; Growth reconstruction. Unresolved because unpublished provisional synthesis is invisible. Required test: prospective action log or analyst protocol study.

## Rejection

7. **Is rejection a distinct analytical action or the output of evidence control and relation admission?** Origin: rejection registers and non-transitivity audits. Evidence: all Neighborhoods. Unresolved because the same decision may be phrased as failed admission. Required test: compare independent decision logs.

## Reflection

8. **Does Reflection discover questions, record questions already noticed, or both?** Origin: three Reflections. Evidence: bounded hidden-question inventories. Unresolved because question timestamps and drafts are absent. Required test: prospective provenance for question formation.

## Stopping

9. **Is stopping an analytical action, an editorial decision, or a governance declaration?** Origin: three different stopping rationales. Evidence: Neighborhoods and Growth Study. Unresolved because the record combines sufficiency judgment, source boundary, and publication closure. Required test: independent stopping review across more cases.

## Reader relation

10. **Which candidate actions change reader orientation rather than only analyst inspectability?** Origin: Meta Review disposition. Evidence: Reader/Explain/Maps exist, but no reader study. Unresolved because artifact utility is not reader effect. Required test: predeclared Reader Test.

## Transfer

11. **Can another analyst perform comparable actions without copying the artifact template?** Origin: single-analyst corpus. Evidence: recurrence/template distinction. Unresolved because no independent execution exists. Required test: give only evidence boundaries and task, then compare action records without normalizing outputs.

## Priority questions

1. Which actions affect real readers rather than only auditability?
2. Which repeated actions recur with another analyst?
3. Is representation separation central or co-dependent?
4. Does synthesis truly occur late, or only appear late in publication?
5. Which action dependencies are necessary rather than chronological?
6. What is the action boundary between admission, rejection, and stopping?
7. Can artifact packaging change while preserving the same inspectable actions?
