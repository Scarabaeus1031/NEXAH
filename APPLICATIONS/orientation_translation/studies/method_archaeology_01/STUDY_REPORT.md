# WHAT ORIENTATION TRANSLATION ACTUALLY DOES
## Method Archaeology 01

## 1. Study question

This study reconstructs observable analytical actions across three completed Orientation Translation sequences and their method-development records. It excludes method redesign, effectiveness claims, canonical Operations, Operators, cognition, automation, scientific generalization, biological comparison extension, Atlas construction, and hypothesis formation.

## 2. Corpus and method

The pre-study corpus contains 99 Markdown files: the 89-file corpus audited by Meta Review 01 plus its ten new artifacts. It includes Pilots 01–03, their Reflections and Neighborhoods, Growth Study 01, Comparison Framework 01, completed Comparison 01, and Meta Review 01.

Actions were admitted only when visibly instructed/performed or reconstructable from exact before/after artifacts. Each candidate records input, output, reference, evidence class, recurrence, counterexample, template pressure, and prohibited inference. Artifact, action, sequence, dependency, and effect remain separate.

## 3. Candidate actions observed

### Present in all three pilots

Fix a source boundary; preserve source identity and limitations; extract observations; separate local representations and scales; compare without ranking; define reader questions; construct and explain bounded routes; mark unsupported implications and uncertainty; reflect on the completed Translation; record unanswered questions and candidate bridges; admit directional relations; reject transitive shortcuts; record negative findings; declare stopping; preserve traceability.

These are recurring descriptions, not a complete or canonical action list.

### Present in two pilots

Comprehensive exact reverse-direction testing and explicit prospective Process Rule auditing are present in P02/P03. A separate Source Representation artifact is also present only in those cases.

### Present in one pilot

Indirect-support admission is exercised only in P01. P03 uniquely makes phase-versus-state separation explicit, but that local distinction is not a general action category.

### Comparison-only

Freeze two evidence bodies; test scoped identity; compare roles and edges without merging; audit reciprocity across cases; preserve asymmetry and incomparability.

### Study-only

Reconstruct Growth rules; distinguish method recurrence from template recurrence; audit artifact behavior; issue a bounded method disposition.

### Template-imposed

The common five-stage OLS order, repeated filenames, table structures, Reader/Explain pairing, and several boundary headings recur by design. Their recurrence does not establish necessity.

### Uncertain

The boundaries among separating, classifying, rejecting, and stopping; whether Reflection generates or records questions; whether synthesis is cognitively delayed; and which actions transfer to another analyst.

## 4. Actions versus artifacts

A Reflection performs support separation, bridge classification, question recording, and limit declaration. A Neighborhood performs admission, rejection, direction testing, non-transitivity audit, and stopping. Conversely, uncertainty recording occurs in Source Records, Models, Explain Mode, Evaluation, Reflection, and Neighborhoods.

Artifact recurrence therefore does not prove action necessity, and action overlap does not prove artifact redundancy.

## 5. Action sequences

Published sequences consistently begin with source fixation and observation, then expose plural representations before route and explanation artifacts. Evaluation, Reflection, Neighborhood construction, and stopping occur later. P02/P03 make reverse auditing and rule testing explicit; Meta Review introduces recurrence/template discrimination.

Loops remain visible where Reflection revisits the Translation and Neighborhood admission revisits Reflection questions. Drafting loops are not recorded. The corpus supports stable publication ordering more strongly than a fixed analytical sequence.

## 6. Action dependencies

Explicit requirements include source fixation before traceable extraction, frozen Translation before Reflection, and immutable comparison inputs before correspondence. Observed temporal order includes observations before representation and rejection records before final stopping. Likely logical dependencies include representation before cross-representation comparison and scoped identity before edge correspondence. Shared artifact order is partly template dependency. No evidence makes the full sequence universally necessary.

## 7. Failure pressures

The candidate actions appear designed to resist source drift, premature synthesis, false equivalence, scale collapse, sequence-to-causality leakage, false reciprocity, route/path collapse, editorial evidence laundering, transitivity, graph completion, forced symmetry, hidden judgment, question-to-claim conversion, aesthetic stopping, explanation smoothing, and template overinterpretation.

The repository demonstrates that records were structured to contain these pressures. It does not demonstrate how readers interpret them or that the actions are effective outside this corpus.

## 8. Asymmetries

P01 lacks a separate Source Representation file and comprehensive reverse audit, uses ring geometry, and is reconstructed retrospectively. P02/P03 use asymmetric local structures and prospective rule registers. Indirect support appears only once. Bridge counts, stopping bases, explanation risks, and evaluation granularity differ. This chronology is retained rather than normalized.

## 9. The delayed-synthesis question

Status: **partly supported but template-dependent**.

Across the published corpus, observations and multiple bounded representations precede route-level, explanation-level, and report-level synthesis. Unsupported implications and limits are made visible before final neighborhood closure. But the shared workflow prescribes much of this order, and unpublished drafting may contain provisional synthesis. The finding is a bounded publication-pattern observation, not a new principle.

## 10. Smallest defensible characterization

The current workflow repeatedly fixes a source, records observations and limits, separates locally relevant representations, compares them without selecting one complete view, constructs bounded reader routes, preserves evidential and negative status, audits local relations, and declares where the investigation stops.

This description is provisional, non-atomic, and incomplete.

## 11. What cannot yet be determined

The corpus does not establish a minimal necessary action set, universal operation sequence, transfer to another analyst, reader effect, cognitive mechanism, cross-domain generality, artifact redundancy, computational implementation, or Operator status.

## 12. Relation to Reader Test

The inventory may help a future separately governed Reader Test identify observable questions: whether readers notice representation differences, preserve evidence boundaries, avoid causal overstatement, distinguish editorial routes from scientific relations, formulate bounded questions, and recognize stopping boundaries. It does not redesign or execute that test.

## 13. Failure-mode audit

The study kept artifact distinct from action, candidate label from Operator, recurrence from universality, template from independent behavior, time from necessity, analyst record from reader effect, and inspectability from correctness. It did not import Question Operator, punctuation, Janus, Micromouse, biological categories, artifact deletion, method revision, Atlas, or hypothesis claims. Final byte and Git verification remains the closing control.

## 14. Disposition

**CANDIDATE ACTION INVENTORY SUFFICIENT FOR READER-TEST DESIGN**

This means the provisional inventory can inform what a future Reader Test observes. It does not authorize that test, further method use, or method revision.
