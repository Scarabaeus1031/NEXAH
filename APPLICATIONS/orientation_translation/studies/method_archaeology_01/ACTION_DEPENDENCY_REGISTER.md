## Unsupported dependencies

The corpus does not establish that every analyst must use the same artifact order, that Reflection is cognitively necessary, that rejection must precede all stopping judgments, or that synthesis cannot occur provisionally during drafting. It establishes only the recorded and governed publication dependencies above.
