# Method Archaeology 01 — What Orientation Translation Actually Does

## Purpose

This study reconstructs the smallest observable analytical actions performed across the completed Orientation Translation corpus. It describes existing practice; it does not redesign, validate, or formalize the method.

## Governing question

> What observable analytical actions occur across the completed Orientation Translation workflow, and how do those actions relate to the existing artifacts?

A secondary question asks which actions recur, which are case-specific, and which may be effects of templates, chronology, or one analyst.

## Relation to Meta Review 01

Meta Review 01 remains authoritative and retains the disposition **METHOD REQUIRES LIMITED REVIEW BEFORE FURTHER USE**. This study neither overturns that disposition nor replaces its recommended Reader Test. It makes no effectiveness claim.

## Meaning of candidate action

An action is a traceable, observable act documented in one or more existing artifacts. “Candidate” means that its boundaries, necessity, recurrence, and transferability remain provisional.

**A CANDIDATE ACTION IS A TRACEABLE DESCRIPTION OF SOMETHING THE EXISTING WORKFLOW DOES. IT IS NOT A CANONICAL OPERATION OR ORIENTATION OPERATOR.**

## Boundary

Permitted: extract explicit or reconstructed actions; map actions to artifacts; preserve chronology, absence, asymmetry, evidence class, dependencies, and failure pressure.

Prohibited: method revision; canonical operation list; Operators; symbols or punctuation semantics; Question Operator; cognitive primitives; executable architecture; OLS/Registry/Kernel changes; scientific claims; biological comparison extension; Atlas; hypothesis; artifact removal; Reader Test execution; commit or push.
