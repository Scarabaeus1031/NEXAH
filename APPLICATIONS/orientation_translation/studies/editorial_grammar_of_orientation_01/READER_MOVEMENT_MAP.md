# Reader Movement Map

## Boundary

These are observed movement patterns, not a mandatory sequence and not evidence
of reader behavior. The corpus proposes routes; it has not measured whether
readers follow or benefit from them.

## Recurring movements

| Movement | Reader question/function | Common form | Sources | Variants and boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | Where can I begin? | question, audience, role, or source-object entry | C-06–C-08, C-19, C-21, C-23 | entry may be topic-, role-, mechanism-, or audience-based |
| Locate | Where am I and what representation is active? | current position, layer, scale, subsystem or stage | C-06–C-08, C-19–C-23 | location can be conceptual, spatial, documentary or architectural |
| Orient | What distinctions matter here? | essential distinctions, responsibility labels, context panel | C-04, C-06–C-08, C-20–C-23 | not a claim of canonical ORIENT execution |
| Compare | What is shared and what differs? | juxtaposition, matrix, contrast path, paired cases | C-01–C-05, C-09–C-12, C-17 | comparison basis must be stated; adjacency is insufficient |
| Deepen | Which representation should I follow? | mechanism, location, branch, evidence or scale route | C-06–C-08, C-19, C-23 | several paths coexist and are not ranked |
| Transition | How can I move to another bounded view? | labelled arrow, bridge, cross-link, next artifact | C-01, C-06–C-08, C-18–C-23 | may mean navigation, procedure or physical process; label required |
| Return | What changes when I revisit the question? | alternative route, reflection, revised view, return to map | C-06, C-13, C-17–C-19 | not present as a universal cycle |
| Cross-link | Which independent space is relevant next? | companion work, bridge candidate, repository link | C-06–C-10, C-19–C-23, C-26 | link does not merge identities, evidence or authority |
| Stop | What cannot be inferred, and where does this study end? | limitation, non-implication, stopping rule, disposition | C-02–C-18, C-20–C-25 | local sufficiency, not final knowledge |
| Next question | What remains open? | unresolved question, alternative, reopening condition | C-06, C-08, C-13–C-19 | question is not acceptance or roadmap commitment |

## Observed variants

### Scientific reader route

```text
source boundary → representation choice → comparison → limitation → stop
```

Visible in C-04 and C-06–C-11.

### Library route

```text
reader position → curated entry → alternative paths → companion work → explain
```

Visible in C-19 and parts of C-21–C-23.

### Review route

```text
candidate → evidence classification → counterexample → disposition → open question
```

Visible in C-02–C-03 and C-09–C-18.

### Repository route

```text
visitor role → subsystem responsibility → authority boundary → owned README
```

Visible in C-20–C-21 and C-26.

## Non-sequence finding

No single route contains every movement. Comparison may precede orientation in
one artifact and follow source orientation in another. Return and interruption
are strong in some book/editorial material but absent from conformance and
technical portals. The family supports multiple movements, not one grammar
sentence.
