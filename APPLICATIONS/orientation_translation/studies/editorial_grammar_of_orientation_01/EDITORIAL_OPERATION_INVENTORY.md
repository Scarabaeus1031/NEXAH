# Editorial Operation Inventory

## Decision rule

`recurring` requires traceable appearance in multiple artifacts and at least
two artifact classes. `local` remains tied to one artifact/series or visual
style. `ambiguous` recurs visually but lacks stable relation semantics.

Twenty candidates were identified: thirteen recurring and seven local or
ambiguous. Labels are review descriptions, not canonical Operators.

## Admission result

- **Recurring:** EO-01 through EO-13 — 13
- **Local or ambiguous:** EO-14 through EO-20 — 7

The family is an observed repertoire. It is not a closed vocabulary, ordered
grammar, operator registry, or claim that every artifact uses every operation.
