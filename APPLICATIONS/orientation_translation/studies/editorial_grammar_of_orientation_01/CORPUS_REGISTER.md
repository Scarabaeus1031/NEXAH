# Corpus Register

## Inclusion rule

Include artifacts that explicitly relate multiple representations, reader
routes, evidence states, scales, or repository responsibilities. Include OLS
only to police the boundary between normative semantics and observed editorial
practice. Prefer completed review artifacts over unrestricted visual mining.

## Explicit exclusions

| Area/artifact | Reason for exclusion |
|---|---|
| complete Are.na book and plate corpus | not locally reviewable at reliable page/plate granularity within this bounded pass |
| Language Books, The Lens, Handbook, Living Equation as fresh primary evidence | existing cross-work reviews were included; a new book survey would enlarge the corpus beyond the prompt's reliability boundary |
| full Architecture and Editorial visual archives | two current, differently purposed visuals were sampled; historical and vision visuals could inflate recurrence through shared style |
| raw scientific datasets and validation images | they test scientific or implementation claims, not reader-facing editorial operations in this review |
| Repository Evolution Review 01 | no matching maintained artifact was located in the current repository scan; Documentation Alignment records its reported conclusions |
| source documents referenced by the three public pilots | frozen pilot artifacts already preserve their source boundaries; this review does not reopen scientific content |

## Corpus limitation

The corpus supports repository-level recurrence, not independence from the
NEXAH editorial lineage. Shared authorship, prompts, templates and visual style
remain confounds.
