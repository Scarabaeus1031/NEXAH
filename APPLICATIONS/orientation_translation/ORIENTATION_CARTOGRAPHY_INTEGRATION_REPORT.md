# Orientation Cartography Integration Report

## Outcome

The integration establishes three separate records:

1. a recurring historical orientation-cartographic practice across selected NEXAH artifacts;
2. a provisional, explicitly audited study-to-map reconstruction workflow;
3. two immutable publication artifacts for the Solar–Lunar–Carbon worked example.

No canonical architecture or implementation was changed.

## Historical artifacts inspected

The bounded inspection included archived Regime, Navigation Grammar, and Applications maps; current Orientation Laboratory and Living Library visuals; Library reader-journey and sequence reviews; Orientation Translation pilot maps, neighborhoods, and comparisons; Planetary Chemistry Atlas 01; the Solar–Lunar–Carbon review; and selected vision artifacts as ambiguity controls. The archived Gate Geometry path was checked but its repository object is an empty text placeholder rather than an inspectable image.

## Admitted candidates

Strong candidates include:

- NEXAH Regime Atlas;
- NEXAH Navigation Grammar;
- Library Reader Journey Review and verified reader paths;
- Photosynthesis Orientation Map and Neighborhood;
- Photosynthesis–Cellular Respiration Comparison;
- Planetary Chemistry Atlas 01;
- Solar–Lunar–Carbon Evidence Map;
- Solar–Lunar–Carbon Museum Edition as a derivative publication layer.

Moderate candidates include Applications Navigation, Living Library, and Orientation Laboratory maps. Their routing or layering is real, but they are narrower, denser, or partly flowchart-like.

## Rejected or ambiguous

- the Planetary Orientation source sketch remains an important but boundary-weak precursor;
- Living Concept Graph remains a vision artifact;
- Gate Geometry Navigation is uninspectable in its present repository form and therefore excluded;
- linear pipelines, dashboards, ordinary contents lists, isolated scientific plots, decorative covers, and aesthetic collages were excluded;
- no Work was admitted as a whole-book map from title or form alone.

## Recurring functions

The corpus repeatedly locates, relates, differentiates, routes, bounds, scales, orients, translates, and supports return. Opening unknown territory appears historically, but only the current prototype gives it a fully audited spatial publication form.

## Relation to the Library

A book containing maps is established. Some books functioning as maps are plausible but require page-level review. The Library functioning as an atlas is supported as an editorial architecture. The total corpus functioning as an orientation environment is plausible but not yet demonstrated end to end.

## Historical continuity

Fields, regimes, gates, layers, paths, atlases, multiple views, and return recur before the phrase Orientation Cartography. The new work therefore does not originate cartographic practice.

## What is genuinely new

The Solar–Lunar–Carbon production records a traceable chain from bounded review through relation and boundary registers to Evidence Map, audit, correction, immutable final, and derivative Museum Edition. It also makes two controls explicit:

- evidence asymmetry must survive visual translation;
- relational distance is qualitative unless measured.
