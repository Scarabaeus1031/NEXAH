# Comparison Scope

## Research question

What correspondences, differences, asymmetries, and unresolved relations can be established between the independently frozen Photosynthesis and Cellular Respiration orientation sequences without collapsing their identities or exceeding their evidence boundaries?

## Comparison object

Two complete versioned sequences are compared externally:

- Pilot 01: `Source → Translation → Reflection → Neighborhood`
- Pilot 02: `Source → Translation → Reflection → Neighborhood`

“Independent” here means independently fixed sources and independently constructed local records. It does not mean independent authorship or inter-analyst agreement.

## Included questions

The comparison asks whether source records, representations, reader questions and paths, local roles, nodes, directional edges, support classes, reverse-edge statuses, rejections, stopping decisions, hidden questions, candidate bridges, evidence boundaries, and process-rule outcomes can be compared along declared dimensions.

It specifically tests:

1. Which candidate referents pass a scoped identity test?
2. Can identical referents occupy different roles?
3. Can distinct referents occupy locally comparable roles without identity?
4. Which representation purposes correspond partially, asymmetrically, or not at all?
5. Do any reviewed directional edges correspond after endpoint, predicate, scale, and support tests?
6. Does the candidate Photosynthesis ↔ Cellular Respiration relation pass four local direction tests?
7. Which differences are substantive, source-driven, procedural, or editorial?
8. Which attempted comparisons remain incomparable in these versions?
9. Is the procedure sufficiently complete for further comparison testing?

## Excluded evidence and questions

The following are not evidence: visual resemblance; identical words alone; knowledge absent from the frozen sources; linked external pages; later revisions; intuitive biological cycles; cultural or symbolic associations; NEXAH-wide concepts absent from either case; Earth–Sun–Moon interpretations; universal operator claims.

The comparison does not ask whether the biological processes form one system, whether either source is scientifically correct, whether one graph is better, whether a universal process pattern exists, or whether an Atlas should be created.

## Governing boundaries

- Correspondence creates no shared node or graph.
- Identity, role, function, scale, position, and direction are separate dimensions.
- Support status is never upgraded by cross-pilot resemblance.
- A local path is not a direct edge.
- A forward edge does not supply its reverse.
- Negative results remain records.
- Two cases cannot establish recurrence as an Orientation Hypothesis.

Where a recurring distinction appears, its only permitted wording is:

> Candidate recurring distinction observed across two cases. Insufficient basis for Orientation Hypothesis.
