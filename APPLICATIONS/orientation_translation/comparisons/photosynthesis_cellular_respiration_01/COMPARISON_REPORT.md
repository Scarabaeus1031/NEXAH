# PHOTOSYNTHESIS ↔ CELLULAR RESPIRATION
## Orientation Comparison 01

## Result

Two complete, fixed application sequences were compared: each includes a source record, translation, Reflection, and Orientation Neighborhood. All used inputs passed SHA-256 preflight. The records are independent in source identity and local construction, but not independently authored or externally validated.

Available objects included source identities, eight versus six representations, three versus four reader paths, local roles, 31/30 versus 21/27 node-edge records, support classes, rejections, stopping decisions, bridge candidates, and process-rule outcomes.

## Findings

Scoped identity was established for ATP, oxygen, ATP synthase, cellular respiration, and photosynthesis. Carbon dioxide, water, electron transport, proton gradient, and carrier classes received only partial identity. Biological inputs, energy representations, compartments, stages, and cellular-context objects were functional analogies rather than identities. Photosynthetic output and respiratory waste classes had no meaningful correspondence.

Roles corresponded most usefully at the level of transformation, carrier, coupling, location, bridge candidate, and stopping boundary, while their occupants and scales often differed. Oxygen and ATP show why referent identity cannot determine role. Wider ecological, agricultural, climate, evolutionary, and scientific-history roles are strongly asymmetric.

Representation comparison produced partial correspondences around electron transfer, ordered transformation, spatial organization, energy coupling, and quantitative boundary. Conditional branching is explicit only in Pilot 02. Pilot 01's ecological, evolutionary, agricultural, climate, and history lenses have no one-to-one Pilot 02 representation.

No confirmed edge correspondence was established. Electron transport → proton gradient is a strong partial correspondence, but it connects only partially identical local process instances. Other candidates were functional analogies, asymmetric relations, or no matches.

Reciprocity was not established anywhere. Photosynthesis → Cellular Respiration is admitted in Pilot 01 as a bounded neighboring-representation edge. Pilot 02 contains only a narrow mention and rejects Photosynthesis as a local node. The four local direction tests therefore yield asymmetric mentions and a candidate bridge, not a cycle.

The principal asymmetries concern scale coverage, representation purpose, topology, indirect support, reverse-edge auditing, candidate admission, rejection granularity, and stopping rationale. These differences make the comparison more informative and make graph union indefensible.

## Framework assessment

The framework remained usable. It forced identity before edge comparison, preserved negative results, exposed false reciprocity, and kept source evidence separate from editorial comparison. Its limitations are equally clear: one analyst produced both pilots and this comparison; the sources are tertiary; Pilot 01 lacks a complete reverse-edge audit; and the procedure has not been independently reproduced.

The results are ready for another reviewed comparison, not for Atlas review. Human approval, versioning of this comparison, independent rerun, inter-analyst agreement, further cases, and Atlas governance remain absent.

## Candidate recurring distinctions

Candidate recurring distinction observed across two cases. Insufficient basis for Orientation Hypothesis.

- entity identity versus functional role;
- sequence versus space;
- mechanism versus consequence;
- local process versus cross-scale context;
- direct relation versus compressed pathway.
