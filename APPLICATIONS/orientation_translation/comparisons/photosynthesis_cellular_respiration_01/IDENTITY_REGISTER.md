## Summary

The register establishes five scoped identities, five partial identities, five functional analogies, and one no-match result. There are no unresolved identity records because every tested candidate received a bounded disposition. Apparent identity failed most clearly for output/waste classes, biological inputs, stage structures, and compartment structures.
