# Evaluation

## Demonstrated

- Two hash-verified application sequences can be inventoried without normalization.
- Candidate identity can be tested separately from local role, scale, and graph position.
- Edge candidates can retain both endpoint identity limits and local support status.
- The four-question reciprocity procedure prevents a one-way mention from becoming a cycle.
- Asymmetry, incomparability, rejection, and no-match results can remain first-class outputs.
- The framework is usable for one bounded comparison and preserves the original files.

## Plausibly enabled

- repeated pairwise comparisons with the same auditable discipline;
- a provisional comparison collection that stores external correspondence records;
- later inter-analyst comparison of identity, role, and stopping decisions;
- Atlas-method testing after versioning and human review;
- evaluation of whether certain comparison dimensions recur across more cases.

These are capabilities suggested by the record structure, not demonstrated outcomes.

## Not demonstrated

| Question | Status | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| input independence | source fixation demonstrated; authorship independence not demonstrated | independently authored pilots |
| comparison traceability | demonstrated internally | external audit of every entailment |
| identity testing correctness | procedure demonstrated | domain experts and independent reviewers |
| role separation correctness | internally consistent | inter-analyst agreement |
| edge-level scientific validity | not demonstrated | source-fidelity and domain review |
| reciprocity beyond recorded scope | not demonstrated | independently admitted reverse relations |
| asymmetry completeness | not demonstrated | repeated comparison and reviewer sampling |
| negative-result preservation | demonstrated in files | governance across later revisions |
| framework general usability | plausible only | comparisons in unrelated domains |
| analyst independence | not demonstrated | separate analysts and blinded reconciliation |
| comparison reproducibility | not demonstrated | independent rerun from the manifest |
| Atlas readiness | not demonstrated | versioned comparison, review approval, repeated cases, Atlas governance |
| hypothesis readiness | not demonstrated | multiple independent cases, explicit hypothesis protocol and testing |

Two cases do not demonstrate universal recurrence. Internal consistency does not demonstrate correctness. No reader study measures whether the comparison improves orientation.
