# Publication Layers

These layers are provisional. Not all are implemented.

| Layer | Purpose | Required fidelity | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research Layer | Reports, matrices, registers, limitations, and source records | complete reasoning and evidence boundaries | implemented in several Orientation Translation studies |
| Evidence Map | Direct spatial reconstruction of reviewed relations | relation types, non-edges, asymmetry, and open questions inspectable | one approved worked prototype |
| Reader Map | Simplified evidence-faithful navigation for a defined reader task | fewer details without changing admitted relations | present in textual pilot paths; not yet tested as a visual publication class |
| Museum Edition | Atmospheric and cultural translation for public entry | traceable to an Evidence Map; same admitted relations and boundaries | one approved derivative prototype |
| Atlas | Curated collection of related maps with separately preserved evidence bases | map identity, comparison rationale, and Atlas admission review | comparative atlas studies exist; audited map collection not yet established |
| Atlas of Atlases | Higher-order orientation structure connecting multiple atlases | must not merge distinct scales, authorities, or mechanisms | Library concept/Work exists; method layer not implemented |

Publication layers do not form a maturity ladder. A research report may remain the correct final form. A Museum Edition cannot replace the Evidence Map, and an Atlas cannot repair insufficient comparison evidence.
