# Cartographic Dimensions

Possible qualitative dimensions include:

| Dimension | Placement question | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence strength | How directly is a relation supported? | Not a universal numeric score. |
| Relation type | Is the relation direct, indirect, environmental, editorial, or open? | Different types must not be collapsed into one line. |
| Scale | At what biological, planetary, temporal, or conceptual level does a representation operate? | Cross-scale placement does not establish a mechanism. |
| Conceptual proximity | Which representations are useful to compare? | Similarity is not identity or causality. |
| Reader movement | Which entries, alternatives, bridges, returns, and stops are offered? | A reader path is an editorial construction. |
| Uncertainty | Where are support, interpretation, or completeness unresolved? | Uncertainty is not merely “distance from truth.” |
| Boundary strength | Is a transition admitted, weak, open, rejected, or not reviewed? | Absence and rejection must be distinguished. |
| Degree of integration | How much of a representation participates in the current space? | Integration is scope-local, not inherent importance. |
| Openness | Which regions remain available for later research? | Open territory is not an invitation to invent. |
| Historical sequence | When did an artifact or relation enter the corpus? | Chronology does not imply conceptual progress. |

These dimensions may inform placement. **They are not automatically measurable axes.** Any numeric coordinate, distance, area, or scale requires its own declared measurement model and evidence.
