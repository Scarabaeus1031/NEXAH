# Reconstruction Workflow

> Provisional. One worked prototype. Not a canonical method.

| Phase | Input | Operation | Output | Failure condition | Audit requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Question | bounded research question | state what the map must orient and must not claim | scoped question | question implies a desired theory or visual answer | verify prohibited conclusions |
| Bounded Corpus | named sources and versions | freeze admissible evidence | corpus manifest | open-ended source growth or unrecorded substitution | source and version check |
| Review | bounded corpus | separate supported, indirect, weak, open, and rejected material | completed review | inference silently becomes evidence | claim-to-source trace |
| Relation Matrix | review findings | declare endpoints, direction, type, support, boundary | relation register | relation lacks why/support/non-claim | row completeness audit |
| Boundary Register | review findings and rejections | record non-edges, stopping points, unknowns | boundary register | silence is mistaken for permission | prohibited-edge audit |
| Orientation Space | matrices and registers | group only sufficiently coherent regions while preserving asymmetry | bounded relational space | one mechanism is inferred from co-placement | region and scale audit |
| Evidence Map | orientation space | translate status into line, region, density, and empty-space rules | research-facing map | visual hierarchy contradicts evidence hierarchy | relation crosswalk |
| Visual Audit | Evidence Map and source records | inspect every edge, omission, label, proximity, and legend | audit findings | an unsupported implication remains | independent checklist review |
| Correction Loop | audit findings and current map | correct only against registered evidence and boundaries | corrected candidate | aesthetics overrides source requirement | correction log with rationale |
| Approved Evidence Map | corrected candidate | verify dimensions, checksum, labels, relations, and non-edges | immutable final image | mismatch or unresolved critical finding | file and relation verification |
| Museum / Reader Edition | approved Evidence Map | translate atmosphere and entry without changing admitted relations | public-facing derivative | a metaphor adds/strengthens a relation | design translation audit |
| Derivative Atlas | multiple approved related maps | curate without merging evidence bases | possible Atlas | visual family is mistaken for comparative validity | separate Atlas admission review |

```text
Question
   ↓
Bounded Corpus
   ↓
Review
   ↓
Relation Matrix
   ↓
Boundary Register
   ↓
Orientation Space
   ↓
Evidence Map
   ↓
Visual Audit
   ↓
Correction Loop
   ↓
Approved Evidence Map
   ↓
Museum / Reader Edition
   ↓
Derivative Atlas
```

Stopping is valid at any phase. An incomplete or rejected map is preferable to a visually complete misrepresentation.
