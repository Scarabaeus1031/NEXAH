# Design Translation Register

| Research property | Source requirement | Evidence Map translation | Museum Edition translation | Editorial interpretation | Aesthetic expression | Reader risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strong supported chain | retain ordered supported relations and intermediates | solid explicit backbone | central mountain route | use as primary entry | dark continuous ridge/path | inevitability or universality |
| Comparative context | keep separate scales and mechanisms | separate planetary region, softer links | neighboring continent | related but not merged | muted blue landmass | stronger relation inferred from adjacency |
| Weak lunar branch | few local relations; no biological bridge | sparse isolated region | isolated island | incompleteness is meaningful | small pale lunar landmass | isolation read as irrelevance or absoluteness |
| Open questions | remain unresolved and named | broken/open relations in a question region | sparse unexplored territory | invite research without admission | blank paper, faint routes | mystery aesthetic overwhelms uncertainty |
| Not admitted | no connection | absence of line | empty distance/no bridge | preserve prohibition through silence | open space | absence overlooked or filled imaginatively |
| Evidence asymmetry | unequal support must remain visible | unequal density and line strength | unequal connectivity and occupation | resist visual balancing | different region weights | area read quantitatively |
| Qualitative relation | no physical metric | diagrammatic placement | compass and qualitative distance note | landscape encodes orientation only | atlas convention without scale bar | map read as geography |
| Publication change | admitted relations unchanged | research legibility | cultural accessibility | simplify atmosphere, not evidence | old-atlas landscape | authority or age inferred from style |

The source requirement governs. Editorial interpretation and aesthetic expression are reversible choices. Reader risk remains part of the artifact record.
