## Corrected misrepresentation

An earlier draft contained a physical kilometer scale. It was rejected because no measurable geographic distance exists in the reviewed orientation space. The final image replaces it with:

> **RELATIONAL DISTANCE IS QUALITATIVE**

## Reader risks

- the mountain range may imply inevitability;
- an island may be read as irrelevance rather than weak current connection;
- unexplored territory may aestheticize missing evidence;
- continent size may be read as importance or quantity;
- old-atlas atmosphere may imply historical authority.

These are controlled by the subtitle, legend, asymmetry note, qualitative-distance statement, and traceability to the Evidence Map.

## Approval

Approved as a public-facing derivative of the Evidence Map, not as independent research evidence.
