# Orientation Translation

Orientation Translation is a non-canonical applied research program for constructing evidence-bounded reader orientation from fixed public representations. It remains physically in Applications and conceptually participates in Research. It does not define OLS semantics or Kernel behavior.

## Program areas

- `pilots/` — fixed-source Orientation Translation executions;
- `neighborhoods/` — bounded local relation spaces derived from pilots;
- `comparisons/` — comparisons that preserve independent inputs;
- `reviews/` — reflection and cross-pilot analysis;
- `studies/` — methodological and domain-bounded research;
- `methods/` — explicitly provisional method captures, not canonical standards;
- `visualizations/` — approved or audited visual publication packages.

## Authority boundary

Research results here may document recurring practices, provisional workflows, and bounded examples. They do not allocate Registry identity, introduce Operators, alter the Orientation Language, or change the maintained Kernel unless a separate authorized process does so.
