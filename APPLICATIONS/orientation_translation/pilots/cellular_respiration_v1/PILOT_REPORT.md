# CELLULAR RESPIRATION
## Orientation Translation Pilot 02

## Purpose

Pilot 02 produces a second independently frozen application sequence:

`Source Fixation → Orientation Translation → Reflection → Neighborhood Construction`

It tests candidate process rules without asserting reproducibility, performing cross-pilot comparison, constructing an Atlas, or formulating an Orientation Hypothesis.

## 1. What source representation was fixed?

English Wikipedia's **Cellular respiration**, page ID `102871`, revision `1355541234`, timestamp `2026-05-22T13:08:03Z`, MediaWiki SHA-1 `c96af996f65545bcbadf4d732804efcb109aaee9`, accessed 17 July 2026.

The source is a tertiary, collaboratively edited representation. Visible maintenance signals, uneven section depth, multiple ATP-yield figures, eukaryotic/aerobic emphasis, and limited ecological coverage remain explicit limitations. No external biological source repaired or supplemented it.

## 2. What orientation object was constructed?

A source-bounded general-reader orientation around cellular respiration as fuel oxidation, electron transfer, material transformation, spatial organization, energy coupling, conditional branching, ATP use, and quantitative uncertainty.

The object is not a complete metabolic network, scientific review, ontology, or physiological model.

## 3. Which representations emerged?

1. Electron transfer
2. Transformation sequence
3. Spatial organization
4. Energy coupling
5. Conditional branching
6. Yield boundary

These six representations emerged from the source's own mixture of equation, pathway, cycle, compartments, membrane-gradient mechanism, branches, and accounting. Their separation is editorial, not canonical.

## 4. Which editorial decisions were introduced?

- six representation boundaries;
- a general-reader starting position;
- four question-led, non-ranked paths;
- bundling NADH/FADH2 as “reduced carriers” while preserving ambiguity;
- bundling chemically different outputs as a local “waste products” role;
- one explicitly bounded molecular-to-cellular edge from ATP to cellular work;
- exclusion of enzyme-level detail and most intermediates;
- refusal to resolve ATP numbers or citation limitations;
- an asymmetric neighborhood with no prescribed rings.

## 5. Which reader paths were produced?

- **Follow the transfer** — from fuel and carriers through gradient to ATP.
- **Locate each operation** — cytosol, matrix, and inner membrane in the source's eukaryotic account.
- **Separate the branches** — aerobic respiration, anaerobic respiration, and fermentation.
- **Audit the ATP number** — assumptions, transport, leak, shuttle differences, and unresolved totals.

Every path records prerequisites, expected orientation gain, limits, an alternative route, and prohibited implications. None is optimal or recommended.

## 6. What remained unsupported?

- a universal respiratory pathway or mitochondrial layout;
- one correct ATP yield selected by the pilot;
- complete regulation, measurement, evolutionary, physiological, clinical, or ecological accounts;
- exact entry of alternative fuels into the pathway;
- reciprocity of directional relations;
- transitive shortcut edges;
- identity between cellular respiration and breathing;
- food-web, climate, or global carbon-cycle conclusions;
- measured reader benefit;
- cross-pilot similarity, comparison, or Atlas eligibility;
- Earth–Sun–Moon patterns, universal functional triads, recurring operator classes, universal transition roles, cross-domain equivalence, or cosmological analogy.

## 7. What did Reflection reveal?

Reflection showed that:

- the source interleaves six explanatory purposes;
- ATP occupies several local roles rather than one simple output role;
- “without oxygen” does not adequately separate fermentation and anaerobic respiration;
- sequence and location are compatible but non-identical maps;
- yield disagreement is first an evidence-and-assumption boundary;
- source support weakens in several later contextual and efficiency claims;
- hidden questions can be named without importing answers;
- source-supported, editorial, and unsupported bridges remain separable.

Reflection remained provisionally distinct because it generated a relation-and-question inventory without rewriting the frozen translation.

## 8. What neighborhood was admitted?

An asymmetric local structure with:

- 21 nodes;
- 27 directional edges;
- 27 Direct Source Support edges;
- 0 Indirect Source Support edges;
- 0 admitted Editorial Bridges;
- explicit reverse-edge status for every admitted edge;
- 13 recorded rejection classes/examples;
- one explicitly audited cross-scale edge.

The structure follows a central process definition, an aerobic transformation/coupling chain, anaerobic and fermentation branches, spatial locations, ATP-supported cellular work, and variable outputs. It does not reproduce Pilot 01's shape.

## 9. Why did the neighborhood stop?

The local question is sufficiently represented once a reader can inspect the source's definition, principal process chain, spatial mechanism, energy coupling, branch distinctions, output role, and evidence limits. Additional enzyme detail would turn orientation into pathway encyclopedism. Ecological, measurement, regulatory, physiological, and alternative-fuel expansions require a new source audit.

The stop is epistemic, editorial, and practical. Another analyst could reasonably split or omit some bundled nodes. The 21/27 size is neither optimal nor stable by virtue of being finite.

## 11. Which procedures failed or changed?

No procedure failed decisively. Two required explicit modification:

1. Status vocabulary needed a local “source-supported relation” subtype in Reflection while preserving the same authority level.
2. Freeze discipline had to distinguish the translation **core** from the additive final process report so Reflection and Neighborhood could remain post-pilot analyses without rewriting core artifacts.

The indirect-support rule was not applicable because no indirect edge was needed. This remains untested rather than reproduced.

## 13. Is the sequence ready for independent comparison?

**Comparison-ready with limitations.** The source, representations, paths, statuses, directional edges, reverse statuses, rejections, stopping decision, and hashes are explicit enough for a later comparison protocol. Limitations include one tertiary source, no independent source-fidelity review, bundled node classes, no independently authored construction, and no test of indirect admission.

This readiness judgment concerns record quality only. It creates no node identity, edge correspondence, role match, reciprocity comparison, asymmetry finding, or Atlas record.
