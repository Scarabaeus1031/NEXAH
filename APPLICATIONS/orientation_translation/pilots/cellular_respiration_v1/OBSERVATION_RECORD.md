## Pre-orientation tensions

| ID | Tension | Status | Prohibited move |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-01 | Overall reaction versus multi-stage mechanism | Process observation | Do not treat the equation as the complete mechanism. |
| T-02 | Linear sequence versus cyclic and membrane processes | Process observation | Do not force every representation into one line. |
| T-03 | ATP production versus ATP investment and transport cost | Process observation | Do not describe ATP only as an output. |
| T-04 | Oxygen absence versus fermentation versus anaerobic respiration | Process observation | Do not collapse distinct branches into “anaerobic.” |
| T-05 | Nominal versus realized ATP yield | Process observation | Do not select one number without external adjudication. |
| T-06 | Molecular mechanism versus limited organismal/ecosystem examples | Process observation | Do not infer a complete cross-scale account. |

The choice to foreground these tensions is an **editorial procedure**. None is promoted to a new scientific claim.
