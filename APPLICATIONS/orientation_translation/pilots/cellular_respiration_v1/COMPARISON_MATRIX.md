# Comparison Matrix

This compares the six local representations; it does not compare Pilot 02 with any other pilot.

| Representation | Primary object | Explanatory purpose | Dominant scale | Compatible with | Missing or weak transition | Must not imply |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Electron transfer | Electrons, carriers, acceptor | Follow transfer | Molecular | Coupling; branching | Full carrier detail across every respiratory mode | That all acceptors or chains are equivalent |
| Transformation sequence | Substrates and pathway stages | Follow declared changes | Molecular to subcellular | Space; coupling | Regulation and network alternatives | That biology is a single linear pipeline |
| Spatial organization | Cytosol, matrix, inner membrane, prokaryotic cytoplasm | Locate operations | Cellular/subcellular | Sequence; coupling | Complete cross-organism comparison | That location alone explains mechanism |
| Energy coupling | ATP, substrate-level phosphorylation, gradient, ATP synthase | Relate energy release to accessible ATP | Molecular/membrane | Transfer; sequence; yield | Independently validated efficiency | That ATP is created without inputs or losses |
| Conditional branching | Aerobic respiration, fermentation, anaerobic respiration | Separate alternatives | Molecular to organism examples | Transfer; sequence | Full environmental and evolutionary context | That all oxygen-independent processes are the same |
| Yield boundary | ATP accounts, transport, leak, shuttle differences | Preserve quantitative variation | Molecular accounting | Coupling; space | Reconciled quantitative conclusion | That one cited number is universal |

## Compatible descriptions

- **Process observation:** electron transfer and energy coupling overlap but answer different questions: what moves versus how a gradient is used.
- **Process observation:** sequence and spatial organization can be aligned stage by stage, yet location is not the sequence itself.
- **Process observation:** branching reuses some early transformations while diverging by acceptor and later handling.
- **Process observation:** yield differences become more intelligible when transport and membrane organization are visible.

## Incompatible or unequal scopes

- **Process observation:** the overall reaction compresses detail that the stage representation expands.
- **Process observation:** the source's mitochondrial account cannot be silently transferred to all prokaryotic respiration.
- **Process observation:** a plant/ecosystem CO2 statement cannot support the molecular mechanism in reverse.
- **Process observation:** nominal ATP totals and realized-yield discussions coexist at unequal evidential strength.

## Missing transitions

- regulation between demand and pathway activity;
- measurement from experimental observation to ATP-yield claim;
- systematic organism-to-ecosystem scaling;
- a complete mapping from alternative fuels into the glucose-centered sequence;
- source-wide reconciliation of aerobic, anaerobic, and fermentation terminology.

These are recorded gaps. They are not answered with outside sources.
