# Cellular Respiration Orientation Translation Pilot v1

This is the second independent Orientation Translation application. It asks whether one fixed tertiary representation of **Cellular Respiration** can be made more navigable while preserving its own evidence boundaries.

The pilot applies the established sequence:

`OBSERVE → REPRESENT → COMPARE → ORIENT → EXPLAIN`

It does not copy the substantive lenses, paths, topology, scale structure, or conclusions of Pilot 01. The fixed source is English Wikipedia revision `1355541234`.

## Status and authority

- **Application status:** informative, source-bounded OLS-guided pilot
- **Source status:** public, collaboratively edited tertiary representation
- **OLS release:** `OLS-RELEASE-1.0.0`, used only within its published scope
- **Scientific authority:** not claimed
- **Conformance:** not claimed; no independent OLS-5 assessment
- **Reader outcome:** not measured
- **Selection:** no route is ranked, selected, or declared optimal

## Independent representation structure

Six representations emerged from the fixed source:

1. **Electron transfer** — fuels, carriers, and terminal acceptors.
2. **Transformation sequence** — glycolysis through oxidative phosphorylation.
3. **Spatial organization** — cytosol, mitochondrial matrix, and inner membrane.
4. **Energy coupling** — substrate-level phosphorylation and proton-gradient-driven ATP synthesis.
5. **Conditional branching** — aerobic respiration, fermentation, and anaerobic respiration.
6. **Yield boundary** — theoretical totals, realized losses, transport, leak, and variation.

These are editorial representations of the source, not biological entities or a complete model of respiration.
