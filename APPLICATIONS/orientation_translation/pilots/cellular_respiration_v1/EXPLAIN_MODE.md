# Explain Mode

Explain Mode exposes how Reader Mode was produced.

## Construction record

| Reader element | Transformation | Evidence | Editorial choice | Uncertainty preserved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Transfer route | Reorders source statements around electron and energy transfer | Lead; citric acid cycle; oxidative phosphorylation | Treat transfer as one orientation question | Carrier and organism variation omitted |
| Spatial route | Aligns stages with stated locations | Glycolysis; pyruvate oxidation; citric acid cycle; oxidative phosphorylation | Begin with the source's eukaryotic sequence | Prokaryotic spatial detail is incomplete |
| Branch route | Places three source distinctions side by side | Lead; fermentation; anaerobic respiration | Use acceptor status as comparison basis | Coverage and citations are unequal |
| Yield route | Collects dispersed assumptions and totals | Aerobic summary; Efficiency | Refuse to resolve competing figures | Source contains multiple estimates and maintenance signals |
| Pause questions | Converts source tensions into inspection prompts | Observation and comparison records | Ask rather than answer missing transitions | Reader usefulness untested |

## Transformations

1. The source was fixed to one revision.
2. Observations were recorded without importing external corrections.
3. Six representations were separated by explanatory purpose.
4. Comparisons preserved scale, scope, and evidence differences.
5. Four non-ranked paths were composed around reader questions.
6. Reader Mode compressed the result; Explain Mode preserves its construction.

## Omitted interpretations

- cellular respiration as a universal model of energy in life;
- a complete ecosystem carbon account;
- a physiological breathing model;
- medical or performance guidance;
- a resolved ATP-yield standard;
- universal pathway or operator classes;
- cross-domain analogies;
- any Photosynthesis-derived relation.

## Status and authority

- Biological statements: **Source-supported observation** within the fixed tertiary revision.
- Six representations and four paths: **Editorial procedure**.
- Cross-representation tensions: **Process observation**.
- Rules later tested in the sequence audit: **Candidate growth rule**.
- Any external correction or universalization: **Unsupported interpretation**.

The explanation makes the choices inspectable. It does not convert them into proof.
