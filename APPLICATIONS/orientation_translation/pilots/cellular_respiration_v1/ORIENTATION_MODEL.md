# Orientation Model

## Reader position

- **Editorial procedure:** curious general reader with basic cell and molecule vocabulary.
- **Not assumed:** specialist biochemistry, quantitative thermodynamics, or prior pathway mastery.
- **Reader question:** “Which representation helps me locate the process I am trying to understand?”

## Six source-emergent representations

| ID | Representation | Orientation question | Principal source basis | Scale | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Electron transfer | What moves from fuel toward an acceptor? | Lead; oxidative phosphorylation; anaerobic respiration | Molecular | Not one universal chain for all organisms |
| R-02 | Transformation sequence | What changes, and in what declared order? | Glycolysis; pyruvate oxidation; citric acid cycle; oxidative phosphorylation | Molecular to subcellular | Ordered teaching path simplifies network structure |
| R-03 | Spatial organization | Where do the transformations occur? | Glycolysis; pyruvate oxidation; citric acid cycle; oxidative phosphorylation | Cellular and subcellular | Eukaryotic detail exceeds prokaryotic detail |
| R-04 | Energy coupling | How does released potential become ATP? | Lead; substrate-level phosphorylation; proton gradient; ATP synthase | Molecular and membrane | Does not validate efficiency figures |
| R-05 | Conditional branching | What changes with the terminal acceptor and pathway conditions? | Lead; aerobic, fermentation, anaerobic sections | Molecular to organismal examples | Oxygen absence alone does not define one branch |
| R-06 | Yield boundary | Why does one total not describe every account? | Aerobic summary; Efficiency section | Molecular accounting to organismal examples | Preserves disagreement and citation limitations |

## Operator sequence

| Stage | Operation | Output | Status discipline | Prohibited inference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OBSERVE | Record the fixed source and its visible tensions. | Observation record | Source-supported observation / process observation | Admission does not create truth. |
| REPRESENT | Separate six questions the article interleaves. | Representation set | Editorial procedure grounded in source structure | A representation is not the biological object. |
| COMPARE | Inspect scale, purpose, compatibility, and gaps. | Comparison findings | Process observation with explicit source basis | Difference is not cause or hierarchy. |
| ORIENT | Offer non-ranked paths for different questions. | Orientation map | Editorial procedure | A path is not optimal, complete, or prescriptive. |
| EXPLAIN | Render direct navigation and expose construction. | Reader and Explain modes | No status promotion | Explanation is not proof or measured learning. |

## OLS use

The pilot uses the published Universal Base Language sequence. `PROFILE-NAVIGATION` and `PROFILE-EDUCATION` are active; `PROFILE-REPRESENTATION` is dependency-activated. `OP-SELECT` is absent: four alternatives remain available. No OLS concept, operator, profile, derivation, or conformance rule is changed.

## Status firewall

- Source statements remain **Source-supported observations**.
- Patterns visible through organization remain **Process observations**.
- Lenses, paths, role assignments, and stops remain **Editorial procedures**.
- Study 01 rules remain **Candidate growth rules** until the later process audit.
- Claims beyond the fixed revision remain **Unsupported interpretations**.
