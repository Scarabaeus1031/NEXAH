# Pilot Evaluation

This evaluates the Cellular Respiration translation on its own terms. It makes no cross-pilot comparison.

| Dimension | Demonstrated | Plausibly enabled | Not demonstrated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source preservation | One revision, identity, timestamp, SHA-1, scope, and limitations are recorded. | Later reinspection against the same revision. | Independent scientific correctness of the article. |
| Source/editorial separation | Source observations, process observations, and editorial paths are labeled separately. | Reviewers can challenge choices without rewriting source claims. | Perfect classification or reviewer agreement. |
| Traceability | Observations, representations, paths, and limits cite fixed source regions. | Later edge-level auditing and comparison preparation. | External audit of every entailment. |
| Representation distinctness | Six representations have different objects, questions, scales, and boundaries. | Readers can switch maps without conflating them. | Cognitive distinctness for actual readers. |
| Cross-scale discipline | Molecular, membrane, subcellular, cellular, organismal-example, and limited ecosystem statements are marked. | Scale changes can be inspected. | A complete or validated cross-scale model. |
| Path inspectability | Four paths state question, start, sequence, prerequisites, expected gain, limits, alternative, and prohibited implications. | A reader can choose by question. | Path preference, optimality, learning, or usability. |
| Unsupported gaps | Regulation, measurement, full ecological context, and quantitative reconciliation remain explicit gaps. | Future questions can be posed without silent supplementation. | Completeness of the gap inventory. |
| Reader benefit | Direct and explainable renditions exist. | Reduced confusion around pathway, location, branching, and yield. | Measured comprehension, question quality, or transfer. |
| Analyst independence | The pilot uses a newly fixed source and declares its own representations. | A later reviewer can inspect whether structure emerged independently. | Independent authorship or inter-analyst agreement. |
| Reproducibility | Revision and procedural records are explicit. | Another analyst can reconstruct the application. | Repeated execution or equivalent result. |
| Comparison readiness | Identity, source, representations, paths, statuses, limits, and provenance are explicit. | Later protocol can inventory comparable objects. | Any correspondence, similarity, reciprocity, or Atlas eligibility. |

## Negative findings

- The source is uneven in depth and citation quality.
- Six representations are editorially useful but not proven necessary.
- Four paths simplify networked and cyclic processes.
- Reader Mode may hide too much detail for advanced readers.
- Explain Mode may impose high maintenance cost.
- No reader, domain expert, independent analyst, or conformance reviewer has evaluated the result.

## Evaluation boundary

Internal coherence establishes only that the files agree with one another. It does not establish biological correctness, effective learning, process reproducibility, or superiority to ordinary editorial practice.
