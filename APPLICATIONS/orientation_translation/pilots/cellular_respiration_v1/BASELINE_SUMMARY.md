# Baseline Summary

## Control condition

The fixed article is a long, sectioned tertiary representation with substantial molecular detail. Its navigation is mainly topical: lead, aerobic sequence, ATP efficiency, fermentation, and anaerobic respiration.

## What the source already provides

- **Source-supported observation:** a broad definition centered on fuel oxidation, electron acceptors, ATP, and waste products.
- **Source-supported observation:** a detailed aerobic pathway sequence.
- **Source-supported observation:** spatial distinctions across cytosol and mitochondrial structures.
- **Source-supported observation:** conditional alternatives involving oxygen, other inorganic acceptors, and fermentation.
- **Source-supported observation:** several ATP-yield representations and named loss mechanisms.

## What the source does not provide as an explicit reader interface

- **Process observation:** no declared starting position for a general reader.
- **Process observation:** no separation between transfer, sequence, space, coupling, branching, and yield as orientation questions.
- **Process observation:** no non-ranked paths based on different reader questions.
- **Process observation:** no systematic statement of what each transition does not imply.
- **Process observation:** citation signals and quantitative variation are visible locally but not consolidated as an evidence boundary.

## Baseline boundary

The baseline is not judged deficient because it lacks the pilot's editorial organization. It serves a different purpose. The pilot may add navigability, but no reader benefit is demonstrated without an external reader study.
