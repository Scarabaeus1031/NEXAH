## Known source limitations

1. The source is tertiary and collaboratively edited, not scientific authority.
2. Several claims display `citation needed`, including parts of the aerobic summary, transport/efficiency discussion, fermentation, and anaerobic respiration.
3. ATP yields are represented at several levels: nominal textbook figures, 29–30, 28–30, and 30–32. The pilot preserves this variation instead of selecting a correct value.
4. The structure strongly privileges aerobic respiration and eukaryotic mitochondrial organization, while prokaryotic and anaerobic cases receive less detail.
5. The article mixes mechanism, accounting, location, organismal examples, and limited ecosystem context at unequal resolution.
6. Some wording and links may be ambiguous or internally awkward. The pilot records rather than repairs them.
7. The plant/ecosystem statement is narrow and cannot support a general ecological respiration model.
8. The article does not provide a complete measurement, regulation, evolutionary, clinical, or ecological treatment.
