## Overlap and asymmetry

- M-REP-01 and M-REP-03 overlap at each phase, but one orders time while the other locates change.
- M-REP-02 crosses all phase boundaries and cannot be reduced to a phase list.
- M-REP-04 is narrower than the phase account and cannot explain every transition.
- M-REP-05 is a scope-control representation, not another biological stage.
- M-REP-06 interrupts the smoothness implied by M-REP-01 and M-REP-07.
- M-REP-07 crosses scale more widely than the source develops.

## Incomparabilities and collapse risks

Phase landmarks cannot be matched one-to-one with chromosome states; spatial positions cannot be treated as temporal stages; checkpoint status cannot be treated as outcome; Cytokinesis cannot be treated as a phase merely because diagrams place it after Telophase; variation categories cannot be collapsed into errors; result language cannot erase exceptions.

The representations remain a plural set. No internal comparison selects a best or complete map.
