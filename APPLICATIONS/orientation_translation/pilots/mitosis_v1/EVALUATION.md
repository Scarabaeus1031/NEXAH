# Pilot 03 Evaluation

## Demonstrated

- one exact tertiary revision is fixed and traceable;
- seven source-emergent representations remain distinct;
- five reader paths separate time, chromosome state, space, conditions, outcomes, and process boundary;
- phase/state, sequence/causality, and Mitosis/Cytokinesis distinctions are explicit;
- source variation and citation-needed signals remain visible;
- no external biological source or prior pilot topology was imported;
- local candidate nodes and edges can be audited against source codes.

## Plausibly enabled

- a reader may locate a question without following one complete linear summary;
- later reviewers may reproduce or challenge representation and path decisions;
- Reflection may reveal where explanatory smoothness exceeds source structure;
- a directional neighborhood may preserve phase, spatial, conditional, and outcome predicates separately.

These are procedural possibilities, not measured reader outcomes.

## Not demonstrated

- scientific correctness or completeness of the Wikipedia account;
- reader learning, usefulness, preference, or reduced confusion;
- independent analyst agreement;
- completeness or necessity of the seven representations or five paths;
- universal phase, transition, checkpoint, or outcome structure;
- disease, diagnostic, developmental, or organismal conclusions;
- comparison readiness beyond record quality.

## Evaluation boundary

Internal traceability shows disciplined construction, not biological truth. A single collaboratively edited source cannot establish scientific correctness, reader value, reproducibility, or universal applicability.
