## Major orientation findings

- A phase list is necessary but insufficient: chromosome state and spatial reorganization cross phase boundaries.
- Phase, event, state, structure, condition, and outcome require separate records.
- The checkpoint statement is a bounded continuation condition, not a guarantee.
- Mitosis and Cytokinesis are related but independently variable processes in the source.
- The standard sequence is interrupted by open/closed forms, differing anaphase suborder, absent Cytokinesis, and named errors.
- Explanation creates continuity more readily than the source establishes causality.

## Major negative findings

The pilot does not establish a universal phase model, a complete checkpoint mechanism, one organism-independent geometry, a closed cycle, disease causality, diagnostic validity, reader benefit, or scientific correctness. The source does not justify treating every adjacent event as a direct causal edge.

## Source and process limitations

One tertiary revision is the entire evidence body. Linked phase articles and Cell Cycle are excluded. Broad function, evolution, disease, and diagnostic sections are less suitable for the local process neighborhood without additional sources. One analyst performed the construction; independent reproducibility is unknown.
