# Orientation Model

## Reader position

An introductory reader who recognizes chromosomes and cells but needs to distinguish sequence, state, space, condition, and outcome. No learning preference or biological competence beyond that boundary is inferred.

## Bounded orientation object

Mitosis is represented here as a locally ordered but multi-dimensional reorganization: named phase landmarks coordinate changing chromosome descriptions, spatial boundaries, attachment conditions, and possible outcomes. This is a representation of one tertiary source, not the biological process itself.

## Declared relations

Temporal precedence, phase membership, spatial location, attachment, transformation, movement, conditional permission, boundary reconstruction, process adjacency, and represented outcome remain distinct predicates.

## Unresolved regions

The exact checkpoint mechanism, organismal variation outside named examples, causal integration of simultaneous operations, relation between broad and narrow uses of “mitosis,” and scientific status of diagnostic claims remain unresolved by this source.

No representation is universal, necessary, optimal, or inherited from an earlier pilot.
