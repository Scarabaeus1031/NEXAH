## Why these three lenses?

The article itself moves repeatedly among biochemical mechanism, organismal/ecological significance, and agricultural constraints. The pilot makes those shifts explicit. It does not claim that the three lenses exhaust the article; evolutionary history and experimental history could support additional paths.

## Why the routes are not SELECT results

Navigation is active so paths, positions, and constraints can be represented. But the pilot neither knows the reader’s personal goal nor chooses on the reader’s behalf. It therefore does not invoke `OP-SELECT` and creates no `PRODUCT-SELECTION-RESULT`. Phrases such as “use this path if…” expose a conditional purpose; they are not recommendations or claims of optimality.

## Why the Human Context path is qualified

The source directly discusses major crops, C3/C4 differences, yield research, efficiency, and limiting environmental factors. It does not itself provide a developed model of food systems. The final edge is retained because it is a plausible public orientation question, but it is labeled an editorial bridge with missing domains rather than a source-established causal chain.

## Evidence and authority separation

- Wikipedia supplies the public article and its citations.
- OLS supplies semantic rules for the pilot’s construction.
- The pilot supplies editorial lenses, comparisons, paths, and renditions.
- The reader retains choice.
- No component gains another component’s authority.

## What this explanation cannot show yet

It cannot show that a student understands more, that the paths are preferable to Wikipedia’s own structure, that the biological claims are independently correct, or that the pilot conforms fully to OLS. Those require separate reader testing, domain review, and OLS-5 assessment.
