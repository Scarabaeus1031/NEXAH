# Orientation Model

## Reader position

The declared reader is a curious secondary-school student aged 15–17 who knows basic cell biology but not specialist biochemistry. No psychological profile, personal preference, learning diagnosis, or inferred intent is assigned. The question is: **How can I locate photosynthesis across mechanism, living systems, and human context without mistaking one view for the whole?**

## Bounded representation

**Focus:** how a first-time reader can locate photosynthesis across distinct questions and scales. **Context:** one fixed public tertiary article read for introductory orientation. **Construction perspective:** OLS-guided editorial mapping. **Reading perspective:** declared student reader. **Evidence status:** source-linked article claims, not independently validated findings. **Provenance:** every lens resolves to the fixed revision and named sections. **Outer boundary:** this model neither replaces the article nor supplies a complete biological, ecological, agricultural, or climate account.

### Separated lenses

| Lens | Focus, entities, and relations | Scale / time | Source basis | Boundary, uncertainty, unresolved region |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Biological process | organisms, pigments, organelles, reactions; capture and conversion | molecular to organismal; fast reactions to growth | Lead; Overview; membranes; reaction sections | “Photosynthesis” includes oxygenic and anoxygenic variants; no single plant model is universal |
| Chemical transformation | water/electron donors, CO₂, oxygen, ATP, NADPH, carbohydrates; oxidation, reduction, fixation | molecular; femtoseconds to seconds for staged events | Overview; light-dependent/independent reactions; kinetics | equations are summaries; stoichiometry and pathways vary |
| Energy conversion | photons, electron transport, proton gradient, ATP/NADPH; transfer and storage | photon to cellular metabolism | Lead; light-dependent reactions; efficiency | efficiency depends on declared conditions; energy diagrams are representations, not complete mechanisms |
| Ecological foundation | photoautotrophs, biomass, food webs, ecosystems; primary input and trophic support | organisms to biosphere | Lead; Overview; global activity and biomass statements; ecology neighbors | article names broad roles but does not construct a complete food-web or ecosystem model |
| Evolutionary development | early phototrophs, cyanobacteria, oxygenation, chloroplasts; succession and endosymbiosis | billions of years | Evolution and chloroplast-origin sections | chronology includes hypotheses and differently qualified evidence |
| Agricultural relevance | C3/C4/CAM plants, crops, leaf anatomy, yield; limitation and productivity | leaf to field/season | carbon-concentrating mechanisms; efficiency; C3:C4 research; factors | crop productivity is multi-factorial; no agricultural recommendation follows |
| Climate relationship | CO₂, temperature, water, light, biomass; constraint and carbon conversion | leaf conditions to planetary cycles | Lead; Factors; global carbon conversion | the article does not provide a climate model or establish a direct climate-to-food causal chain |
| Scientific history | experiments, named researchers, measurement methods, changing concepts; observation and refinement | centuries of investigation | Experimental history; efficiency/measurement | historical sequence does not itself validate current claims; source coverage may be selective |

These lenses overlap but are not merged into a universal model. The three reader paths in the Orientation Map are higher-level routes through this eight-lens representation, not replacements for it.

## Entities, relations, transitions, and scales

- **Principal entities:** light, pigments, reaction centers, water, carbon dioxide, oxygen, electrons, ATP, NADPH, carbohydrates, chloroplasts, thylakoids, cells, plants, algae, cyanobacteria, crops, ecosystems, atmosphere.
- **Relations:** absorbs, transfers, splits, produces, uses, fixes, stores, limits, supports, differs from, precedes, and is represented as connected to.
- **Processes/transitions:** light capture; electron transfer; proton-gradient and ATP formation; water photolysis; carbon fixation; photorespiration; carbon concentration; evolutionary oxygenation; experimental refinement.
- **Scales:** molecular, membrane/organelle, cellular, organismal, ecological, planetary, historical; fast photochemical events through geological time.
- **Unresolved regions:** variant pathways, disputed early chronology, source citation gaps, cross-scale causal detail, and the incomplete bridge from photosynthesis to food systems.

## Products and transitions

The pilot uses only registered products `PRODUCT-OBSERVATION`, `PRODUCT-REPRESENTATION`, `PRODUCT-COMPARISON-FINDING`, and `PRODUCT-ORIENTATION-FINDING`, connected by `TRANSITION-001` through `TRANSITION-004`. EXPLAIN communicates the orientation finding and does not create an additional product (`OLS4-REQ-0016`).

## Principal prohibited inferences

- article → truth or independent validation;
- model → biological reality or completeness;
- cross-lens agreement/difference → causal explanation;
- offered path → recommendation, unique correctness, or optimality;
- student-facing clarity → measured learning;
- explanation → proof, consensus, authority, or publication approval.
