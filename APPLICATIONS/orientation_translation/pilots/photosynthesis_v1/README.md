# Photosynthesis Orientation Translation Pilot v1

This pilot tests whether the published Orientation Language Specification (OLS) can turn one public knowledge article into a more navigable, evidence-bounded learning experience without changing the source's epistemic status.

It applies the complete universal sequence:

`OBSERVE → REPRESENT → COMPARE → ORIENT → EXPLAIN`

The source is the English Wikipedia article **Photosynthesis**, fixed to revision `1358838651`. The intended reader is a curious secondary-school student aged 15–17 with basic biology knowledge and no assumed specialist biochemistry.

## Status and boundary

- **Status:** informative OLS-guided application experiment
- **OLS release:** `OLS-RELEASE-1.0.0`
- **Conformance:** not claimed; no independent complete OLS-5 conformance assessment was performed
- **Source authority:** Wikipedia remains a public tertiary source, not an independently validated finding of this pilot
- **Pilot authority:** the pilot organizes and explains; it does not prove, recommend, authorize, or establish truth
- **Human-context limit:** agriculture and environmental constraints are source-grounded; the final move to food systems is an explicitly editorial extension with limited direct support in the source article

## Active semantic construction

The Universal Base Language is inherited. `PROFILE-NAVIGATION` and `PROFILE-EDUCATION` are explicitly active. `PROFILE-REPRESENTATION` is dependency-activated by Navigation. `OP-SELECT` is **not** invoked: all three paths remain available alternatives, and no path is declared best, optimal, or uniquely correct.

| Stage | Input | Operation and product | Principal limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OBSERVE | Fixed article revision and visible source status | `OP-OBSERVE` → `PRODUCT-OBSERVATION` | Admission does not create truth or evidence |
| REPRESENT | Recorded observations | `OP-REPRESENT` → `PRODUCT-REPRESENTATION` | The model is not biological reality or a complete account |
| COMPARE | Compatible represented lenses | `OP-COMPARE` → `PRODUCT-COMPARISON-FINDING` | Differences do not establish cause or preference |
| ORIENT | Representation, comparisons, reader position, source basis, limits | `OP-ORIENT` → `PRODUCT-ORIENTATION-FINDING` | Paths are not recommendations or uniquely correct routes |
| EXPLAIN | Orientation and preserved statuses | `OP-EXPLAIN` → explanation in Reader and Explain modes | Communication does not create proof or semantic authority |

All stage records preserve provenance, uncertainty, limitations, applicable declarations, OLS references, and prohibited inferences. See [TRACEABILITY.md](TRACEABILITY.md).
