# Comparison Matrix

## Invocation

- **Operator:** `OP-COMPARE`
- **Inputs:** three compatible representations of the same fixed article, each declared by lens and scale
- **Basis:** starting question, principal scale, source sections emphasized, relation made visible, likely prerequisite, and directness of source support
- **Declarations:** context, construction/reading perspective, representation type, scale, source identity, evidence class, uncertainty status
- **Product:** `PRODUCT-COMPARISON-FINDING`
- **OLS references:** `OLS1-CLS-0025`, `OLS2-CLS-0020`, `OLS2-REQ-0082–0083`, `TRANSITION-003`
- **Prohibited inference:** comparison does not establish causality, preference, selection, validation, prediction, or universal law

| Lens | Object / purpose | Scale and timescale | Representation and evidence form | Unresolved question / scope limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Biological process | Locate what organisms do | molecule to organism; reactions to growth | descriptive article synthesis and diagrams | How should diverse oxygenic/anoxygenic processes be grouped? |
| Chemical transformation | Track matter and redox relations | molecular; femtoseconds to seconds | equations, reaction descriptions, cited measurements | How much detail can be removed before the equation misleads? |
| Energy conversion | Track light to chemical carriers | photon, membrane, cell | mechanism descriptions, efficiency measures | How should conditional efficiency values be compared? |
| Ecological foundation | Locate photosynthesis within living systems | organism to biosphere; present cycles | global maps, biomass/energy estimates, linked ecology topics | The article does not build the intervening food-web model |
| Evolutionary development | Locate changes through Earth history | billions of years | fossils, geological evidence, hypotheses, phylogenetic narrative | Earliest origin and pathway remain differently supported |
| Agricultural relevance | Relate pathways and constraints to crops | leaf to field and season | crop examples, gas-exchange research, limiting factors | Photosynthesis alone does not determine yield or food security |
| Climate relationship | Relate CO₂, temperature, water, biomass, and atmosphere | leaf environment to planetary cycles | factor descriptions and global conversion claims | No integrated climate model or causal policy conclusion is present |
| Scientific history | Show how understanding was constructed | experiments across centuries | historical narrative, measurements, named studies | Historical coverage is selective and not a validation operation |

## Comparison findings

### Agreements and compatible relations

- All lenses retain the same article revision and agree that photosynthesis involves situated conversions of energy and, in many forms, carbon.
- Chemical Transformation and Energy Conversion are compatible at the molecular/membrane scale, while Biological Process supplies organismal context.
- Ecological Foundation and Evolutionary Development both connect photosynthesis to atmospheric oxygen, but on different temporal bases.
- Agricultural Relevance can relate to Biological Process through C3/C4 pathways and to Climate Relationship through limiting factors.

### Differences and incompatible scopes

- A biochemical reaction sequence cannot be treated as an ecosystem, crop, or climate model.
- Geological evidence and laboratory/field measurements are different evidence forms and are not interchangeable.
- Historical development of an idea and biological evolution of a process use different identities and timescales.

### Missing connections and apparent similarities

- The article does not fully connect cellular carbon fixation to food-web structure, or crop physiology to complete food systems.
- “Energy,” “efficiency,” and “productivity” occur across lenses but do not denote one compatible measure.
- Repeated language of conversion or cycle does not establish shared causality across molecular, ecological, and social scales.

The mechanism-related lenses have the tightest continuous support. Wider living-system and human-context routes require synthesis across separated sections. The lenses are complementary, not ranked; choosing one first would require a reader decision or an explicit `SELECT` invocation, neither of which this pilot performs.
