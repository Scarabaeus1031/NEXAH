## Article structure observed

The fixed revision contains an introduction and major sections on overview; photosynthetic membranes and organelles; light-dependent reactions; light-independent reactions and carbon-concentrating mechanisms; order and kinetics; efficiency; evolution; experimental history; and factors affecting photosynthesis.

## Pilot observations, kept separate

1. The source is rich but its article order mixes mechanism, history, scale, and application.
2. A student can receive different coherent routes through the same article without changing the underlying claims.
3. The route to food systems is only partially contained in the article and requires an explicit editorial bridge.

These are observations about the source as a learning artifact, not biological findings.
