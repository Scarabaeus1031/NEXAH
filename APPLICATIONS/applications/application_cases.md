# NEXAH Framework — Application Cases

The NEXAH framework is designed to analyze and navigate stability in complex dynamical systems.

Rather than covering all possible domains, current applications focus on systems where:

- dynamics are continuous  
- instability emerges through transitions  
- structure can be extracted from trajectories  

---

## ⚡ Primary Application — Power Systems

The most advanced and validated application of NEXAH is in electrical power grids.

### Capabilities

- early detection of voltage collapse (~43.9 seconds ahead)  
- identification of transition regions (interface zones)  
- construction of continuous stability fields  
- trajectory-aware adaptive control (IEEE9 prototype)  

### Insight

> Power systems are not binary (stable / unstable).  
>  
> They evolve inside a structured stability field.

---

## 🌐 Dynamical Systems (General)

NEXAH has been applied to continuous dynamical systems such as:

- Lorenz attractor  
- nonlinear flow systems  

### Observations

- structured flow fields emerge  
- trajectories follow preferred paths  
- collapse aligns with field geometry  

---

## 🔢 Discrete Systems (Exploratory)

Experiments on discrete systems (e.g. prime modular systems) show:

- emergent flow-like structures  
- cyclic behavior  
- directional transport  

### Interpretation

> Structured dynamics can emerge even without continuous physics.

---

## 🤖 Multi-Agent Systems (Experimental)

In simulation environments:

- agents operate without reward functions  
- navigation emerges from local field alignment  
- no predefined objective is required  

### Key Insight

```text
structure → field → movement → emergent behavior
```

This suggests a potential alternative to reinforcement learning.

---

## 🧠 Application Pattern

Across domains, NEXAH follows a consistent pipeline:

```text
system → regimes → geometry → field → navigation
```

---
