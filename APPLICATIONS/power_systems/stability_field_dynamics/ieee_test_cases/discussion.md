# Discussion — Interpretation of Stability Field Dynamics

## Structural Interpretation of Stability

The results suggest a fundamental reinterpretation of stability:

- stability is not a binary condition  
- it is not determined by thresholds  
- it is not tied to specific system configurations  

Instead:

→ stability is a geometric property of system evolution  

Specifically:

→ alignment with a low-dimensional structure in phase space  

---

## Collapse as a Structural Transition

Traditional models treat collapse as:

- a sudden failure  
- a threshold crossing  
- a loss of equilibrium  

However, the observed dynamics show that:

- systems approach collapse gradually  
- trajectories align with a collapse manifold  
- instability emerges as structured deviation  

Thus:

> collapse is not triggered  
> it is approached along a structure
