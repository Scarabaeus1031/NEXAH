# PENT-04 — Pascal pentagon 3D lift

Date: `2026-10-10`

Status: `BOUNDED_CANDIDATE_EVIDENCE / NOT_ADMITTED`

## Question

Can PENT-01/PENT-02 be lifted to a pentagonal bipyramid whose ten triangular
faces carry compatible depth-six Pascal lattices, while retaining an exact
projection back to the tested two-dimensional carrier?

## Declared carrier

The carrier has equatorial vertices `V_0 ... V_4`, Zenith `Z` and Nadir `N`.

```text
vertices: 7
edges:    15
faces:    10
Euler:    7 - 15 + 10 = 2
```

Top faces use `(Z, V_f, V_(f+1))`. Bottom faces use the reversed equatorial
orientation `(N, V_(f+1), V_f)`. Every face carries barycentric depth-six
nodes with Pascal weight `C(n,k)`.

The operators are:

```text
r: V_i -> V_(i+1), Z -> Z, N -> N
m: V_i -> V_(1-i), Z -> Z, N -> N
h: Z <-> N, V_i -> V_i
```

On face coordinates, `m` reverses `k`; `h` changes hemisphere and reverses
`k`. The relations are

```text
r^5 = m^2 = h^2 = e
m r m = r^-1
h r = r h
h m = m h
```

## Result

The exhaustive finite test passes.

- ten raw Pascal faces contain `280` face-local nodes;
- barycentric edge gluing leaves `182` unique nodes in one connected complex;
- the induced triangulation has `7` vertices, `15` edges and `10` faces;
- Pascal weights agree on every radial and equatorial seam;
- `r`, `m` and `h` generate `20` distinct weight-, recurrence- and
  metric-preserving actions, combinatorially `D5 x C2`;
- the orientation-preserving subgroup generated by `r` and `m h` has order
  `10` and is isomorphic to `D5`;
- each hemisphere projects onto exactly the same `106`-node PENT-02 carrier;
- the five equatorial edge midpoints are shared by both hemispheres and retain
  weight `C(6,3) = 20`.

## Boundary

This establishes one finite combinatorial and Euclidean 3D lift under the
declared coordinates and gluing. It does not establish prime labels,
resonance, physical dynamics, historical meaning or a universal Pascal solid.
Seven rows occur separately on each hemisphere. Removing apex and equator
leaves five intermediate rows, so the phrase `four inner layers` remains
undefined rather than being inferred from this carrier.
