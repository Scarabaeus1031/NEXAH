## 1. Bounded question

Does a source published before `2026-10-10` define either:

1. the same cyclic five-face quotient of depth-six binomial Pascal carriers;
   or
2. a more general construction that specializes to it without adding a new
   mathematical idea?

The audit concerns the finite combinatorial object only. Metric pentagons,
pentagonal bipyramids, `H2 direct-sum A1`, C5/C6 phase covers, physical
resonance and NEXAH-wide architecture are outside the candidate novelty unit.

## 2. Databases and discovery surfaces

The planned search surfaces are:

- MathSciNet;

- cited primary papers and preprints reached by backward/forward chaining.

General web search may locate a candidate, but no conclusion may rely only on
a search-result snippet, secondary summary, generated page or visual analogy.

## 4. Inclusion criteria

A record enters the comparison set if it is a primary mathematical source
(paper, preprint, proceedings contribution, book chapter or authoritative
technical manuscript) and defines at least one of:

- Pascal/binomial coefficients as weights on a finite graph or network;
- a graph or complex whose vertices are Pascal-triangle positions;
- a quotient or gluing of Pascal, triangular-grid or simplicial carriers;
- a cyclic/dihedral action on such a carrier;
- a general construction that plausibly specializes to SCC-003.

Bibliographic metadata alone may establish title, authorship, venue and date;
structural comparison requires an abstract, theorem, definition or full text.

## 5. Exclusion criteria

Exclude from the structural comparison set:

- classroom or decorative Pascal-pentagon images without a defined object;
- sources using “Pascal graph” only as a name for an unrelated graph family;
- modulo-coloring or divisibility pictures with no relevant quotient/gluing;
- generic quotient-graph theory with no Pascal/binomial carrier;
- standard pentagonal geometry, `D5`, pentagonal bipyramids or `H2` alone;
- NEXAH sources themselves.

Excluded sources may remain in the search log to show why they do not control
the candidate.

## 6. Equivalence and downgrade test

A source is an **exact or controlling predecessor** if its declared object, or
one immediate parameter specialization, supplies all of:

1. a Pascal carrier with vertices `(n,k)` and binomial weights;
2. multiple copies joined along complete left/right Pascal side chains;
3. a cyclic five-face closure, or a general cyclic `m`-face rule containing
   `m=5`;
4. the common-apex and six non-apex depth-six seam identifications;
5. the resulting weighted directed quotient or an isomorphic typed object.

The following are corroborating but not independently decisive:

- the sequence `28,49,70,91,112 -> 106`;
- the exact typed `D5` action;
- the 180 directed quotient edges.

If an exact or controlling predecessor is found, stop novelty wording and
downgrade SCC-003 to `INDEPENDENT_DERIVATION / REPRODUCIBILITY_OR_TEACHING_OBJECT`
until a narrower difference is independently reviewed.

## 7. Baseline ranking

Included sources are ranked by typed overlap:

```text
B0 exact/general predecessor
B1 same Pascal-position graph plus compatible symmetry or quotient operation
B2 same weighted Pascal network, different topology
B3 same gluing/quotient method, different carrier
B4 contextual standard structure only
```

The strongest baseline is the lowest-numbered class supported by inspected
source text, not by title similarity.

## 8. Permitted conclusions

The audit may conclude only one of:

- `EXACT_OR_GENERAL_PREDECESSOR_FOUND`;
- `CLOSE_BASELINE_FOUND_NO_EQUIVALENCE_ESTABLISHED`;
- `NO_EXACT_PREDECESSOR_LOCATED_IN_DECLARED_SEARCH`;
- `SEARCH_INCOMPLETE_ACCESS_OR_COVERAGE_LIMIT`.

None of these alone establishes worldwide novelty, priority, publishability or
mathematical significance. Independent mathematical and literature review
remain separate gates.
