## Canonical A0 sequence

The A0 Book already declares three bounded cultural transitions:

1. **Record / Play** at the entrance: a source is cut, recorded and replayed;
   the record does not become the whole source.
2. **Crystal View** in Chapter 06: one hosted artifact is inspected from
   several positions; changing appearance illustrates projection and does not
   replace the mathematical controls.
3. **Rooms of the Machine** in Chapter 08: the closing atlas relates the book
   to the Science Lab without merging its instruments into one mechanism.

The source-bound chain is therefore:

```text
source
  -> declared cut / record
  -> replay / selected view
  -> conditioned multi-view comparison
  -> return atlas
  -> human judgment
```

This is the architectural home of the supplied Record Player and Crystal
imagery. It also explains why `ILAU:RECORD_PLAY_432` is a view instrument and
not an ontological or physical claim.
