# NEXAH Research Paper Readiness Audit — Scope

Date: 2026-08-30  
Mode: `BOUNDED_DOCUMENTARY_PAPER_READINESS_AUDIT`

## Question

What paper, if any, is supported by the existing NEXAH research record under the 2026-08-30 framework freeze?

## Corpus inspected

- The complete `RESEARCH/` tree in NEXAH Core: 1,543 files, 227 directories, approximately 869 MB.
- All 138 Markdown records in that tree were screened by path, title, status language, claim language, and relation to the frozen framework. Major records were read directly.
- Source code, data, images, animations, arrays, HTML, and machine-readable results were inventoried under their owning packages. They were treated as artifacts, not as automatic scientific authority.
- Controlling records outside the tree were read only where needed: the framework freeze; NOS-01, WNI-01, TITAN-01, RID-01, SWM-01, NEXUS-01, OTC-01, and RPR-01 decisions; ORION V1 baseline; OLS 1.0 release records; IEEE Geometry validation; and EXP-00 closure records.

## Authority order

1. Human Authority and the 2026-08-30 framework freeze.
2. Closed final decisions and certified release records.
3. Reproducible bounded results with explicit limitations.
4. Working synthesis, draft, interpretation, and historical records.
5. Visual or symbolic expression.

Later authority does not erase earlier evidence; it controls current interpretation.

## Exclusions

No external literature search, new experiment, new model, new theory, software change, ORION change, paper rewrite, or publication action was performed. Existing `ABSTRACT.md` and `PAPER_DRAFT.md` were read only.

## Reading rule

The audit separates:

- `WHAT EXISTS`
- `WHAT IS CURRENT`
- `WHAT IS EVIDENCE`
- `WHAT CAN SUPPORT A PAPER`
- `WHAT REMAINS UNESTABLISHED`

Potential value is not counted as demonstrated value. A coherent record is not by itself a novel scientific contribution.

## Repository boundary

The pre-existing untracked custody archive under `RESEARCH/HISTORY/ROUTED_CUSTODY_2026-08-25/` was inventoried as historical custody material and was not changed or promoted into the paper path.
