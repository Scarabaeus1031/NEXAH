# Paper Classification

## Candidate classes

| class | fit | judgment |
|---|---|---|
| A — New scientific theory | Poor | No new law, theorem, or independently validated mechanism is established |
| B — New mathematical formalism | Poor | Typed notation exists, but novelty and formal results are not established |
| C — New algorithmic method | Poor | No new algorithm with comparative performance evidence is established |
| D — Methodological / conceptual framework | Strongest | Frozen type boundaries, evidence discipline, and stopping rules form a coherent method |
| E — Technical system / architecture | Partial | OLS, RID, and ORION artifacts exist, but the integrated machine remains incomplete |
| F — Reproducibility / provenance / scientific-workflow framework | Compatible secondary | Provenance, trace, representation, and authority separation are central |
| G — Applied case-study paper | Secondary only | IEEE, WNI, TITAN, and EXP-00 can illustrate; none should become the central claim |
| H — Position / perspective paper | Viable fallback | Appropriate if methodological distinctiveness cannot be shown against prior art |
| I — Not yet paper-ready | True for the current manuscripts | The contribution is plausible, but the existing draft and abstract are not current |

## Primary classification

`PRIMARY_PAPER_CLASS=D_METHODOLOGICAL_CONCEPTUAL_FRAMEWORK`

Compatible secondary framing: `F_REPRODUCIBILITY_PROVENANCE_SCIENTIFIC_WORKFLOW`.

## Why

The defensible center is not transition geometry, symbolic recurrence, autonomous navigation, or a universal architecture. It is a typed audit discipline for representation-dependent work: register source state and revision; distinguish source, structure, representation, observation, and view; declare transformations and comparison criteria; preserve loss, ambiguity, uncertainty, result, trace, provenance, interpretation, and Human Authority; and stop when evidence is insufficient.

This survives removal of all NEXAH-specific metaphors. It is supported by formal documents, machine-readable schema work, narrow implementations, and bounded positive, negative, and fail-closed cases. Its novelty relative to established provenance, workflow, type-system, transition-system, and human-in-the-loop methods is not established.

## Non-classifications

- Not a new scientific field.
- Not a validated universal transition theory.
- Not an autonomous decision-support system.
- Not a finished integrated software product.
- Not an applied engineering result with operational utility.
- Not a claim that the cases share one physical or causal mechanism.
