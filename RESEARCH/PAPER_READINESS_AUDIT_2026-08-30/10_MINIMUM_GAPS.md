# Minimum Gaps

The paper contribution is plausible, but two specific gaps prevent a submission-ready classification.

## Gap 1 — Prior-art and novelty review

Perform a bounded literature comparison against provenance and reproducibility frameworks, scientific workflow systems, labelled/partial transition systems, type/schema approaches, multi-representation model transformation, and human-in-the-loop governance. The output must state which elements are known, which combination may be distinctive, and which novelty claims must be withdrawn.

Why blocking: without it, the paper can describe NEXAH but cannot responsibly claim an academic contribution beyond existing methods.

## Gap 2 — Frozen-source manuscript consolidation and skeptical review

Create a new conventional manuscript from the frozen authorities, with a claim-to-evidence table, immutable artifact references, one formal case, one applied case, negative controls, and explicit nonclaims. Then obtain one technically qualified external review focused on whether the method adds anything beyond established audit/provenance practice and whether the evidence supports each claim.

Why blocking: the existing abstract and draft are stale and overclaimed; no outsider has tested whether the neutral method is understandable or useful.
