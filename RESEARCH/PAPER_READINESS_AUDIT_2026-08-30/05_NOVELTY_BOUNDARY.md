# Novelty Boundary

## What is genuinely distinctive in the current record

- The simultaneous non-collapse of source, state, representation, observation, view, transformation, result, trace, provenance, interpretation, knowledge, value, and Human Authority.
- An explicit vocabulary for preserved, transformed, different, partial, unresolved, and undefined outcomes.
- Fail-closed treatment of missing attestations, destructive representations, non-citable packages, and unrealized execution.
- A traceable path from conceptual types to a machine-readable schema and bounded case records.
- Use of positive, negative, and self-correcting cases as part of the method rather than hiding failed generalization.

These are demonstrated features of the NEXAH corpus. They are not yet demonstrated as novel relative to prior art.

## Known prior-art neighbours

The local framework itself identifies relevant neighbouring families:

- labelled and partial transition systems;
- hybrid and state-transition systems;
- attributed and provenance graphs;
- scientific workflow and reproducibility methods;
- schema and type-system approaches;
- belief-state and uncertainty representations;
- multi-representation and model-transformation formalisms;
- human-in-the-loop governance and decision records;
- category- or coalgebra-oriented formulations.

The audit does not claim equivalence with any of these and did not perform a literature search.

## What may be new only as a synthesis

The possible contribution is the integration of representation-change typing, explicit loss and unresolved states, provenance/trace separation, interpretation boundaries, and Human Authority into one audit record. That may be a useful synthesis even if each component has precedent.

## What is ordinary systems engineering

- versioned identities and manifests;
- schemas and fixtures;
- deterministic serialization;
- audit logs and traces;
- release baselines;
- modular interfaces;
- explicit error and unknown states.

These can strengthen the paper but cannot be presented as novel merely because NEXAH implements or names them.

## What remains unestablished

- superiority over existing provenance or workflow methods;
- a uniquely necessary set of types;
- formal soundness or completeness;
- measurable reduction of scientific error;
- external user usefulness;
- cross-domain scientific validity;
- operational or commercial value.
