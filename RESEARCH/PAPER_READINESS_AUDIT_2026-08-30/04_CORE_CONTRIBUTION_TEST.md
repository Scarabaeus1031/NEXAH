# Core Contribution Test

## Candidate central academic contribution

NEXAH contributes a typed, provenance-preserving method for auditing representation-dependent work. For each object and revision, it requires an explicit source state, representation or view, transformation, and comparison criterion; records what is preserved, changed, lost, ambiguous, unresolved, or undefined; and keeps outcome, trace, provenance, interpretation, and Human Authority distinct. The method is intended to prevent a common documentary failure: treating two representations as identical, treating recoverability as attestation, or treating interpretation as measured fact. Its current support consists of a non-normative formal proposal, a machine-readable schema with fixtures, a narrow certified implementation baseline, and bounded positive, negative, and fail-closed cases. It does not establish a new theorem, universal mechanism, predictive law, autonomous decision system, or cross-domain physical equivalence. Its academic distinctiveness remains conditional on a dedicated comparison with established provenance, workflow, transition-system, type-system, and human-in-the-loop methods.
