# WNI, NOS, and TITAN Roles

## WNI-01

`WNI_ROLE=FORMAL_CASE_STUDY`

WNI-01 recovers standard winding-number rules under declared conditions and shows that grid or representation preservation is conditional. It distinguishes exact topological preservation from coordinate error and preserves destructive failures. It is reproducible local formal evidence that the method can express invariance, loss, and fail-closed outcomes. It does not establish a new topological result, prime-grid structure, or general NEXAH law.

## NOS-01

`NOS_ROLE=SYNTHESIS_DESTRUCTION_CONTROL`

NOS-01 is the strongest evidence that a recognizable core remains after NEXAH-specific expression is removed. It recovers a coherent architecture with interface gaps and keeps ORION, RID, OLS, interpretation, and Human Authority separated. It supports internal coherence and metaphor independence, not external novelty or usefulness.

## TITAN-01

`TITAN_ROLE=EXTERNAL_CONTROL_EXAMPLE`

TITAN-01 tests whether an independently sourced scientific case can be typed without turning the source science, names, analogies, or expressions into NEXAH evidence. The result was that the existing architecture handled the case without new capability. It demonstrates boundary discipline. It does not validate the framework scientifically and should remain a short control example, preferably outside the main results section.

## Combined paper role

Together these cases provide three different controls:

| case | control |
|---|---|
| WNI-01 | Formal invariance and destructive representation control |
| NOS-01 | Metaphor-removal and architecture-coherence control |
| TITAN-01 | External-source typing and interpretation-boundary control |

They should not be presented as three domains confirming one mechanism.
