# Final Paper Readiness Decision

## Decision

`C_PAPER_CONTRIBUTION_PLAUSIBLE_BUT_REQUIRES_ONE_OR_TWO_SPECIFIC_GAPS`

The current frozen work already supports the core of a methodological/conceptual paper about typed, provenance-preserving evaluation of representation change. It can explain how to register source and revision, separate representation from observation and interpretation, declare transformations and comparison criteria, preserve loss and unresolved states, distinguish result from trace and provenance, and reserve consequential decisions for Human Authority.

The corpus does not support a new scientific theory, mathematical result, general algorithm, autonomous decision system, universal transition mechanism, or validated applied engineering claim. IEEE Geometry is a secondary case study. WNI-01 is the clearest formal case. NOS-01 is a synthesis/destruction control. TITAN-01 is an external typing control. RID, OLS, and ORION show that parts of the discipline have formal or implemented expressions, but not an integrated validated machine.

The contribution survives removal of symbolic and narrative material. The limiting issue is not lack of work; it is the absence of a bounded prior-art comparison and a current neutral manuscript that has survived skeptical external review.

## What paper the frozen record supports

**Primary:** a methodological/conceptual framework paper.

**Compatible secondary framing:** reproducibility, provenance, or scientific-workflow discipline.

**Not supported:** theory, universal science, new mathematics, validated predictive/control application, or complete research-software system.

## Minimum route to readiness

1. Complete the bounded prior-art and novelty review.
2. Consolidate a new frozen-source manuscript and submit that package to one skeptical technical reviewer before any submission decision.

No new experiment, software, ORION change, or research activation is required for this route.
