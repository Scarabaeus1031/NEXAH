# 🔥 III. Structural Claims

## Claim 1 — Gate Definition

A gate is defined as:

```text
Gate(s) =
low density
+ low coherence
+ rotation breakdown
```

Interpretation:

- density → structural support  
- coherence → alignment with flow  
- rotation → local stability

```text
R(s) = |curl(F)|
```

