# Phase Unification Summary

## Core Statement

Phase, drift, and topology are not system-specific properties.

They emerge from transition dynamics.

---

## Systems Considered

The following system classes were analyzed:

- Discrete systems (prime modular transitions)  
- Continuous chaotic systems (Lorenz, Rössler, Halvorsen)  
- Coupled oscillator systems (Kuramoto)

---

## Common Structure

Across all systems, the following structure is observed:

1. A phase coordinate θ(t) can be defined  
2. Phase increments Δθ(t) exhibit structure  
3. Mean drift μ_Δθ is non-zero (directional bias)  
4. Phase accumulates over time (winding)  
5. Transition dynamics induce coherent behavior  

---
