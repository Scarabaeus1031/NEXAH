# 🧠 PRIME MODULAR DYNAMICS — COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS

## Overview

We compare prime residue dynamics across:

- mod 7
- mod 11
- mod 13
- mod 17

Goal:

> Identify structural regimes in transition-driven dynamics

---

## 🔷 mod 7 — Dense Mixing Regime

- high transition density
- strong cycles
- coherent flow patterns
- rapid state mixing

Interpretation:

→ behaves like a **low-dimensional flow system**

---

## 🔷 mod 11 — Clustered Regime

- partial structure
- local attractor regions
- fragmented connectivity

Interpretation:

→ **local dynamics dominate global structure**

---

## 🔷 mod 13 — Diffuse Structured Drift

- weak transition bias
- higher entropy
- reduced coherence

Interpretation:

→ **structure exists but is not strongly organizing**

---

## 🔷 mod 17 — Sparse Channel Transport

- highly selective transitions
- dominant jump distances
- directed pathways

Interpretation:

→ system behaves like a **transport network**

---

## 🔬 Key Insight

Prime modular systems do not exhibit a single universal behavior.

Instead, they fall into **distinct dynamical regimes** depending on modulus:

- mixing
- clustering
- drift
- transport

---

## 🔷 Structural Interpretation

The system can be described as:

- discrete state space
- transition operator
- emergent geometry via embedding

This leads to:

→ regime-dependent dynamics

---

## 🔷 Conclusion

> Prime residue dynamics define a class of discrete systems exhibiting
> multiple structural regimes, including mixing, clustering, drift, and transport.
