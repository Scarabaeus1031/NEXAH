- Polygon drawings lose factorization and multiplicity unless annotated.
- Totient ratios lose polygonal layer and angular position.
- The phase sequence loses the visual and historical meanings assigned to nodes.

### A — Added

- Gate, portal, mirror, beam, field, open/closed and transmission meanings.
- Node ordering and visual hierarchy.
- Calendar, geographic and linguistic readings.
- Any claim that 2599 is intrinsically coupled because it lies two below 2601.

### U — Unresolved

- Whether the demonstrator improves human accuracy, speed, provenance recovery or uncertainty detection relative to a normal table.
- Whether a future application needs the angular model rather than standard modular arithmetic notation.
