## Use rule

When a historical topic becomes relevant:

1. identify the exact source artifact;
2. separate standard mathematics from NEXAH representation and symbolism;
3. formulate a current bounded question in neutral language;
4. point to or create an owning active research object only if needed;
5. retain the historical pointer and record non-implications;
6. do not copy the surrounding archive merely to preserve context.

Python notebooks or Colab material outside the frozen repository are not part
of this pointer map and require no archaeological recovery unless a specific
current question makes them necessary.
