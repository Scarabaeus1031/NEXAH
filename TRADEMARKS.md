## Permitted descriptive use

You may use the NEXAH name when reasonably necessary to:

- identify the origin of covered material;
- provide attribution or citation;
- describe compatibility or interoperability; or
- state accurately that a work is a fork, adaptation, or derivative.

Modified or independently maintained distributions should identify themselves
as modified, independent, or unofficial and should not use NEXAH logos or
visual identity in a way that suggests official status. A descriptive name such
as “an independent fork of NEXAH” is permitted when accurate.
