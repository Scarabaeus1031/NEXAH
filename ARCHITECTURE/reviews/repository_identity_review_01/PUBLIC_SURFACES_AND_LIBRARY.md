# Public Surfaces and Library

## Inspection boundary

This assessment inspected the live public pages at
[nexah.de](https://nexah.de/),
[the Visitor Guide](https://nexah.de/visitor-guide/), and
[NEXAHEDRON](https://nexahedron.com/) on 25 July 2026, together with the
maintained [Library](../../../LIBRARY/README.md), [Manifesto](../../../MANIFESTO.md),
and repository entry documents.

The websites are presentation evidence. They do not replace repository,
specification, Registry, or scientific authority.

## 1. nexah.de

### Communicated role

nexah.de presents itself as the public and intellectual home of the
Orientation Ecosystem. It routes visitors to the Library, Atlas, Laboratory,
repository, ORION, and NEXAHEDRON.

The homepage already makes several essential ideas visible above or near the
first screen:

- designing Human orientation in complex systems;
- connecting research, tools, maps, publications, and applications;
- keeping provenance, limits, and Human judgment visible;
- choosing where to begin rather than receiving one answer.

The public ecosystem map assigns distinct roles to nexah.de, the repository,
ORION, and NEXAHEDRON. The Visitor Guide further says that Version 1 makes
orientation inspectable and does not make decisions.

### Alignment

This is strongly aligned with the reviews. The site does not need
“Orientation Methodology” as a homepage identity.

### Remaining ambiguity

The homepage offers “Orientation Science” as the scientific entrance and asks
how orientation can become a discipline. Framed as an open question, this is
admissible. Without immediate status language, however, some visitors may read
it as an already established discipline rather than an active research
programme.

The phrase “one coherent journey” is useful for navigation but should not be
read as one integrated runtime, theory, or method.

## 2. NEXAHEDRON

### Communicated role

NEXAHEDRON presents itself as a Version 1 public development preview that helps
people orient across existing knowledge. Its central promises are modest and
well aligned:

- see a subject together with context, evidence, limits, and open paths;
- begin with a question, source, claim, observation, map, dataset, or
  uncertainty brought by the Human;
- inspect source, claim, boundary, evidence, uncertainty, and lineage;
- connect representations without replacing them;
- retain Human-owned intention and responsibility.

### Distinct role

The public map gives the clearest distinction:

```text
nexah.de
→ public and intellectual home; Library, Atlas, and ecosystem routes

NEXAHEDRON
→ Human-facing reference implementation; one bounded Orientation Session
```

This distinction strengthens repository identity because it separates public
explanation from situated use.

### Naming overlap

NEXAHEDRON calls itself “The Orientation Laboratory,” while the Manifesto and
nexah.de also use Laboratory for research and public explanation. The root
README more precisely calls NEXAHEDRON the reference Workspace.

No renaming is required, but the phrase **human-facing reference Workspace**
should remain visible wherever the public roles are compared. Otherwise
“Laboratory” can make the application surface and research function appear to
be the same owner.

## 3. Manifesto

The Manifesto is the strongest complete identity narrative. It already says:

- reality is richer than one representation;
- representations are purpose-, context-, method-, and evidence-bound;
- failures, contradictions, missing information, and uncertainty remain
  visible;
- repeated forms are research invitations, not universal laws;
- orientation is not a final answer;
- the system cannot understand or authorize on the Human's behalf;
- evidence, computation, outcome, cause, and action are distinct;
- NEXAH is not a theory of everything, belief system, oracle, or replacement
  for specialist science.

Its ending—moving responsibly toward the next question—is better aligned with
the repository evidence than any promise of universal answers.

The only notable tension is that its recurring nine-step laboratory movement
can look canonical when extracted from context. Architecture separately
clarifies that repository-level movements are vocabulary, not mandatory
workflow. Future quotation should preserve that qualification.

## 4. Library

### As architecture

The Library reads as documentation of an emerging research practice, not as a
loose publication archive. Its maintained README establishes:

- stable Work and Edition identity;
- source authority and canonical Registry boundaries;
- human-readable entry before software;
- curated relations and reading paths;
- provenance-bound derived views;
- proposal isolation and explicit canonical status;
- an explanation contract whose audience may change while evidence and claim
  boundaries may not.

These are direct expressions of the repository's methodological coherence.

### As public collection

The public collection is less self-explanatory. Books, atlases, whiteboards,
field notes, and posters contain different scientific and editorial statuses.
Titles such as “Orientation Science,” “Orientation Theory,” or “Unified
Transition Fields” can suggest a more settled body of science when separated
from Registry status, evidence records, and maintained limitations.

Visual recurrence can reinforce the project's questions and representation
practice. It can also make unrelated or exploratory structures appear to share
one mathematics.

### Verdict

The Library reinforces NEXAH's identity when entered through the Library
architecture, Atlas of Atlases, canonical sequences, or provenance-aware
catalog. It can dilute that identity when encountered as isolated publications
without a visible status and reading boundary.

The collection does not need a new unifying interpretation. It needs its
existing identity, provenance, status, and reader-path structure to remain
attached to public encounters.

## 5. Onboarding

The strongest onboarding sequence is already present:

```text
root README or Visitor Guide
→ choose a responsible surface
→ begin with a question or bounded source
→ inspect representation, evidence, and limits
→ retain Human interpretation and responsibility
```

The weakest onboarding sequence is:

```text
direct Research Abstract / Research Index / isolated visual
→ encounter strong mechanism or theory language
→ infer whole-project identity before seeing scope and authority boundaries
```

The required improvement is not another onboarding system. It is a visible
status bridge from those direct entrances back to the maintained repository
identity.
