# NEXAH Repository Identity Review 01

## Question

Would a new researcher naturally understand the repository in the same bounded,
methodologically coherent way described by Discovery Atlas Review 01,
Repository Synthesis Review 01, and Methodology Review 01?

## Status

Independent, informative communication and research-direction review.

This review does not create a new identity, mission, subsystem, architecture,
website concept, or scientific claim. It assesses the identity already
communicated by maintained entry points and selected public surfaces as
inspected on 25 July 2026.

## Verdict

**The principal front doors are substantially aligned; the deeper narrative is
not yet consistently aligned.**

The root README, Manifesto, Architecture introduction, Kernel onboarding,
nexah.de Visitor Guide, and NEXAHEDRON already communicate:

- bounded representations;
- evidence, provenance, uncertainty, and limitations;
- separate responsibility and authority;
- human interpretation before action;
- orientation rather than certainty.

A visitor who follows those paths can reach the same restrained identity as the
three reviews without encountering the phrase “Orientation Methodology.”
That omission is appropriate because the methodology remains an informative,
emerging synthesis.

The main divergence is inside older or narrower research entry material.
Prominent documents still foreground a unified transition-geometry framework,
fundamental mechanism, causal activation, and navigation/control. Direct entry
through those documents—or through isolated Library works and visuals—can
produce a stronger and less accurate identity than the maintained front door.

The repository therefore needs clarification and status propagation, not
rebranding.
