# Identity Alignment

## Executive assessment

The repository currently communicates two overlapping identities:

1. a maintained, repository-wide identity as an **evidence-bound orientation
   ecosystem** with separate responsibilities and human authority; and
2. a narrower research identity as a **transition-geometry framework** seeking
   mechanisms, topology, navigation, and control in dynamical systems.

The first is aligned with the three completed reviews. The second is a
legitimate research program, but it becomes misleading when read as the
identity of the whole repository.

## 1. Alignment with the three prior reviews

| Prior review conclusion | Current presentation | Alignment |
|---|---|---|
| A non-authoritative evidence navigation layer is justified | root README routes to Research, validation, system state, local claims, and authority owners; no implemented repository-wide Evidence Atlas is presented as authority | Strong in principle; claim-level discovery remains distributed |
| NEXAH is a coherent federated research program, not one unified theory | root README, Architecture, Manifesto, and Repository Map explicitly separate six responsibilities and reject universal theory or one runtime | Strong at primary front doors |
| Cross-domain coherence is methodological rather than mechanistic | evidence boundaries recur throughout the front doors, but the shared research practice is seldom named or summarized | Present but mostly implicit |

| Novelty, universality, and common mechanism are unestablished | root limits and Manifesto state this clearly; some research entry documents still use stronger “unified,” “fundamental,” and causal language | Uneven below the front door |

## 2. What the principal front doors communicate

### Root README

The [root README](../../../README.md) is the best current repository identity
statement. It says:

- NEXAH is an evidence-bound orientation ecosystem;
- relationships, limits, and possible transitions among bounded
  representations are made inspectable;
- six subsystems have separate authority;
- language, implementation, applications, Library, and editorial governance do
  not silently replace one another;
- orientation is not recommendation, authorization, execution, or outcome;
- universal transition laws, broad real-world validity, autonomous execution,
  and one unified runtime are not claimed.

This closely matches the synthesis and methodology reviews.

### Manifesto

The [Manifesto](../../../MANIFESTO.md) communicates the underlying purpose more
fully than any other document. It emphasizes representation boundaries,
context, evidence, uncertainty, human authority, non-final answers, and
responsible movement toward the next question. Its explicit “What NEXAH Is Not”
section strongly limits philosophical and scientific overreach.

Its rhetoric is broader and more philosophical than the root README, but its
boundaries prevent it from becoming a closed doctrine.

### Architecture

The [Architecture introduction](../../README.md) clearly defines an ecosystem
with no unified runtime and six separately owned responsibilities. Its primary
map is explicitly informative rather than a capability, conformance, or
integration claim. This is highly aligned.

### Kernel onboarding

The [Kernel Start](../../../nexah/START_HERE.md) is the clearest operational
expression of the emerging method:

```text
observations
→ typed representation
→ structure and relationships
→ orientation from a declared position
→ evidence-bound explanation
→ human review
```

It starts a new domain with a question, requires declared data and provenance,
preserves unknowns, and stops before action. It does not call this a universal
method or infer domain validity from software compatibility.

## 3. Where identity diverges

### Research portal: bounded but narrower

The maintained [Research README](../../../RESEARCH/README.md) clearly says that
the work is active research rather than a finalized theory and rejects
universal laws. Nevertheless, its central subject is complex dynamical systems,
structured fields, transition corridors, phase mismatch, navigation, and
control.

That is a legitimate program identity, not a complete repository identity.

### Research Abstract: stronger than the current synthesis

The [Research Abstract](../../../RESEARCH/ABSTRACT.md) presents NEXAH as a
geometry-oriented framework and advances strong operational interpretations of
phase mismatch, directional coherence, and control. Although it contains
limitations later, a reader following the Research README's first recommended
link encounters the scientific mechanism before the repository's broader
methodological boundary.

### Research Navigation Index: highest communication risk

The [Research Navigation Index](../../../RESEARCH/RESEARCH_INDEX.md) uses:

- “unified operational research architecture”;
- “Fundamental Transition Mechanism”;
- “causal activation layer”;
- “phase mismatch activates transitions.”

These phrases are stronger than the current evidence register and the bounded
language in the Research README. They can make a provisional research lineage
look like the settled center of NEXAH.

### Library direct entry

The Library architecture preserves identity, provenance, canonical status, and
human review. But a reader entering through a single book, poster, or Are.na
channel may see “Orientation Science,” “Orientation Theory,” unified fields, or
recurrent visual motifs without first seeing their evidence status.

The Library therefore communicates the emerging practice well as a governed
collection, but less reliably as a collection of isolated public artifacts.

## 4. Current repository narrative

No single one of the following phrases describes every surface:

| Surface | Dominant narrative |
|---|---|
| Root README | research and framework behind an evidence-bound orientation ecosystem |
| Manifesto | human-oriented, editorially governed orientation infrastructure and laboratory |
| Architecture | six coordinated responsibilities with separate authority |
| Research | transition geometry and dynamical-system research framework |
| Library | human-readable visual Library and orientation entrance |
| nexah.de | public and intellectual home for designing Human orientation |
| NEXAHEDRON | human-facing Orientation Laboratory and reference Workspace |

This plurality is not itself a defect; the repository is federated. The defect
appears when a local narrative is presented without enough indication that it
is one program or surface within the larger ecosystem.

## Alignment verdict

The repository's public and architectural center already communicates the
strongest supported identity. A researcher entering through the root and
following maintained boundaries should reach the same conclusions as the three
reviews.

The repository does not yet guarantee that result across all prominent paths.
Its communication is **front-door aligned, internally uneven**.
