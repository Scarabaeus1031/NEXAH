# Communication Risks and Minimal Improvements

## 1. Strongly supported ideas that remain insufficiently visible

### Methodological coherence

The shared discipline—declared representation, scoped operation, evidence and
status preservation, explicit limits, and separated authority—is visible in
many documents but rarely summarized at the point where local research
programs begin.

It should become more explicit as a relationship among existing
responsibilities, not as a named subsystem.

### Common practice is not common mechanism

This is the most important review conclusion missing from prominent Research
navigation. It should be visible wherever “unified,” “fundamental,”
cross-system, or recurring geometry is introduced.

### Evidence preservation across transitions

Root, OLS, validation, Kernel, and Library all support this principle. It is one
of the strongest actual connections across the repository, yet visitors may
notice recurring shapes and terms before noticing the preservation rule.

### Declared context and representation boundaries

These are explicit in semantic and implementation documents but less visible
in older conceptual research summaries and isolated public visuals.

### Uncertainty and non-promotion

The repository strongly distinguishes observation, evidence, validation,
publication, authority, and outcome. That strength deserves more prominence
than claims of framework breadth.

### Questions before adapters or action

Kernel onboarding and NEXAHEDRON already begin with a question. The Manifesto
ends with the next question. This is a coherent mission signal, though actual
improvement in reader question quality remains unvalidated.

## 2. Communication risks

### Unified theory by accumulation

The combination of a large Library, formal OLS, architecture diagrams,
cross-domain experiments, and repeated vocabulary can look like a unified
theory even when every individual boundary document rejects that conclusion.

### Mechanism inflation

“Fundamental Transition Mechanism,” “causal activation layer,” and unqualified
statements that phase mismatch activates transitions exceed the current
repository-wide evidence. These phrases are the clearest high-risk examples.

### Completed-framework impression

Terms such as framework, infrastructure, ecosystem, laboratory, language,
Kernel, core, and operating system appear together. Without the root maturity
table and system-state limits, readers may infer a completed integrated system.

### Philosophical-system impression

The Manifesto is carefully bounded, but aphorisms and broad statements can be
detached from their evidence clauses. Public excerpts should retain the
non-doctrinal and non-universal limits.

### Visual authority

Polished posters and recurrent geometry can look evidentially stronger than the
experiments they represent. Direct public Library paths do not always carry the
same status detail as repository records.

### “Orientation Science” as accomplished discipline

The phrase is defensible as the name of an open research programme or
publication. It is not yet supported as an established discipline with
independently validated methods and effects. The question form currently used
by nexah.de is important and should remain.

### Laboratory role collision

Research, nexah.de, the Manifesto, and NEXAHEDRON all use Laboratory language.
The public role map resolves this, but individual pages may not.

### “Better questions” as measured result

The repository supports better-question formation as a purpose and open
hypothesis. Orientation Translation explicitly says reader effect is untested.
Communication must not turn the aspiration into an empirical outcome.

## 3. Minimal, high-leverage improvements

These are recommendations only. This review does not implement them.

### A. Keep the root identity unchanged

Do not replace “evidence-bound orientation ecosystem” with “Orientation
Methodology.” The current root identity is more accurate and preserves the
federated architecture.

One optional sentence could make the review result visible:

> Across these responsibilities, the strongest shared structure is an
> evidence-preserving orientation practice—not a claim that every domain shares
> one mechanism.

### B. Add status headers to the two strongest divergent Research entrances

At the top of `RESEARCH/ABSTRACT.md` and `RESEARCH/RESEARCH_INDEX.md`, add a
short maintained-status notice:

- this is the transition-geometry research programme, not the whole repository
  identity;
- mechanism statements are working interpretations under bounded evidence;
- the root README and Research README control current scope.

This is more useful than rewriting the whole research archive.

### C. Qualify four phrases in Research navigation

Review:

- “unified operational research architecture”;
- “Fundamental Transition Mechanism”;
- “causal activation layer”;
- “phase mismatch activates transitions.”

The smallest correction is to label them conceptual, candidate, or
experiment-local and link the supporting evidence and limitations. No research
result needs to be deleted.

### D. Preserve the public role map everywhere roles meet

Where nexah.de and NEXAHEDRON are linked together, retain:

```text
nexah.de = public and intellectual home
NEXAHEDRON = Human-facing reference Workspace
```

This avoids creating new names while resolving Laboratory overlap.

### E. Attach a compact reading boundary to public Library entrances

At the Library or Atlas entrance, state:

> Works preserve different statuses and perspectives. A shared visual motif is
> an invitation to compare, not evidence of one theory or mechanism.

Link to existing Work identity, provenance, and Registry status rather than
creating another catalog.

### F. Make the evidence path adjacent to “Orientation Science”

Keep “How can orientation become a discipline?” as a question. Add a nearby
path to current methods, evidence, and limits so that the answer is visibly
under investigation.

### G. Do not add a methodology marketing layer

No new homepage section, acronym, icon, service, or architecture element is
needed. Methodology Review 01 belongs in researcher navigation and review
history, not as a public product claim.

## 4. Priority

| Priority | Improvement | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| High | status notices and phrase qualification in Research Abstract/Index | prevents the strongest unsupported first impression |
| High | Library reading boundary attached to direct visual entry | limits visual and title-driven overinterpretation |
| Medium | one shared-practice sentence at a repository synthesis point | makes existing coherence visible without rebranding |
| Medium | keep Workspace descriptor beside NEXAHEDRON | resolves role collision |
| Medium | evidence/limits path beside Orientation Science | preserves the open-question status |
| None | new architecture, identity, methodology layer, or repository move | unsupported and counterproductive |

## 5. Success criterion

After these small corrections, a reader entering through any prominent path
should be able to tell within the first page:

1. which responsibility or research program they entered;
2. whether its claims are semantic, computational, empirical, editorial, or
   aspirational;
3. what evidence and limits govern it;
4. that shared practice does not establish a shared mechanism;
5. where Human interpretation and authority remain.
