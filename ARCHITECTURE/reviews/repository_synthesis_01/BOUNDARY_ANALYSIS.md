# Boundary and Internal-Consistency Analysis

## 5. What the repository does not establish

### Universal transition physics

Research documents repeatedly discuss transition geometry, coherence, fields,
topology, and control. The maintained Research README nevertheless calls the
work an active program rather than a finalized theory and rejects universality,
general proof, and production control guarantees
([scope](../../../RESEARCH/README.md#L186-L204)). The repository does not
establish universal physics or a physical theory of orientation.

### A formal theory of everything

The Foundation's “Structural Theorems” are explicitly semi-formal, empirically
supported propositions rather than mathematical proofs
([source](../../../RESEARCH/FOUNDATION/structural_theorems.md#L25-L43)). OLS is a
language specification, not a proof that all domains share one ontology or
mechanism.

### General AI or autonomous decision authority

The architecture preserves human interpretation and separates orientation from
recommendation, authorization, execution, and observed outcome
([root boundary](../../../README.md#L131-L154)). The repository does not
establish general intelligence, autonomous judgment, or decision legitimacy.

### General causal or control claims

Implemented navigation and intervention values are explicitly heuristic
([package README](../../../nexah/README.md#L12-L28)). The Validation portal
reports associations and intervention responses but no generalized causal or
control law
([source](../../../RESEARCH/VALIDATION/README.md#L159-L174)).

### Universal grammar

The editorial grammar review supports a recurring operation family but rejects
a cross-domain grammar and candidate formal system
([assessment](../../../APPLICATIONS/orientation_translation/studies/editorial_grammar_of_orientation_01/CANDIDATE_GRAMMAR_ASSESSMENT.md#L5-L11)).
It explicitly does not extend or interpret OLS.

### General implementation conformance

OLS 1.0 is canonical, but neither the release nor Architecture automatically
certifies every implementation. The current package is a scoped computational
baseline with local state identities and exploratory heuristics.

### Operational prediction or real-world domain validity

IEEE Geometry is a benchmark protocol. Its manifest prohibits treating
non-convergence as a certified physical boundary, geometry as a causal
precursor, or benchmark behavior as real-world generalization
([manifest](../../../APPLICATIONS/power_systems/ieee_geometry_v1/case_manifest.json#L109-L134)).
Network fixtures similarly do not issue control commands.

### Mathematical meaning from visual similarity

Architecture and experimental sources state that figures are informative
representations and do not replace evidence. Repeated curves, wheels, fields,
or color systems therefore do not establish shared mathematics.

### Special numerical privilege

The frozen tests do not support unique Mod-17 predictive status, the fixed
7-to-17 bridge, dynamic stabilization by Mod 17, or the predefined 31/32/33
anomaly. Exact CRT and residue facts do not supply these missing conclusions.

## 6. Internal consistency

### Formal authority consistency

No direct formal contradiction was found among the maintained Architecture,
OLS release, implementation scope, modern validation bundles, applications,
and Evidence Register. Their explicit authority boundaries prevent a semantic
release from becoming empirical truth and prevent application output from
becoming architecture.

### Material tensions

Absence of formal contradiction does not mean the repository communicates
consistently.

#### “Theorem” terminology versus proof status

`structural_theorems.md` uses theorem language while disclaiming mathematical
proof. The disclaimer resolves the formal status, but the title invites a
stronger inference than the evidence supports.

#### Unified research narrative versus distributed implementation

The Research Index describes a “unified operational research architecture,”
while System State says there is no unified runtime and logic remains
distributed across scripts
([System State](../../SYSTEM_STATE.md#L334-L404)). These statements can coexist
only if “unified” is read as conceptual organization, not implemented or
validated unification. That distinction is not always prominent.

#### “Visual evidence” versus representation boundary

The Research structure labels figures as visual evidence, while Architecture,
wheel results, and IEEE sources state that visuals do not establish capability
or mathematics. The responsible reading is “visualization of evidence,” not
independent evidence. The terminology remains communication debt.

#### Historical findings versus frozen tests

Prime Modular Resonance uses language such as recurrence manifold, transport,
and emergent geometry, while its maintained status says scaling claims remain
unresolved and newer bundles reject specific Mod-17 and boundary hypotheses.
The newer sources do not erase the history, but later readers can encounter the
stronger historical wording first.

#### Validation vocabulary

The Validation portal defines “Empirical” as repository-supported and
explicitly says it does not mean independent replication or universal proof
([definition](../../../RESEARCH/VALIDATION/README.md#L40-L52)). This is internally
declared, but it differs from what some scientific readers may assume from
“validated” or “empirical.”

#### Evidence Register selection

The register's statuses are generally faithful to their cited sources. Its
selection is not neutral: recent prime work, boundaries, and documentation
reviews are more granular than older dynamical-system programs. A synthesis
that counts entries without examining this sampling would overstate prime work
and understate the broader experimental archive.

### Consistency verdict

The repository is **architecturally consistent but rhetorically uneven**.
Maintained boundary documents usually correct stronger implications elsewhere.
The main risk is not incompatible contracts; it is that readers encounter
exploratory or historical language without the later limiting source.

## Boundary rule for synthesis

Any repository-wide synthesis must preserve:

```text
common method ≠ common mechanism
shared vocabulary ≠ shared mathematics
canonical semantics ≠ implementation conformance
benchmark replay ≠ domain validity
representation ≠ observed reality
association ≠ causality
orientation ≠ decision authority
```
