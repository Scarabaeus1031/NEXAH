# Architecture Reviews

This directory preserves completed reviews of repository narrative, placement,
and architectural alignment. These artifacts document how a conclusion was
reached; they do not change architecture merely by being stored here.

## Reviews

- [Documentation Alignment 01](documentation_alignment_01/README.md) — assessed
  the smallest documentation changes needed after Orientation Translation
  emerged as both an application and a methodological research program.
- [Discovery Atlas Review 01](discovery_atlas_01/README.md) — assessed whether
  NEXAH needs a claim-level consolidation layer and proposed a minimal,
  non-authoritative Evidence Atlas.
- [Repository Synthesis Review 01](repository_synthesis_01/README.md) — assessed
  whether the bounded evidence forms a coherent research program, and separated
  methodological coherence from an unestablished unified scientific theory.
- [Methodology Review 01](methodology_review_01/README.md) — assessed whether
  the repository's strongest cross-domain contribution can be described as an
  emerging Orientation Methodology without turning it into a new authority,
  architecture layer, common mechanism, or completed scientific claim.
- [Repository Identity Review 01](repository_identity_review_01/README.md) —
  assessed whether the maintained front doors and public surfaces already
  communicate that bounded identity, and located the remaining divergence in
  stronger research summaries and context-detached visual publications.
- [Repository Identity Alignment Pass 01](repository_identity_alignment_pass_01/README.md)
  — applied only the recommended status, scope, evidence, and visual-reading
  boundaries to the maintained direct entrances identified by the Identity
  Review.
- [NEXAH Freeze Planning Review 01](nexah_freeze_planning_review_01/README.md)
  — determined the smallest remaining work before a canonical Framework
  repository freeze and separated true freeze blockers from evidence
  navigation, post-freeze improvements, and public-launch work.

Current architecture remains defined by the maintained
[Architecture README](../README.md), [System State](../SYSTEM_STATE.md), and the
responsible subsystem documents.
