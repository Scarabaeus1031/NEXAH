# NEXAH Research & Ecosystem Architecture — Adoption Decision Record

**Decision:** Adopted

**Decision authority:** Repository owner

**Decision date:** 2026-07-30

**Constitutional position:** Subordinate to the NEXAH Ecosystem Constitution
v1.0

## 1. Owner Decision

The repository owner explicitly accepted all four adoption questions:

1. the described repository responsibilities;
2. the existence of one binding cross-repository Research & Ecosystem
   Architecture;
3. the Public Interface Mapping as part of that architecture;
4. the Umbrella Orientation Research Question while Transition Geometry
   remains an independent, bounded research program within NEXAH.

## 2. Adopted Authority

The document is adopted as the canonical cross-repository research
architecture for:

- the Umbrella Orientation Research Question;
- the subordinate Open Systems and Rails Research Question;
- cross-repository roles;
- maturity/evidence and authority classification;
- Public Interface Mapping;
- separation of Current Verified State from Open Research Horizon.

This authority is descriptive and coordinative. It answers:

> Which part of the adopted research architecture does this repository,
> interface, or artifact own, implement, certify, investigate, or present?

## 3. Explicit Non-authority

Adoption does not make the architecture:

- a new Constitution;
- an OLS specification;
- an ORION certification;
- a Runtime architecture;
- a product promise;
- scientific validation;
- authority over artifacts owned by another repository or responsibility;
- a transfer of semantic, editorial, evidential, certifying, technical, or
  Human authority.

Every authority class remains with its owning responsibility. The adopted
architecture is canonical only within the scope stated in Section 2.

## 4. Preserved Boundaries

- The Constitution remains the highest governance authority.
- The Repository Map remains the directory-level navigation authority and is
  supplemented rather than replaced.
- OLS 1.0 remains the released semantic authority.
- Kernel implementation maturity does not define OLS semantics.
- ORION Version 1 remains frozen at `at_slice_iv_certified`.
- Library Registry retains editorial identity authority.
- NEXAHEDRON remains a bounded Human-facing experiment and interaction layer.
- NEXAH Experience remains the public presentation and routing layer.
- The Human retains interpretation, Reflection, Rest, decision, and effect
  authority.

## 6. Adoption Diff Boundary

The adoption pass is intentionally limited to:

- this Decision Record;
- removal of Draft status from the adopted architecture;
- preservation of its exact Authority and Scope;
- replacement of the Draft Promotion Gate with adopted change control;
- update of the three existing index entries;
- generation of the fingerprint manifest.

No scientific content, repository responsibility, certified scope,
implementation, Runtime, public website, or external repository is changed by
the adoption pass.

## 7. Change Control

Later changes to the adopted Research Questions, repository roles, authority
classes, Public Interface Mapping, or Current State / Research Horizon boundary
require:

1. a new explicit architecture diff;
2. source and repository-ref verification;
3. an Authority-Conflict Review;
4. an explicit decision by the competent NEXAH authority.

Editorial corrections that do not change meaning remain subordinate to the
same boundaries and must preserve reviewability.
