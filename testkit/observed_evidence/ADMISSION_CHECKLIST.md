# Observed-Evidence Admission Checklist

Use this checklist before any external record is called an observation or
observed outcome in NEXAH.

## Source and rights

- [ ] source owner and stable identifier recorded
- [ ] acquisition method and timezone-aware timestamp recorded
- [ ] license and redistribution status reviewed
- [ ] immutable raw checksum recorded
- [ ] dataset version and correction policy recorded
- [ ] privacy, security, retention, and redaction reviewed

## Semantics and quality

- [ ] event time and recording time distinguished
- [ ] topology or schema identity recorded for every interval
- [ ] entities aligned by a documented method
- [ ] variables, units, references, and scales explicit
- [ ] missingness and quality flags preserved
- [ ] transformations and exclusions reproducible
- [ ] uncertainty stated claim by claim; unknown remains unknown

## Evaluation integrity

- [ ] research question and claims predeclared
- [ ] development and evaluation split frozen
- [ ] representation and parameters frozen before evaluation inspection
- [ ] outcome definition and window frozen
- [ ] metrics, stopping rules, and insufficiency policy frozen
- [ ] later revisions receive a new manifest version

## Orientation and outcome

- [ ] Orientation Report references only cutoff-available evidence
- [ ] scenario and computation records remain separately typed
- [ ] outcome follows orientation in time
- [ ] outcome source relation and its basis recorded
- [ ] method-selection relation and its basis recorded
- [ ] outcome scope matches orientation scope
- [ ] all six firewall checks pass
- [ ] episode written only through the guarded gateway

If any required item is unknown, the case remains incomplete or indeterminate.
The checklist is not a substitute for domain, scientific, ethical, or legal
review.
