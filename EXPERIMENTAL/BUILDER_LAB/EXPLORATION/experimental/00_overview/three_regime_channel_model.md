# 🧭 NEXAH — Three-Regime Channel Model
### (Structured Transition Dynamics in Reconstructed State Space)

---

# 🧭 Purpose

This document defines the **Three-Regime Channel Model** observed in NEXAH validation experiments.

It formalizes how dynamical systems transition between:

```text
stable → transition → unstable
```

and how trajectories move through **structured regions and pathways** in state space.

---

# 🧠 Core Idea

Instability does not occur as a single event.

Instead, systems evolve through:

```text
structured regions connected by preferred transition paths
```

---

# 🔷 1. The Three Regimes

---

## 1. Stable Regime

```text
high coherence
low curvature
bounded motion
```

### Characteristics:

- trajectories remain localized  
- motion is structured and repeatable  
- system exhibits stable dynamics  

---

## 2. Transition Regime

```text
increasing curvature
geometric deformation
directional drift
```

### Characteristics:

- trajectories leave stable region  
- motion becomes directional  
- structure begins to deform  

---

## 3. Unstable Regime (Collapse Region)

```text
loss of coherence
rapid divergence
unbounded motion
```

### Characteristics:

- trajectories move away from stable structure  
- collapse becomes observable  
- system behavior is no longer contained  

---

# 🔗 2. Transition Structure

The regimes are not isolated.

They are connected by:

```text
transition regions and pathways
```

---

## Transition Region

Defined as:

```text
set of states where:
- curvature increases
- drift emerges
- coherence decreases
```

---

## Key Observation

```text
multiple trajectories enter the same transition region before collapse
```

This indicates:

```text
a reproducible structural precursor to instability
```

---

# 🧭 3. Channel Hypothesis

---

## Definition (Working)

Channels are:

```text
preferred pathways through which trajectories enter and traverse the transition region
```

---

## Important Clarification

Channels are currently:

```text
qualitative observations
```

They are NOT yet:

- formally defined manifolds  
- discrete objects  
- counted structures  

---

## Observed Behavior

- trajectories follow similar but not identical paths  
- variations exist, but structure persists  
- entry into transition region is consistent
