JANUS transition orientation atlas
==================================

Interpretation:
Orientation is defined in a 2D diagnostic space:
x = JANUS vs log-curvature correlation
y = low-JANUS / high-curvature overlap fraction

Negative x indicates anti-coupling.
Positive x indicates co-coupling.
High y indicates localized transition-candidate overlap.
