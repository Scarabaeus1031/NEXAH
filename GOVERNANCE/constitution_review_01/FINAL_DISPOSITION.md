# Final Disposition

## Authority review

No canonical repository-wide NEXAH Constitution was located. OLS-0's stated
constitutional role is explicitly limited to the Orientation Language
Specification suite and therefore does not block a separate repository-level
review. It does prevent this review from claiming authority over OLS.

## Finding

Current evidence supports seven durable cross-system constraints:

- bound before extending;
- preserve provenance and history;
- keep statuses distinct;
- preserve plurality without collapse;
- keep negative knowledge visible;
- do not equalize material asymmetry; and
- let nothing become canonical silently.

Representation humility, provisionality, inspectability, reader honesty, and
open questions remain important, but they are incorporated as rationale,
supporting principles, or local research ethics rather than additional articles.

The produced Constitution is a review candidate only. It creates no authority,
changes no existing governance, and cannot canonize itself.

## Overall disposition

**CURRENT PRINCIPLES SUPPORT A PROVISIONAL CONSTITUTIONAL CANDIDATE**
