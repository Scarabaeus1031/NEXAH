# Limitations

- **Shared authorship:** repository artifacts include human and AI-assisted work.
  The review cannot reconstruct contribution shares or infer governance rights
  from authorship.
- **Repository-local evidence:** conclusions describe the inspected repository,
  not all private drafts, conversations, public use, or external practice.
- **No independent governance review:** this review was produced inside the same
  project it evaluates. It is not independent ratification.
- **Recurrence is not legitimacy:** repeated practice supports a candidate but
  cannot authorize constitutional status.
- **Inspectability is not correctness:** a traceable, reviewable artifact may
  still be wrong, incomplete, biased, or scientifically invalid.
- **Reader effect is unvalidated:** current artifacts do not demonstrate that
  NEXAH improves orientation, learning, or decision quality for readers.
- **Incomplete public/Are.na mirroring:** public Are.na ordering, descriptions,
  and reader behavior are not fully represented by repository evidence.
- **Historical gaps:** Git history begins after some conceptual work and cannot
  establish every earlier formulation or status decision.
- **Ambiguous authority labels:** terms such as “canonical,” “constitutional,”
  “approved,” and “authority” sometimes apply only within a subsystem. OLS-0's
  constitutional label is the clearest example.
- **Constitutionalizing current habits:** recent source-bounded review practice
  may reflect current tools and editorial attention rather than durable need.
- **Over-procedural risk:** a Constitution can become a workflow manual. The
  candidate therefore omits directories, templates, commands, review packages,
  and detailed lifecycles.
- **Evidence concentration:** asymmetry, reader honesty, cartographic stopping,
  and some negative-knowledge practices are especially well documented in
  Orientation Translation and less directly tested elsewhere.
- **No public authorization:** creating this candidate does not constitute human
  adoption, public ratification, or a change to repository authority.

These limitations constrain the disposition to a provisional candidate.
