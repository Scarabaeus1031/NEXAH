# Contradiction and Polarity Review

| Tension | Finding | Constitutional treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Openness ↔ boundedness | **Productive polarity.** Exploration remains open; preserved claims require boundaries. | Bound consequential extension, not curiosity. |
| Plurality ↔ canonical specification | **Scope difference.** Representations may coexist while a defined subsystem selects one authoritative semantic release. | Plurality does not prohibit explicit, scoped canonicalization. |
| Exploration ↔ stopping | **Productive polarity.** Open questions invite work; stopping prevents unsupported expansion. | Preserve both the question and the stop condition. |
| Visual coherence ↔ evidence asymmetry | **Potential contradiction.** A coherent visual can falsely equalize support or imply missing edges. | Material evidence differences control presentation; silence may be information. |
| Living systems ↔ frozen artifacts | **Productive polarity.** Practices evolve through immutable or versioned evidence states. | Freeze governed artifacts; evolve through explicit successor states. |
| Reader accessibility ↔ evidential precision | **Productive polarity, unresolved in effect.** Renditions can simplify language, but reader benefit remains unvalidated. | Audience changes may not alter evidence, uncertainty, or boundaries. Reader effects cannot be claimed without testing. |
| Local autonomy ↔ architectural governance | **Scope difference.** Subsystems own responsibilities; architecture governs cross-boundary effects. | Local work remains autonomous until it claims authority outside its scope. |
| Provisional experimentation ↔ repository stability | **Productive polarity.** Experiments inform the project while proposal/experimental labels protect stable surfaces. | Experimental recurrence does not silently promote status. |
| Continuity ↔ correction | **Productive polarity.** Earlier records remain available while errors and supersession are explicit. | Correction creates a traceable new state; history is not erased. |
| Invitation ↔ authority | **Productive polarity.** NEXAH invites participation but use, popularity, or authorship does not create approval authority. | Contribution remains open; canonical change remains explicitly authorized. |

## Historical changes rather than present contradictions

Early framework texts sometimes speak as if relations, transition geometry, or
systemic evolution were general foundations. Current evidence-bound documents
constrain these as hypotheses, representations, or local findings. The change
is a maturation of claim boundaries, not evidence that the historical files
should be rewritten.

## Unresolved tensions

- How shared authorship should translate into durable governance authority.
- How public Are.na editorial state and repository governance should be jointly
  reviewed without giving either silent priority.
- Whether reader accessibility and demonstrated orientation effect can be
  evaluated without turning editorial experience into a single metric.
- Whether a future constitutional authority should be a person, role, review
  process, or other explicitly constituted body.

The candidate preserves these tensions; it does not manufacture their solution.
