# Constitutional Layer Test

The test asks whether a principle is a durable constraint on NEXAH's integrity,
not whether its wording is attractive or frequent.

## Eight-test conclusion

Seven articles survive all tests when phrased at the level of durable
constraint. The test rejects a catalogue of current techniques. It also rejects
the idea that repetition alone creates constitutional authority.

The candidate is therefore deliberately smaller than the full principle
register: boundedness; traceability; status separation; plurality without
collapse; negative knowledge; material asymmetry; explicit inspectable change.
