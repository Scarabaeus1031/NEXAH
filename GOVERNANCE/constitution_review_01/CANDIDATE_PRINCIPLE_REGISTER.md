# Candidate Principle Register

## Admitted constitutional candidates

| Candidate | Durable formulation | What it constrains | What it does not authorize | Evidence base |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Boundedness | Declare the relevant source, scope, question, representation, and authority boundary before preserving consequential extension | scope drift, support laundering, displaced authority | mandatory templates for informal exploration | source-bounded studies; OLS ownership; Library proposal isolation; subsystem authority map |
| Traceability | Consequential claims, relations, transformations, and authority changes remain recoverable to sources and decisions | orphan claims, invisible transformations, erased rationale | correctness merely because a record is traceable | OLS Trace IDs/change records; Registry provenance; snapshots/diffs; validation records |
| Status separation | Different epistemic and operational statuses do not silently substitute for one another | research becoming canon, implementation becoming semantics, visualization becoming evidence | isolation between related forms or prohibition on translation | root authority map; OLS normative/informative split; research/evidence/editorial classifications |
| Plurality without collapse | Multiple representations may coexist without implied identity, equivalence, rank, or merger | premature unification and comparison by appearance | blocking explicit choice, editorial sequence, or authorized canon | Manifesto; comparison protocol; Library journeys; representation reviews |
| Negative knowledge | Material rejections, limits, failures, unknowns, and stopping decisions remain visible when they affect interpretation or change | false closure, silent deletion, unsupported transitivity | permanent logging of every abandoned thought | rejection registers; unsupported-edge audits; OLS deferred/rejected history; validation failures |
| Material asymmetry | Unequal evidence, direction, authority, scale, or completeness remains visibly unequal | forced symmetry, false reciprocity, visual equalization | a ban on comparison across difference | comparison framework; orientation cartography; evidence maps; authority boundaries |
| Explicit inspectable change | Canonical identity, semantics, architecture, and constitutional status change only through declared scope, preserved history, and explicit human authorization | silent canonicalization, implicit approval, destructive replacement | a new approval body or authority over existing local governance | OLS-0/6; Library Architecture; Editorial OS; Writer safeguards |

## Constitutional vocabulary caution

Words such as “canonical,” “operator,” “profile,” and “conformance” retain their
owning subsystem meanings. The candidate Constitution does not redefine them.
Its terms describe repository integrity only.
