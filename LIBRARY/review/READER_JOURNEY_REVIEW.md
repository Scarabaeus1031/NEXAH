## Answer modes

### Reader Mode

A short curated answer. No visible scores, Operator IDs, or provenance detail.
The Kernel must still retain an internal explanation for every result.

### Explain Mode

On request, reveal reasons, Operators, Series, relations, canonical/proposal
state, and provenance.

## Success criteria

- A reader understands why each next step is offered.
- Navigation Entities and Works are not confused.
- The same Library can orient newcomer, builder, and researcher differently.
- Canonical facts and Proposal hypotheses remain visibly separate.
- The Kernel can explain every result through curated metadata or explicit provenance.

## Decision boundary

IDs remain blocked until these journeys have human observations and the Kernel can
answer the five reader questions without hiding uncertainty or provenance.
