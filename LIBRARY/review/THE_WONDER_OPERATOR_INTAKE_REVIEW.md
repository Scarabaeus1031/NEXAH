## Why `practice`

The Book teaches through reader participation rather than primarily through
reference or exposition. It asks the reader to pause, notice, compare, reflect,
choose attention, protect curiosity, cultivate presence, and carry the next
orientation into life.

`practice` is therefore the strongest current Library Function. `learning`
remains a plausible secondary reading, but the current schema admits only one
primary Function.

## Plate and Transition Evidence

Chapter 16 provides direct internal evidence for two reviewed NEXAH patterns:

```text
THE PLATE
A Slice of the Living Field

TRANSITION
The Movement Between Plates
```

The Plate is described through Boundary, Orientation, Scale, Focus, Tools,
Context, and Time. Plates may overlap, connect, integrate, expand, and continue.
Transition is presented as the passage from one bounded orientation toward a
new one.

This makes the Work a strong designed exemplar of the Reflective
Representation Cycle. It does not independently prove that the pattern was
already implicit across older Works.
