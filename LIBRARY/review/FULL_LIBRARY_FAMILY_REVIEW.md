## Family rules

- `work`: a bounded authored sequence that can stand as one publication.
- `environment`: an open research, experiment, or laboratory space.
- `navigation`: an entry, index, collection, series hub, or reference layer.
- `asset`: reusable supporting material rather than a publication.
- `unresolved`: used only when a Family cannot yet be proposed responsibly.

Whiteboard, Atlas, Field Guide, Visual Essay, and Laboratory Report are not
Families. They belong to Type, Form, or Library Function in Pass B2.
