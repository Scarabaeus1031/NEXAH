# Orientation Foundations — Batch A

This directory is the first bounded visual research prototype under the
Publication Catalog.

It contains:

- visual triage for all 96 pages in four Orientation foundation Works;
- 69 pages marked eligible for possible deeper extraction;
- 12 page-level Research Records for clearly eligible pages;
- exact Are.na Channel and Block references;
- source fingerprints for stale-evidence detection;
- explicit claim boundaries and queued human review.

It is non-canonical. It does not change the Registry, Proposal Layer, controlled
Operators, Kernel, Living Concepts, or Are.na.

## Sources

1. ORIENTATION SCIENCE — 35 pages
2. ORIENTATION DESIGN — VOLUME II — 20 pages
3. ORIENTATION THEORY — VOLUME III — 21 pages
4. THE ARCHITECTURE OF ORIENTATION — VOLUME IV — 20 pages

The corpus strongly implies a shared publication program, but this record does
not define a canonical four-volume series. ORIENTATION SCIENCE is a Research
Prospectus without an explicit volume number; Volumes II–IV are explicitly
numbered.

## Files

- `batch.yaml` — batch scope, counts, mutation boundary, and series assessment
- `triage/` — one record per Work, one lightweight result per page
- `records/` — selected page-level Research Records awaiting human review
- `BATCH_A_REPORT.md` — findings, exclusions, and next review gate

Source images are not stored here. Are.na remains the publication authority.
