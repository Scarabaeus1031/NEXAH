## Publication-program assessment

The four Works form a **strongly implied** Orientation foundation program, not
an accepted canonical series.

- ORIENTATION SCIENCE defines a proposed field and research agenda.
- ORIENTATION DESIGN — VOLUME II describes an intentional design discipline,
  method families, and research questions.
- ORIENTATION THEORY — VOLUME III develops map ecosystems, orientation versus
  navigation, research pillars, and open questions.
- THE ARCHITECTURE OF ORIENTATION — VOLUME IV positions NEXAH between
  disciplines and describes cartographer, laboratory, bridge, museum, studio,
  and atlas roles.

The numbering of Volumes II–IV is explicit. Calling ORIENTATION SCIENCE Volume
I would be an editorial inference and is therefore not recorded as fact.
