## 🔬 Mathematical Definition (v1)

Let:

- \( \dot{x} \) = system velocity  
- \( F(x) \) = local field direction  

Then coherence is defined as:

\[
C(x) = \frac{\dot{x} \cdot F(x)}{|\dot{x}| \, |F(x)|}
