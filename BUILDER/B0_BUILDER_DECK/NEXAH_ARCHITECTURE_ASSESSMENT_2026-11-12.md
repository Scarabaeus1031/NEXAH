**Status:** `BUILDER_ARCHITECTURE_DECISION_SUPPORT / NO_ACTIVATION`

**Owning view:** B0 Builder Desk / Architect View
**Authority boundary:** Mission Control controls priority and activation;

## Executive assessment

NEXAH is architecturally substantial. The substance does not lie in one new
algorithm or one finished product. It lies in a multi-layer system that can
keep source, transformation, evidence, representation, residual and human
decision visibly separate while still connecting them through a replayable
Return.

The strongest current architecture is therefore not a universal machine. It
is a **source-bound assurance instrument pattern**:

```text
DECLARED SOURCE + QUESTION
  → TYPED CONTRACT / CUT
  → REPLACEABLE QUALIFIED CORE
  → GOVERNANCE ENVELOPE
  → RECEIPT + REPLAY + RESIDUAL
  → HUMAN VIEW
  → RETURN / ABSTAIN / STOP
```

This is a working architectural reading over existing components, not a new
Capability ID, Navigator identity, product name or scientific claim.

## Why the word “substantial” is justified

| Dimension | Present evidence | Architectural reading |
|---|---|---|
| Breadth | `45` registered Builder tools; `36` runnable | a real tool estate rather than a single demonstrator |
| Contracts | OLS 1.0, typed records, claim and return boundaries | sources and transformations can be governed explicitly |
| Execution | Kernel, ORION, comparators, adapters and bounded applications | multiple qualified cores exist, but no General Runtime is integrated |
| Receipts | CRIC, NRRC, UDF, Methods 1–5, replay and fail-closed checks | trace and Return are first-class architectural objects |
| Human inspection | A0, Orientation Bench, PI Lens, THE MACHINA and NEXAHEDRON | complex multi-view systems can be made operable and inspectable |
| Provenance and control | Source Graph, Science Navigator and Mission Control | identity, evidence, currentness and priority remain separate |

The same evidence also fixes the ceiling: `0` components are human-validated
and `0` are production-ready. Substantial means **built, bounded and
inspectable**; it does not mean externally useful, commercially ready or
scientifically universal.

### Reconciliation verdict

The Architect View is accurate when it shows six capability families, but the
families are not equally mature. `DEFINE`, `RECORD`, `INSPECT` and `NAVIGATE`
form the present architectural core. `PROCESS` supplies replaceable bounded
engines. `EXPERIENCE` is the Human carrier and the principal validation gap.

The portfolio should therefore not be flattened into a single maturity label
and should not be represented as 45 independent products.

## Pragmatic value assessment

The achieved value has three different classes that must not be collapsed:

| Value class | Present assessment | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **architecture and asset value** | **high / evidenced internally** | reusable contracts, processors, receipts, views, provenance and a coherent source-to-Return grammar exist as a real technical estate |
| **use value** | **plausible / unvalidated externally** | the estate may help a reviewer inspect competing views, hidden loss, unsupported closure and necessary abstention more reliably |
| **market value** | **unknown** | no external user, buyer, repeated task, willingness to pay or production operation has yet established demand |

The product-shaped asset is therefore not one Klein, Fourier or PI display.
It is the reusable assembly grammar plus the Bench surface that can make a
bounded multi-view review inspectable. A useful category description is
**multi-view evidence and decision workbench** or **source-bound assurance
workbench**. These phrases describe the present architecture; they do not
create a new product identity, Capability ID or market claim.

| AI lifecycle governance | [IBM watsonx.governance](https://www.ibm.com/products/watsonx-governance/model-governance) | inventories models, records factsheets, evaluates and monitors lifecycle risk | remain processor-agnostic and compare multiple source-bound views rather than govern only one model lifecycle |
| interactive analytics, digital twins and review dashboards | established visualization and engineering products | strong display, state exploration and operational integration | bind every view to declared source, operator, residual, replay and Human Return without claiming that presentation is evidence |

No individual primitive is unique to NEXAH. The potentially distinctive
property is their integration into an explicit assurance loop:

```text
SOURCE + QUESTION
  -> VIEW / CUT / OPERATOR
  -> RETAINED / LOST / ADDED / UNRESOLVED
  -> RECEIPT + REPLAY + CLAIM CEILING
  -> HUMAN RETURN / ABSTAIN / STOP
```

A practical assembled comparison would otherwise require an enterprise
ontology or workflow layer, a lineage system, a provenance package, an
interactive review interface and a separate decision log. NEXAH has built a
small instrument-first version of that pattern. It has not yet shown that the
integration is more useful than a strong assembly of existing tools.

## Likely first users and bounded uses

The strongest prospective design partners are small expert teams for whom a
lost source, hidden transformation or unsupported conclusion is costly:

1. AI assurance, model-risk and responsible-AI reviewers;
2. systems-engineering, MBSE and digital-thread review teams;
3. visual-analytics, HCI and explainable-AI research groups;
4. due-diligence, investigation and complex evidence-review teams;
5. research-infrastructure, reproducibility and data-stewardship groups.

Museums, media labs and experimental education are a credible second route for
the experiential layer, but they test presentation and engagement rather than
the general assurance hypothesis. The recommended first product test remains
one expert review with a fixed source package and a strong ordinary baseline.

## Architectural recommendation

### 1. Freeze the ecosystem topology

Keep the current project bodies and authority boundaries stable:

1. **NEXAH + NEXAHEDRON** — Human/public orientation and experience;
2. **A0 / THE MACHINA** — method, research and instrument flagship;
3. ORION, Navigator, Mission Control, THE EYE and B0 — infrastructure and
   control surfaces, not additional flagships.

Do not add another top-level Desk, format, runtime, machine family or visual
surface unless a declared Human task cannot be served by the existing estate.

### 2. Adopt one reference assembly pattern, not one monolith

For every future case, compose only the required parts:

```text
SOURCE
  → OLS / CASE CONTRACT
  → QUALIFIED EXISTING PROCESSOR
  → CRIC / NRRC / UDF-STYLE RECEIPT
  → SOURCE GRAPH POINTERS
  → SMALLEST EXISTING HUMAN VIEW
  → RETURN / ABSTAIN / STOP
```

The processor remains replaceable. NEXAH earns its place only when the
Governance Envelope makes a relevant loss, uncertainty, residual or decision
boundary clearer than the baseline.

### 3. Run exactly one vertical Human validation slice

The recommended first slice is a **bounded multi-view evidence review**:

- one real reviewer and one real decision question;
- a small fixed source set with known authority;
- a strong baseline workflow using ordinary documents, tables and links;
- a NEXAH workflow assembled only from existing components;
- predeclared measures such as source-retrieval accuracy, missed conflicts,
  unsupported conclusions, review time and correct ABSTAIN/STOP decisions;
- blind or counterbalanced order where practical;
- an explicit stop when NEXAH adds no useful decision quality.

This slice fits the strongest existing architecture and directly attacks the
largest uncertainty. It does not require a new platform. PI Lens may remain an
internal reference instrument, but it is not itself evidence of general Human
utility.

### 4. Treat currentness repair as Gate 0

Before presenting any integrated architecture as current, reconcile the
remaining Mission-Control hash mismatches. Currentness repair is maintenance,
not a new Builder programme and not evidence of usefulness.

### 5. Productize only after a positive bounded result

If the Human slice is positive, preserve it as a reusable Assurance Object:
source snapshot, contract, operators, outputs, residual, claim ceiling,
Human Return, receipt and replay. Only then decide whether a repeatable product
wedge exists. If the result is null or negative, retain the result and stop
without constructing another abstraction layer.

## Recommended gates

| Gate | Finish condition | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| `G0 · CURRENTNESS` | controlling hashes and projections agree, or every unresolved mismatch is explicitly owned | repair / hold |
| `G1 · CASE CONTRACT` | real question, Case Owner, baseline reviewer, measures, information boundary and STOP are frozen | authorize / reject |
| `G2 · MINIMUM ASSEMBLY` | existing components serve both baseline and NEXAH paths without a new platform | run / stop |
| `G3 · HUMAN RETURN` | result, residual, errors, time and abstention are recorded and reviewable | positive / null / negative |
| `G4 · PORTFOLIO DECISION` | evidence supports reuse, redesign or closure | productize / retain / stop |

Only `G0` is presently justified as routine maintenance. `G1` requires a
separate Human Owner decision. Later gates do not exist by implication.

## What the Architect does not recommend

- integrating all `45` tools into one runtime;
- adding more displays to prove integration;
- treating PI Lens, Klein topology or matrix holonomy as a Penrose result;
- declaring NEXAH superior because it carries more metadata;
- starting a second Human case in parallel;
- creating a new top-level identity, registry or Capability ID;
- public product, science or novelty claims before the applicable gates.

## Risk register

| Risk | Present signal | Architectural response |
|---|---|---|
| surface proliferation | many Desks, instruments and historical variants | two flagships; everything else infrastructure, inventory or archive |
| false integration | visual proximity can look like implemented coupling | distinguish verified, bounded, missing and view-only edges |
| custom-format burden | several local carriers and profiles | standard core and formats first; custom envelope only with round-trip value |
| founder overload | large corpus and continual building | one active priority, one case, one next decision |
| currentness drift | eleven unresolved Mission-Control mismatches at assessment time | G0 before integrated-current claims |
| utility overclaim | rich internal QA but no external Human validation | baseline-controlled G1–G3 slice |

## Architect decision statement

**Recommendation:** preserve the achieved architecture, stop horizontal
expansion, repair currentness, and prepare one baseline-controlled multi-view
evidence-review case. Do not build a General Machine. Use existing components
to test whether the NEXAH Governance Envelope improves a real Human decision.

**Current disposition:** `ARCHITECTURE HOLD / G0 MAINTENANCE PERMITTED /
HUMAN VALIDATION CASE NOT YET AUTHORIZED`.
