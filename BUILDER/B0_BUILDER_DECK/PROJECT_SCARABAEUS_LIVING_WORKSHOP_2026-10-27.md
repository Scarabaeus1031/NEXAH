## Decision

SCARABÆUS 1033 is retained as the existing independent workshop and artistic
source inside the wider NEXAH field. This brief does not create a new brand,
Navigator identity, website, Studio authority or publication state. It binds
the current website source, the existing public-house role, Rödelheim
Observatory and the supplied visual direction into one Builder project.

The public site should not become another Mission Control, Science Desk or A0
Builder Desk. Its proposed role is the curated public projection of the work:

## Core proposition

Working title: **SCARABÆUS 1033 — The Living Workshop**

Working promise:

> Images become worlds. A picture may become a book, a moving image, a sound,
> a room, an exhibition or a memory.

The site should feel like entering a workshop rather than reading a corporate
overview. It can be atmospheric and mythic, but it remains human, navigable
and explicit about the difference between art direction, existing work,
historical material and factual status.

## Architecture decision

The Builder cut follows one controlling rule:

> One object, one stable identity and one source owner — with several bound
> views.

Consequences:

- A0 remains the Owner workspace and orientation engine; it links to or stages
  reviews of public surfaces but does not become their editable source.
- B0 owns build routing, the active-cut record, dispositions and returns; it
  does not copy source files or maintain a parallel changelog.
- the Scarabäus repository owns its public shell, editorial text, public work
  pages, code and Git history;
- NEXAHEDRON remains the Human View and is frozen for this cut. Later links
  from a playable object to a Scarabäus work page are bounded trapdoors, not a
  merger of the two sites;
- Science retains evidence and claim authority wherever a public work refers
  to research;
- no A0 page, internal bench, inventory or source-bound record is copied into
  the public workshop merely to make it visible.

Operational reading:

```text
SCIENCE       checks, bounds and describes
SCARABÆUS    translates, builds, plays and transforms
HUMAN VIEW    lets a person experience, choose and return
```

## Visual direction

The supplied boards are retained as `VISION_REFERENCE / NOT_FACTUAL_RECORD`.
They establish a coherent direction:

- deep black, nocturnal blue, copper and restrained gold;
- cinematic landscape and warm workshop light;
- serif narrative type plus narrow technical labels;
- fine diagrams, paths and constellations used as orientation marks;
- slow transitions, stillness and deliberate pauses;
- materiality: paper, book, screen, room, sound and gathering;
- a human scale beneath the larger mythology.

Avoid:

- dense infographic walls as the primary public interface;
- invented counts, progress percentages or institutional claims;
- presenting every concept-board sentence as adopted language;
- exposing Builder backlogs, Mission Control or scientific registers;
- making the Scarabæus mythology a comprehension test.

## Content ratio

Working editorial ratio:

- `60%` atmosphere and narrative;
- `25%` concrete selected works;

This is a direction heuristic, not a measured release requirement.
