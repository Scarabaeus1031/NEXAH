# Parked Builder Concept — AMT / Game of Worlds World Build

Status: `PARKED_BUILDER_CONCEPT / NOT_ACTIVE / NOT_A_FINDING`

Date: `2026-10-25`

Owner direction: Die bestehende Idee soll im Builder Desk erhalten werden,
damit `Game of Worlds` später nicht nur weitere Screens erhält, sondern als
zusammenhängende, begehbare Welt erweitert werden kann.

## Identity decision

This brief reuses the existing `Game of Worlds`, A0 Builder Desk and
Orientation Controller Bench surfaces. It creates no new Navigator identity,
Study, scientific finding, physical mechanism, publication state or active
Builder priority.

`AMT` is a working route/glyph name supplied by the Human Owner. Its expansion
and exact semantics are not frozen here.

## Core picture

```text
MOVING DOMAIN BELT                          FIXED REFERENCE

[ WATER ] — joint/channel — [ LAND ] — joint/channel — [ SKY ]  →  [ SPACE / +1 / TOWER ]
    frame                         frame                         frame             holds

The belt carries frames. The Tower does not travel with the belt.
```

- **Belt / frames move:** Water, Land and Sky are three distinct world frames
  carried through the route. Their contents and clocks are not silently merged.
- **Tower holds:** Space / `+1` / Tower is the stationary reference at the
  right-hand side, not a fourth domain of the same type.
- **Joints and channels connect:** transitions have explicit seams. A channel
  may carry a typed state or view record; it does not erase the boundary
  between worlds.
- **A real world, not a longer dashboard:** the eventual Builder question is
  how these frames become spatial rooms, passages and controllable encounters
  while the return path remains visible.

## 90-degree view

The current left-to-right reading `t → t+1` may later receive a second,
rotated view:

```text
UPPER SIDE     strip / pump / outgoing pole
                    │
                 joint
                    │
LOWER SIDE     counter-side / receiving or return pole
```

This is a view transformation and Builder hypothesis. “Anode / cathode” is an
orientation analogy only; it does not assert an electrical, chemical or
physical mechanism.

## Required conceptual correction before implementation

The present play text says that PLAY moves all three worlds **and the +1
point** while the chosen lens remains still. The parked concept instead asks
for the domain belt to move while **Space / +1 / Tower remains fixed**. A
future build must resolve this explicitly rather than changing the current
game silently.

## Owner correction — Bucket / twin-handle Lantern

The earlier `Pythagorean cup / siphon` reading was too literal and is rejected
for this concept. The intended object is a **bucket**, not a cup. Its arch is a
carrying handle; more precisely, the assembly has **two moving handles / rails**
like the paired sides of a jug or bucket.

The two anchor nodes and the stair / level body stay fixed. The two rails travel
around that fixed structure. Pink and Green are the paired carriers and appear
at their ends as two noses or projecting cuts.

```text
                         FRESNEL LANTERN / SHIP
                                  ▲
                PINK HANDLE / RAIL ╲ ╱ GREEN HANDLE / RAIL
                    moves around   ╳   moves around
                                  ╱ ╲
                       ● ┃ FIXED STAIRS ┃ ●
                       A ┃ BOOK / LEVELS┃ B
                         ┃    STATIC    ┃
                    PINK NOSE         GREEN NOSE
                                  ↓
                    moving WATER · LAND · SKY belt
```

### Controlling movement

- **Static:** two nodes, the stairs / level spine and their ordered relation.
- **Moving:** two railings / handles circulate around the stairs. The object
  does not move because the steps themselves travel.
- **Carried:** the Fresnel Lantern / little ship is hung into the paired handle
  structure above the moving domain belt.
- **Twin noses:** Pink and Green are the two terminal projections of the paired
  route. Their exact cut, contact and hand-off relation remains to be drawn.
- **Several books:** the bucket may carry several books or volumes. These are
  distinct level/view carriers, not water levels and not automatically one
  continuous book.

### Bucket grammar

The bucket is a container and carrier of layers:

`LOCAL / OBSERVATION → RELATION → PATTERN → MODEL → PRINCIPLE / ORIENTATION`

The useful metaphor is that it holds complexity and keeps levels connected
while the two handles provide movement between them. The bucket is not the
knowledge itself, and the map is not the territory.

The paired escalator image gives the motion rule: understanding and action can
travel in opposed or complementary directions while the steps and endpoints
remain legible. The railings move around the stairs; the fixed nodes preserve
the reference.

### Preserved analogies, no longer the mechanism

- **Blockflute ports:** may still inspire a control surface with seven front
  holes plus a rear thumb role and paired terminal controls. This remains an
  interface analogy, not a hydraulic circuit.
- **Pressure / Pascal / hovercraft:** may remain later engineering references
  if an actual lift or pressure subsystem is separately drawn. They do not
  explain the current bucket-and-handles geometry.
- **Pythagorean cup / siphon:** retained only as a superseded interpretation
  record. It is not the present object's mechanism.
- **Fresnel:** remains the optical role of the Lantern unless another coupling
  is explicitly built and tested.

## Game of Life connection — W plates / “4D chess” view

The current Game of Worlds LIFE view already contains a compatible layered
architecture. Its four visible plates classify one generation into distinct
transition roles:

1. `EVENTS` — the addressed source events;
2. `i · BIRTH` — cells changing `0 → 1`;
3. `j · SURVIVE` — cells retained as `1 → 1`;
4. `k · DEATH` — cells changing `1 → 0`.

These are **W plates** in the parked Builder vocabulary: separated working
planes of one recorded update, not four independent worlds. “4D chess” is an
interaction metaphor for reading position, plate/role and generation together;
it is not evidence of four spatial dimensions.

```text
                 FIXED CELL ADDRESSES / TWO REFERENCE NODES
                                      │
         EVENTS ──joint── BIRTH ──joint── SURVIVE ──joint── DEATH
            W0             Wi               Wj               Wk
                                      │
                         generation g → generation g+1
                                      │
                              lower RETURN record
```

### Joint types

- **Address joint:** the same cell address is readable across the relevant
  plates of one generation.
- **Rule joint:** B3/S23 assigns the transition class from the neighbourhood;
  the visual plates do not create a second rule.
- **Temporal joint:** the classified update closes `g` and produces the next
  state at `g+1`.
- **Return joint:** the lower return preserves the generation, rule, selected
  view and transition record so the next cycle can be inspected.

The return does **not** mean that the Death plate directly feeds births. Native
Game of Life still computes the next generation from the complete previous
grid and its neighbourhood rule. Birth, survival and death are typed views of
that transition.

### Owner separation rule

The controlling correction is:

`1i / jk ≠ Q°`

`Q°` therefore remains a separate reference and is neither identified with nor
derived from the grouped W-plate roles `1, i, j, k`. The slash is preserved as
Owner notation; it is not interpreted as arithmetic division until a separate
grammar defines it. The earlier string `g24 B€||⁄||` is superseded by this
clearer separation rule and receives no mathematical or executable meaning.

## Open decisions

- Does Water / Land / Sky replace the present Chemistry / LIFE / Physics
  labels, or is it a higher-level environmental view over them?
- Is Space / `+1` / Tower one object, three roles of one reference, or a visual
  grouping that must remain separated in the data model?
- What exactly is the “pump”: state advance, visual transport, channel
  selection or another bounded interaction?
- How is the Lantern mechanically hung between or above the two moving rails?
- Do Pink and Green each form a complete loop, or do their two noses exchange
  the carried object at a defined joint?
- Are the several books independent volumes, ordered levels of one atlas, or
  replaceable decks carried in the same bucket?
- Which port roles, if any, belong to a later control surface rather than the
  core bucket geometry?
- Are W plates fixed display levels while the transition rail moves through
  them, or are they replaceable decks carried by the bucket?
- Which exact joint binds the lower LIFE return to the next world/deck without
  introducing feedback into the native cellular automaton?
- Does the selected lens remain stationary independently of the fixed Tower?
- Which record proves Return without implying feedback between native worlds?

## Boundary

This brief parks a Builder direction. It does not authorize research,
implementation, publication or deployment; does not identify anode/cathode
physics; and does not claim that Water, Land, Sky, Space, Tower, Bench,
Assembly Map or the current Game of Worlds are already one integrated system.
