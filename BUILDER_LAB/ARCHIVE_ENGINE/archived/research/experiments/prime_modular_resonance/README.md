## 🔷 Core Example (mod 7)

We define:

$$
r_n = p_n \bmod 7
$$

$$
\theta_n = \frac{2\pi}{7} r_n
$$

$$
x_n = \cos(\theta_n), \quad y_n = \sin(\theta_n)
$$

→ Discrete residues embedded into continuous phase space
