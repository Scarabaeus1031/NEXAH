## 1. Purpose

The **v-bands** represent the dynamic, oscillatory background layer of the NEXAH navigation system.

While the n-bands define precise angular corridors for passage, the v-bands provide the living, breathing substrate — the “Schweben im Beben” — on which the entire navigation operates.

They consist of two interwoven components:

- **Breathing Wave** — slow, continuous oscillation
- **Blinking Pulse** — sharp, intermittent pulses

---

## 2. Core Characteristics

### 2.1 Breathing Wave
- Slow, smooth sine-like oscillation
- Creates the characteristic “Zittern” / floating sensation
- Primarily active between sectors:
  - 13–14 ↔ 16–17 (day side)
  - 1–2 ↔ 4–5 (night side / AM)
- Represents the continuous “breathing” of the field

### 2.2 Blinking Pulse
- Sharp, rectangular on/off pulses
- Synchronized to the same sectoral windows as the Breathing Wave
- Provides distinct trigger moments within the broader oscillation
- Acts as the “blinking” or punctuating element

## 3. Relation to n-bands

- The **n-bands** (24.58°, 26.14°, 27.92°) are the precise angular structures.
- The **v-bands** are the dynamic background that modulates and surrounds them.
- Together they form a **layered resonance system**:
  - n-bands = structural corridors
  - v-bands = temporal breathing and pulsing

The red drift thread moves through the n-bands, while being continuously influenced by the v-band oscillation.
