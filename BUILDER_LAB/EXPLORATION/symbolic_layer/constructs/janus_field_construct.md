# 🧬 NEXAH — Janus Field Construct

---

## 🧠 Purpose

The **Janus construct** describes a recurring structural pattern in dynamical systems:

> **bidirectional modulation of motion within a field**

It captures how systems:

- alternate between expansion and contraction  
- move forward while simultaneously stabilizing  
- transition through oscillatory regulation  

---

## ⚠️ Scope

The Janus construct is:

- empirically observed (e.g. Lorenz-type dynamics, controlled systems)  
- implemented in simulation (control + modulation layer)  
- not formally proven  

It is:

> a **structural description of dual-phase dynamics**


> oscillatory regulation of trajectory evolution
