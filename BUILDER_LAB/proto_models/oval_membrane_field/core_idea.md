# Core Idea — Oval Membrane Field

---

## 🧠 Central Hypothesis

The transition space in NEXAH is not a simple boundary.

It is a **layered oval field** composed of:

- an **inner oval**
- an **outer oval**
- a structured **membrane field** in between

Thus, the geometry of transition is not linear, not flat, and not infinitely thin.

It is a **nested shell structure**.

---

## 🔁 Oval in Oval

The basic form is not a single oval.

It is:

> **oval within oval**

This means that transition space is generated through the interaction of two non-identical curved structures.

The result is not static containment, but:

- displacement
- pressure
- resonance
- local asymmetry
- pulsation

---

## 🧩 The Membrane Field

The space between inner and outer oval is not empty.

It is an active **membrane field**.

This membrane field is the zone in which:

- exchange becomes possible
- structural pressure accumulates
- transfer pathways emerge
- comparison between layers occurs

Thus:

> transition is not located on the shell  
> but within the membrane between shells

---

## 🥚 Shell Analogy

A useful model is the layered structure of an egg:

- inner core
- transfer medium
- outer shell

In this analogy:

- the **inner region** corresponds to the active root-space
- the **membrane field** corresponds to the exchange layer
- the **outer shell** corresponds to the containing counter-structure

The important point is:

> inner and outer do not interact directly  
> but through an intermediate structured zone

---

## 🔬 Internal Layering of the Membrane

The membrane itself is not one single layer.

It can be interpreted as having at least three sub-layers:

1. **inner membrane face**  
2. **middle transition layer**  
3. **outer membrane face**

This means the membrane is not a line, but a spatially extended structure.

It has:

- thickness
- gradation
- internal differentiation

---

## ⚡ Pulsation

The nested oval structure creates pulsation.

This happens because inner and outer oval are not perfectly aligned.

They are:

- shifted
- stretched
- partially mismatched
- dynamically coupled

As a result, the membrane field is never neutral.

It periodically generates:

- contraction
- expansion
- local pressure increase
- release dynamics

Thus:

> pulsation is a geometric effect of nested shells under displacement

---

## 🔻 Local Cut and Difference

The membrane field is also the place where **difference** enters the system.

A cut does not occur outside the geometry.

It appears inside the membrane structure when:

- one layer can no longer remain aligned with another
- comparison becomes active
- asymmetry is introduced
- a branch must be selected

This makes the membrane the site of:

- transition
- selection
- instability
- directional redefinition

---

## 🔁 Relation to Root Shrinking

Root shrinking is interpreted here as a membrane-mediated process.

The inner root region does not simply collapse inward by itself.

It changes through exchange across the membrane field.

This means:

- the core contracts
- the membrane redistributes
- the outer field responds

Thus:

> shrinking is not isolated loss  
> but structured transfer across layered space

---

## 🌧️ Exchange Channels

The fine “rain-like” or vein-like structures visible in some NEXAH plots are interpreted here as:

- membrane channels
- transfer paths
- fine structural veins
- micro-couplings between layers

These are not treated as noise.

They may represent the local pathways through which:

- pressure is redistributed
- information is transferred
- energy is exchanged
- coherence is modulated

---

## 🔋 Polarity Structure

The oval membrane field also supports a polarity interpretation.

The inner and outer structures can act like opposite poles.

The membrane then becomes the transfer medium between them.

This gives a structure similar to:

- anode / cathode
- source / sink
- compression / release
- inward / outward charge gradient

Thus:

> the membrane field is activated by potential difference

---

## 🧠 Geometric Meaning

The oval membrane field provides the **spatial carrier** for:

- field split
- coherence modulation
- temporal knot formation
- local cuts
- pulsation
- root shrinking

It is the place where transition becomes spatially real.
