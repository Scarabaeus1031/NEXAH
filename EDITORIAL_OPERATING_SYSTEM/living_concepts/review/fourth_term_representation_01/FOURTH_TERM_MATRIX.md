## Model evaluation

| Model | Evidence for | Evidence against | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| M0 — no recurrence | cardinality and roles vary strongly | external/situated reference relations recur across several independent visuals and current Operators | reject as too strong |
| M1 — four like components | occasional four-part lists or quadrants | candidate fourth terms are differently typed; most cases do not have four components | reject |
| M2 — three internal + external reference | Pearl and Torus have three internal bands; several other cases have external observers | no reviewed case combines both conditions cleanly; Pearl's observer is internal; Torus has multiple external relations; other external-observer cases have two or six internal structures | not supported as a common geometry |
| M3 — orthogonal `j` axis | historical quadrature language and 90° experimental injections | no reviewed declaration of `j²=-1`; current JANUS diagnostic suppresses 90° alignment; no stable semantic link | reject |
| M4 — conjugate `±j` orientations | dual, mirror, counterrotation, and JANUS motifs | no explicit `+j/-j` pair, conjugation rule, or invariant in the bounded corpus | reject |
| M5 — multiple distinct representation models | every primary case; current representation and Operator reviews | does not provide a single elegant cardinal formula | accept |

## Term-specific decisions

### `+1`

Not supported as a general fourth component. The additional role is usually
not of the same type as the internal regions.

### `Q(t)`

Unavailable for the proposed external-reference role without redefinition.
Current Research already uses it for a phase partition:

```math
Q(t) = \mathcal Q(\phi(t), \dot\phi(t))
```

### `+j` and `-j`

Not admitted. A visual right/left, inside/outside, mirror, or counterrotation
pair is insufficient. Admission would require all of:

1. a declared complex carrier space;
2. `j² = -1` or an equivalent multiplication rule;
3. a consistent sign convention for quarter-turn direction;
4. an explicit conjugation or inverse rule;
5. a preserved quantity or relation;
6. recurrence beyond one historical experiment family.

None is currently demonstrated together.

### Observer / reference / handle

This is the strongest recurring differently typed role, but its placement and
function vary. Sometimes the observer is the center, sometimes outside,
sometimes between projections, and sometimes a selection handle. The current
Registry already represents this plural structure through Observer,
Projection, Orientation, Scale, Relation, and Boundary. It does not require a
new fourth-term identity.

## Flip and the Iota breachpoint

The following editorial sequence is plausible:

```text
declared frame
→ boundary pressure
→ orientation reversal / Flip
→ structural gap
→ new representation
```

But the reviewed sources do not contain this as one declared chain.

- `Flip` belongs to the Work-level Move taxonomy;
- `Transition Gap` declares a necessary structural jump;
- historical scripts inject 90°/flip behavior by construction;
- current Research uses Iota in several unrelated meanings;
- no accepted source establishes `Flip → Iota Breachpoint`.

Classification: `editorial_bridge · unsupported_as_mechanism`.
