# Fourth-Term Representation Review 01 — Review Protocol

**Status:** completed bounded review · non-canonical  
**Review date:** 2026-07-19  
**Mode:** observational reconstruction with active counter-analysis

## Research question

Does NEXAH repeatedly use a representation geometry consisting of three
internal registers and one differently typed fourth term? If it does, is that
term a fourth component, an external reference, an observer, a time-dependent
quantity, an orthogonal `+j` or `-j` term, or something else?

The review does not assume that a fourth-term geometry exists.

## Competing models

| Model | Working hypothesis | Admission requirement |
|---|---|---|
| M0 | no recurring pattern | apparent recurrence dissolves under source-level inspection |
| M1 | four like components | four terms have the same role, dimensional type, and representational status |
| M2 | three internal registers plus one external reference | three internal roles recur and one distinctly external reference is explicitly represented |
| M3 | three registers plus an orthogonal `j` axis | a complex structure is declared and `j² = -1` or an equivalent quarter-turn operation is consistently defined |
| M4 | three registers plus conjugate `±j` orientations | both orientations form a declared conjugate pair with a preserved invariant |
| M5 | multiple distinct representation models | related observer, boundary, projection, and transition motifs recur without stable cardinality or a common fourth-term semantics |

## Evidence classes

1. **Canonical repository evidence** — Registry records and accepted
   Architecture decisions. These define current identities and boundaries, not
   the truth of the hypothesis.
2. **Current Research and bounded review evidence** — current reports,
   experiments, and completed non-canonical reviews with explicit limits.
3. **Work-level visual evidence** — authored Library visuals and descriptions.
   These support editorial usage, not scientific validity.
4. **Historical or Experimental evidence** — exploratory scripts, archived
   logs, and symbolic models. These establish lineage only.
5. **Session-supplied visual evidence** — images supplied for this review but
   not versioned inside the repository. Their SHA-256 values preserve local
   identity; they are not canonical repository sources.

No positive conclusion may be based only on visual resemblance.

## Review procedure

1. Describe every case without using `3+1`, `j`, or `breachpoint`.
2. Count only source-declared roles; do not derive roles from visual layout.
3. Determine whether terms are internal, external, static, time-dependent,
   homogeneous, or differently typed.
4. Test whether the fourth term changes the representation or merely labels it.
5. Search explicitly for `j² = -1`, complex multiplication, quarter-turn
   semantics, conjugation, phase direction, and an invariant.
6. Compare the Work-level Move taxonomy with the controlled Registry without
   merging them.
7. Search for two-, five-, seven-, ten-, thirteen-, and seventeen-part
   counterexamples.
8. Assign one final disposition from the five allowed outcomes.

## Exclusions

The review creates no Concept, Operator, identity, graph edge, Kernel behavior,
Architecture component, mathematical formalism, or Are.na mutation. It does
not validate cosmology, numerology, monument alignment, or a universal
transition mechanism.
