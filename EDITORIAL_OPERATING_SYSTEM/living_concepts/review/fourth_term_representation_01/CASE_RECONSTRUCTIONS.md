# Fourth-Term Representation Review 01 — Case Reconstructions

Each neutral reconstruction precedes interpretation. The labels `3+1`, `j`,
and `breachpoint` are intentionally absent from the neutral descriptions.

## C1 — The Pearl & The Diamond

### Neutral reconstruction

The visual declares three nested rings: Earth as pearl/center, Juno as
shell/mediator, and Jupiter as field/context. A second panel names a
center–observer–observed relation. A five-leaf horseshoe describes flow,
exchange, balance, release, and return. The observer is associated with the
center rather than drawn as a separate external point.

### Assessment

This is strong evidence for **three differentiated internal roles**. It is not
evidence for a distinct fourth term: the observer is already assigned inside
the three-ring model, while the five-leaf construction introduces another
cardinality. Classification: `three_internal_roles_without_fourth_term`.

## C2 — Projection — Two Worlds, Many Views

### Neutral reconstruction

The visual places two projection landscapes on opposing sides, a central
observer outside and between them, seven named bridges, several auxiliary
maps, and a return path. The two landscapes present inside/outside projections
of a shared structure but are not reduced to one view.

### Assessment

The observer is differently typed, but the internal structure is two
projections plus seven bridges, not three registers. This supports situated
projection and JANUS-style complementary views, not a stable fourth-term
geometry. Classification: `alternative_projection_model`.

## C3 — The Torus Universe

### Neutral reconstruction

The central torus is divided into outer, middle, and inner shells. It also has
north and south star gates, east and west entry/exit gates, major and minor
flows, and a pumping core. Additional panels use four-way stacking, a
three-part rendering fraction, and an observer overlay.

### Assessment

Three shells recur clearly, but there is no single fourth element: gates,
flows, core, observer, and rendering categories perform different functions.
The visual is therefore evidence for nested register geometry plus multiple
interfaces, not one differently typed term. Classification:
`three_registers_multiple_external_relations`.

## C4 — Transition Gap & Root Thread

### Neutral reconstruction

Two lateral loops cross near a central thread. The composition adds a
restricted axis, a switching node, orientation drift, a transition gap, a
recursive return, and a four-stage route to return. The page explicitly says
that some transitions are continuous while others require a structural jump.

### Assessment

This is the strongest Work-level source for a discontinuous transition motif.
Its structure is not three internal terms plus one external term. The gap is a
declared discontinuity, while the switch and root thread are separate roles.
It does not name `Flip` or an imaginary direction. Classification:
`transition_gap_model`.

## C5 — Universal Operator Map

### Neutral reconstruction

Two projection planes face one another. An observer occupies an external
central position, seven bridges connect the planes, and surrounding panels
present several independent geometries: a 17-part wheel, a seven-bridge path,
a five-level lock gate, a 20×20 grid, a quaternion construction, and a final
closure radial field.

### Assessment

The observer is clearly outside the two main planes, but no three-register
interior exists. The map strongly supports **external observation of plural
representations**, and equally strongly contradicts fixed cardinality.
Classification: `external_observer_plural_model`.

## C6 — Mandelbrot Field Architecture

### Neutral reconstruction

The visual declares a global field container with six indexed layers, an
Euler lattice with multiple directions, an Iota ring, a decision cone, tube
routing, and an observer handle. The observer selects orientation and a
projection creates a visible slice.

### Assessment

This is direct evidence for an observer/reference term that is differently
typed from the represented field. It is not a three-register model: the main
field declares six layers and several additional structures. Classification:
`external_observer_multilayer_model`.

## C7 — The Moves / Operator Library / Language of Moves

### Neutral reconstruction

The three supplied Library visuals present Move repertoires rather than a
single state geometry:

- one board lists ten fundamental moves;
- another lists a larger core repertoire and twelve nature-based examples;
- another presents nine principal moves, a five-state orientation wheel, and
  a composed stack.

`Flip` is described locally as inversion, orientation reversal, or an
inside/outside exchange. `Mirror` is reflection or duality. `Rotate` changes a
reference. `Twist`, `Fold`, `Project`, `Return`, and `Resonate` have different
transformational roles.

### Assessment

The Move taxonomy is evidence that NEXAH repeatedly explains change through
transformations. It does not define a four-term geometry or an ordered route
from `Flip` to an Iota event. The existing Registry contains seventeen
controlled Operators and does not contain controlled Operators named Flip,
Mirror, Twist, Rotate, Counterrotate, or Return. These Work-level Moves must
therefore remain separate from Registry semantics. Classification:
`move_repertoire_not_state_geometry`.

## C8 — Current Research uses of Q(t)

### Neutral reconstruction

`RESEARCH/CORE_CONCEPTS/structure_quantities.md` and `equations.md` define:

```math
Q(t) = \mathcal Q(\phi(t), \dot\phi(t))
```

as a recursive phase partition with quadrants for expansion, compression,
memory, and transition.

### Assessment

The current repository does not define this `Q(t)` as an external observer or
moving reference point. Reusing it for that role would collide with an
existing meaning. Classification: `internal_phase_partition`.

## C9 — Historical orthogonal and flip experiments

### Neutral reconstruction

Historical logs describe a middle coupling layer as a quadrature state, and
several exploratory scripts inject orthogonal pushes or hard-coded 90° flip
terms. One script explicitly contains `TODO: echte trajectory einsetzen`;
another adds a flip term by angle threshold. JANUS Building Log 02 reports a
90° alignment score of zero in its current diagnostic and says pure
orthogonal crossing is not preferred.

### Assessment

These sources establish a historical experimental interest in quadrature,
orthogonal intervention, and flips. They do not define `j² = -1`, do not
establish a conjugate `±j` pair, and do not connect a validated flip to an
Iota breachpoint. The JANUS result is counterevidence to a universal
orthogonal gate. Classification: `historical_experimental_lineage_only`.

## Cross-case recurrence

The stable recurrence is not numerical cardinality. It is a distinction
between:

```text
bounded representation(s)
        ↕
situated observer, reference, projection, or interface
```

The number of internal regions varies from two to many, and the differently
typed term may be internal, external, distributed, or absent.
