# Operator Recurrence Review 02 — Counterexamples

## CE1 — Artifact count overstates independence

Three general-grammar visuals repeat the same palette and examples; two living
library visuals repeat the same natural exemplars. Counting seven files as
seven independent confirmations would inflate recurrence.

## CE2 — Different headline inventories

The supplied palettes contain different numbers and combinations of functions.
No stable alphabet size emerges.

## CE3 — Complete explanations omit named fundamentals

The Butterfly Work functions without explicit separation, reversal, folding,
or projection. The paired synthesis omits separation. Several core palettes
omit return. These absences contradict universal necessity.

## CE4 — Scale and layering remain confined

Scale change and layering are headline functions in one artifact family but do
not recur independently with equivalent roles.

## CE5 — Reflection is not reversal

The corpus separately depicts counterpart reflection, front/back reversal,
inside/outside exchange, and role exchange. Treating them as one Move would
erase authored differences.

## CE6 — Connection is not always co-orientation

A static link, a bridge, interweaving paths, and reciprocal adaptation all
create relations, but only some require mutual change.

## CE7 — Resonance has incompatible scopes

The Works use alignment language for waves, patterns, collective coordination,
attention, and social interaction. Functional recurrence is editorially clear;
one mechanism is not.

## CE8 — Return and release diverge

Return can preserve or close a cycle. Release can separate participants and
begin another cycle. Their adjacency does not establish equivalence.

## CE9 — Emergence changes grammatical role

Some visuals present emergence as a selected action; others show it as the
outcome of composition or interaction. Its status as a Move is uncertain.

## CE10 — Ordered lists do not produce fixed syntax

Numbered palettes suggest order, but example stacks omit, reorder, and repeat
functions. No invalid sequence is declared.

## CE11 — Similar examples do not prove a common mechanism

Möbius surfaces, proteins, waves, insects, social interaction, maps, and
electronics are placed under adjacent functions. The visuals demonstrate
editorial portability, not scientific identity.

## CE12 — Work language does not create authority

Calling an action an operator inside a Work does not create a controlled
identity, formal contract, or canonical term. This review makes no such
promotion.

## Negative result

The counterexamples reject a universal language and a fixed recurring grammar.
They do not erase the independently recurring function families or the
meta-function of selective composition.
