## Support decisions

### Strongly supported

- situated observation;
- relation formation;
- directional reference change;
- reflection/counterpart production;
- broad sidedness and form transformation;
- representation change;
- alignment/coherence;
- return/cyclic continuity;
- selection and composition of functions.

### Locally supported

- distinction/separation;
- reciprocal adaptation and co-orientation;
- memory/trace retention;
- scale change.

### Uncertain

- universal counterbalance;
- layer/stack as one function;
- threshold/release as one function;
- emergence as an operation rather than an outcome.

### Contradicted

- one fixed number of Moves;
- one mandatory order;
- necessity of Separate, Flip, Fold, Project, or Return in every Work;
- equivalence of adjacent labels;
- one geometry or mechanism underlying all examples.
