## Required versus optional

No Move is demonstrated as universally required. `Observe`, `Rotate`, `Mirror`,
`Twist`, and `Resonate` occur in every visual, but several appearances are in
example stacks rather than mandatory procedures. The visuals repeatedly state
or demonstrate that Moves can be selected and composed. Recurrence therefore
supports availability, not necessity.

## Terms not merged

- `Observe` and `Sense` are functionally adjacent but retain their labels.
- `Mirror` and `Flip` are not merged: reflection/duality differs from reversal.
- `Rotate` and `Counterrotate` are not merged: one changes reference; the other
  is explicitly paired as counter-action.
- `Return`, `Release`, and `Remember` are not merged.
- `Connect`, `Bridge`, `Pair`, and `Co-orient` form a relation family but remain
  distinct operations.
- `Resonate`, `Align`, and `Coherence` are not assumed to be synonyms.
