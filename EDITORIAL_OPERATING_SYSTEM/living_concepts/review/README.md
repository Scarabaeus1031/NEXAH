# NEXAH Review Toolbox

This directory contains non-canonical review instruments used to inspect
authored Works, compare independent reconstructions, preserve counterexamples,
and synthesize bounded findings without silently creating authority.

The sequence below describes the growth of the review capability. It is **not**
an ontology of NEXAH, a maturity ladder, a universal research process, or a
canonical architecture. Each review owns only the question and evidence stated
in its report.

## Current toolbox sequence

```text
RECURRENCE
    ↓
INDEPENDENCE
    ↓
PROCESS
    ↓
MODEL SPACE
    ↓
REPRESENTATION
```

The arrows show how later review questions became possible. They do not make
later reviews more authoritative than earlier ones.

## Review type and result status

All five reports are completed review artifacts. Their findings have different
strengths:

- Reviews 01 and 02 establish and replicate a bounded Work-level recurrence
  result.
- Orientation Process Review preserves multiple coexisting models rather than
  selecting one.
- Orientation Model Space establishes a **preliminary** descriptive atlas, not
  a stable model architecture.
- Orientation Territories Review reports **moderate** support and retains a
  non-territorial counterexample.

`completed review` therefore does not mean `canonical`, `validated
externally`, or `methodologically final`.

## Shared safeguards

The toolbox repeatedly uses:

- neutral reconstruction before name comparison;
- editorial-dependence correction;
- explicit support classes;
- active counterexample search;
- delayed comparison with earlier reviews;
- evidence and authority boundaries;
- preservation of negative and uncertain findings.

These safeguards recur across the sequence, but this index does not turn them
into a canonical method or require every future review to use one fixed form.

## Authority boundary

The indexed reviews are descriptive and non-canonical. They create no Registry
entries, controlled Operators, Meta-functions, Living Concepts, OLS semantics,
Kernel behavior, Architecture, Overlay authority, or Are.na changes.

Work-level language remains separate from Registry terminology. Synthesis does
not upgrade evidence. A visual overview, if later admitted, will summarize this
toolbox only and will not define it.
