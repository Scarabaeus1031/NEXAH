# Discovery Engine Lineage Review

**Status:** historical evidence review · not current production architecture

## Scope

Reviewed sources include the historical
`EXPERIMENTAL/BUILDER_LAB/DISCOVERY_ENGINE/` series and the archived Theory
Evolution, Hypothesis Generator, and Meta Discovery modules referenced by the
Phase X brief.

The current Discovery Engine README explicitly describes the module as an
exploration phase and legacy origin layer. The later
`DISCOVERY_OBSERVATIONS.md` further narrows V4–V22 to a historical Lorenz
experiment series whose outputs motivate hypotheses rather than general laws.

## Archived theory machinery

The archived Theory Evolution Engine randomly samples simple equation
candidates and ranks their fit to stored resilience results. The Meta Discovery
Engine estimates high-scoring architecture regions and generates additional
random graphs near them. These are heuristic search prototypes.

They may contribute:

- computational method lineage;
- historical terminology;
- hypotheses to reproduce;
- examples of why claim and provenance boundaries are necessary.

They do not contribute canonical theory or Concept meaning.

## Corrected overclaims

| Historical language | X0 decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| autonomous architecture discovery system | deprecated_claim | Search and scoring were defined by authored heuristics and fixtures |
| automatic discovery of stable architectures | deprecated_claim | Results depend on the resilience score, graph generator, and sampled domain |
| universal architecture / universal law | deprecated_claim | No general cross-domain validation establishes universality |
| random equation generation as theory evolution | deprecated_claim | Candidate fitting is not theoretical explanation |
| spectral association as causal stability law | deprecated_claim | Association within a selected representation does not establish causality |
| self-improving canonical knowledge | rejected_production_principle | Violates human editorial authority and proposal isolation |

## Open research questions

1. Are V16–V22 structures robust across seeds, integrators, neighborhood sizes,
   estimators, and surrogate controls?
2. Which historical transition representations survive the current typed and
   evidence-bound pipelines?
3. Can a narrowly scoped hypothesis generator produce auditable proposals
   without implying automatic meaning or theory?
4. Which historical terms were later retained with changed definitions in the
   Field, Transition, JANUS, or Orientation layers?

## Governance conclusion

The Discovery Engine is valuable as intellectual lineage and a warning against
claim inflation. Phase X may cite its observations, hypotheses, open questions,
and terminology. It must not reactivate autonomous canonicalization,
universal-law claims, random equations as theory, or correlation as
explanation.
