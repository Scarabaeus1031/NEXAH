# Orientation Model Space 01 — Common Structure

## Reading rule

These components are recurring descriptive features, not required stages. A
component may overlap another, occur continuously, remain implicit, or be
absent from a local model.

## Strong

### Initial configuration

Every reviewed Work family presents a distinguishable starting configuration:
an available field, a referenced arrangement, an actor-environment relation,
or participants already coupled in a shared field.

**Boundary:** this is not universally a discrete act of perception.

### Encounter or situated attention

Encounter, detection, sensing, witnessing, or an established view occurs
explicitly or implicitly across the independent families. Recurrence Review 02
independently found situated attention in all four independence-corrected
units.

**Boundary:** passive observation, active sensing, and ongoing encounter are
adjacent but not equivalent.

### Relation

All families create, traverse, alter, or maintain relations. Relation formation
also recurs strongly in both recurrence reviews.

**Boundary:** links, bridges, reciprocal coupling, reflection, and
interweaving do not share one demonstrated mechanism.

### Transformation or reorganization

All families show a change between an initial and later configuration. The
change may concern frame, counterpart, sidedness, form, representation,
coherence, or relation.

**Boundary:** no single geometry or kind of action underlies all changes.

### Changed continuation

Every family reaches or remains within a changed condition. The result may be
integration, alignment, response, shared pattern, release, return, or another
encounter.

**Boundary:** a changed condition is not necessarily an endpoint or a physical
action.

### Composition

Every independent unit selects or presents subsets, stacks, cycles, or
sequences of transformations. Both recurrence reviews support composition;
the process review confirms it while qualifying discrete selection.

**Boundary:** composition has no fixed syntax, mandatory alphabet, or shared
formal semantics.

## Moderate

### Differentiation

Several families distinguish alternatives, participants, sides, or parts.
Prior separation is local rather than universal.

### Possibility

Finite palettes, implied alternatives, and open trajectory fields recur, but
possibility is represented differently in each family.

### Comparison

Paired structures, reciprocal response, and reveal-more questions support
comparison as a recurring tendency. A discrete comparison step is not stable.

### Selection

Selection is explicit in some editorial processes and absent as a separable
event in continuous coupling. It is recurring but non-universal.

### Feedback

Feedback is explicit in relational and several living examples, local or
implied elsewhere. No shared feedback rule is established.

### Return or renewed encounter

Return, re-entry, refinement, release, and renewed encounter recur across
families, but not in every path.

## Local

### Direct response

Some examples move from detection to response without a visible middle stage.
This is local and may reflect compression or omission.

### Reciprocal adaptation

Mutual or environmental adjustment is coherent in the relational and Living
Library families but is not a corpus-wide base operation.

### Open continuation

An unresolved field of trajectories is explicit in one family and possible in
some palettes. It is not general.

### Trace or memory

Trails, retention, memory, and remembered states appear locally. Their role as
state, capacity, outcome, or process is unstable.

## Uncertain

### Evaluation

Testing, refining, or asking which change reveals more can imply evaluation,
but explicit criteria are missing and one family lacks a discrete evaluation
stage.

### Action

The Works show conceptual, representational, relational, spatial, editorial,
and metaphorically physical changes. No shared action category is supported.

### Closure

Some paths close or return; others stop or remain open. No common closure
condition exists.

### Emergence as process

Emergence alternates between an authored Move and an outcome of other changes.
Its process role is not stable.

## Smallest recurring structure

The strongest shared structure is deliberately minimal:

```text
initial configuration
        ↕
situated relation
        ↕
transformation / reorganization
        ↕
changed continuation
```

Composition often organizes the transformations. Feedback, return, selection,
openness, and coupling modify this structure but are not mandatory components.
The bidirectional marks preserve the finding that ordering may be editorial,
logical, reciprocal, or temporal rather than uniformly causal.
