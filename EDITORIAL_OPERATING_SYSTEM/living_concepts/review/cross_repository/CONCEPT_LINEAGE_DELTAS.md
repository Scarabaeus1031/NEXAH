# Concept Lineage Deltas

**Phase:** X0.5 · non-canonical review

This document records what the two historical repositories change—and do not
change—about the five recommended X1 dossiers.

## JANUS

**New evidence:** A Janus gatekeeper formulation associates duality with a
passage point and polarity transfer. A separate Geometria Nova visual series
uses Janus for dual-field overlays, quaternionic projection, rotation, and
vertical pulse structures.

**Dossier consequence:** X1 should distinguish at least three scopes:

1. mythic/editorial archetype;
2. historical visual-geometric family;
3. current controlled Operator and Research usage.

**Unchanged boundary:** Similar duality is not enough to merge these scopes.
The current Operator definition remains authoritative for Operator use.

## Aperture

**New evidence:** The archive uses field aperture in a lunar phase valve
hypothesis involving neutrino inflow and resonant energetic systems.

**Dossier consequence:** The timeline should show this as a historical,
speculative formulation before current Research formalization and pedagogical
Library use.

**Unchanged boundary:** The passage contains no support for the physical claim.
It cannot define or validate present Aperture Geometry.

## Vessel

**New evidence:** Historical sources use vessel metaphorically for geometric
forms and literally for experimental containers.

**Dossier consequence:** Add an explicit lexical exclusion rule so ordinary
container and metaphorical uses do not become Concept Occurrences.

**Unchanged boundary:** No earlier independent Vessel Concept definition was
found. The dossier remains grounded in current Core Concepts Research.

## Resonance

**New evidence:** Resonance is pervasive in both repositories. The historical
glossary defines several narrower forms, while other files make much broader
cross-scale, physical, cosmological, or symbolic assertions. Scarabaeus v1.0
shows that Resonance was already an organizing project theme and experimental
motivation.

**Dossier consequence:** A single linear definition is not viable. X1 should
separate at least:

- editorial or metaphorical resonance;
- mathematical or structural recurrence;
- physical resonance in its established technical sense;
- named historical NEXAH models;
- unsupported universalizing claims.

Definitions should be scoped and versioned. Historical models should remain
visible without being silently inherited.

**Unchanged boundary:** Frequency does not imply Concept coherence. Repository
recurrence is not validation, and the two repositories are project lineage,
not independent replication.

## Living Equation

**New evidence:** No exact tracked-text match was found in either historical
repository at the pinned commits.

**Dossier consequence:** Record the negative search result and keep the dossier
focused on whether a current Work title has acquired an independently defined
Concept meaning.

**Unchanged boundary:** Absence in two repositories does not establish the
first origin of the phrase and does not create a Concept.

## Deferred terms

### Stack

Thirty-four historical matched lines were found in NEXAH-CODEX, but the sampled
uses are compounds or technical descriptions such as frequency stack, proof
stack, ring stack, and software-related Substack references. X0's decision not
to promote Stack to X1 remains justified.

### Observer, Transition, Field, Bridge, Fold, and Scale

These terms have substantial historical depth, but the archive mixes ordinary,
symbolic, editorial, mathematical, and physical meanings. They should enter a
later scoped review only when a concrete reader question requires them. High
frequency is a reason for stricter boundaries, not faster identity allocation.

## X1 dossier rule added by X0.5

Every dossier should include a compact provenance table with separate columns
for:

- current controlled or Research meaning;
- historical formulation;
- relationship between them;
- claim-support status;
- editorial decision still required.

Historical text may be quoted or summarized as evidence of lineage. It may not
be presented as the current definition unless a human editor explicitly adopts
it through the later Concept governance process.
