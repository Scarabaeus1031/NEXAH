# Atlas Representations

## Admission rule

This review admits completed descriptive atlas boards only. Four boards were
inspected. Because the Solar–Lunar–Carbon Evidence Map and Museum Edition are
two publication layers of one reviewed space, the four boards represent three
independent reconstruction families.

| Board | Underlying reconstruction | Dominant representation | Secondary forms | Territorial? | Independence note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Planetary Orientation source board | Planetary Chemistry Atlas 01 | hybrid layered architecture | matrix, directed flow, comparison table | no, not dominant | independent reconstruction |
| Solar–Lunar–Carbon Evidence Map | Solar–Lunar–Carbon Orientation Space 01 | territorial evidence map | network, pathways, evidence legend | yes | independent reconstruction |
| Solar–Lunar–Carbon Museum Edition | same reviewed space as the Evidence Map | territorial landscape | continents, island, frontier, pathways | yes | derivative publication layer; not an independent confirmation |
| Orientation Model Space 01 | Orientation Process synthesis | topological territorial landscape | overlap map, process-family network | yes | independent reconstruction |

## Neutral reconstructions

### Planetary Orientation

The board arranges elements, molecules, planetary atmospheres, and observable
properties in successive vertical bands. Connection lines cross bands, while
comparison tables and process panels provide lateral qualification. The
reviewed study explicitly rejects reading this as a single chemistry-to-
property causal chain. Its useful structure is layered comparison, not
geographic territory.

### Solar–Lunar–Carbon Evidence Map

The board separates a strong solar–biological region, planetary context, a
weakly connected lunar region, and an explicit Open Questions region. Spatial
distance, connection style, and regional placement jointly communicate
support and incompleteness. Territory is the dominant representation, but the
map still depends on graph-like edges.

### Solar–Lunar–Carbon Museum Edition

The same reviewed relations are rendered as continents, bridges, an isolated
island, and a large unknown frontier. The publication intensifies the
territorial metaphor but adds no evidence, relation, mechanism, or conclusion.
It is therefore evidence about visual translation, not recurrence.

### Orientation Model Space 01

Six process families occupy overlapping regions around relation,
transformation, and continuation. The map rejects hierarchy and a universal
sequence. Its regions are descriptive neighborhoods with variable overlap and
uncertain boundaries rather than mathematical state-space basins.

## Exclusions

- The Photosynthesis Orientation Neighborhood is explicitly the beginning of
  a possible Atlas, not an existing Atlas.
- Draft boards, proposals, unfinished whiteboards, and retained historical
  imagery were not admitted.
- Titles containing *Map* or *Atlas* were not sufficient for admission.
- Textual neighborhoods and scientific source diagrams were not silently
  converted into atlas boards.

## Counted result

- Atlas boards reviewed: **4**
- Independent reconstruction families: **3**
- Territory-dominant independent families: **2**
- Non-territory-dominant independent families: **1**

The corpus therefore supports recurrence with an explicit counterexample. It
does not support a universal territorial form.
