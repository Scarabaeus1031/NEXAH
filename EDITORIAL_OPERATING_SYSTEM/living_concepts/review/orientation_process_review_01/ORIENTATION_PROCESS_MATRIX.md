## Initial condition

Every family begins from a distinguishable configuration: available field,
actor-environment relation, referenced configuration, or two participants in a
shared field. Calling all four “perception” would exceed the evidence.

## Intermediate process

Every family depicts changes between initial and later configurations. The
changes are structurally distinct in WF-A and the editorial build layer of
WF-B, visually chained in WF-C, and continuously coupled in WF-D. A recurring
intermediate process is supported; a fixed intermediate layer is not.

## Possibility space

Finite alternatives are explicit in WF-A and WF-B. WF-C implies alternatives
through a palette but continues one example chain. WF-D explicitly denies a
fixed path but does not enumerate discrete alternatives. Possibility is
recurring; its representation is not uniform.

## Selection

Selection is explicit in WF-A and the editorial construction panel of WF-B,
uncertain in WF-C, and absent as an isolatable event in WF-D. Selection is a
recurring local process, not a universal stage.

## Action and transition

All families show transition. They do not show one shared kind of action.
Changes may be representational, conceptual, relational, spatial, editorial,
or metaphorically physical.

## Feedback

Feedback is explicit in WF-D and multiple WF-B examples, local in WF-A, and
structurally implied in WF-C. Feedback recurs but is not governed by one rule.
