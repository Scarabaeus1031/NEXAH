# JANUS — Concept Dossier

**Phase:** X1 · reference dossier

**Status:** human review required · non-canonical

**Proposal key:** `concept:janus`

**Existing controlled identity:** `NX-OP-0016` · Janus Operator

## Editorial finding

JANUS is a strong broader-Concept candidate because the repository already
defines a complementary-perspective principle that appears in theory, visual
language, architecture, and research. It is not merely a recurring word.

The same name has nevertheless been used for three different things. The
accepted architecture separates them:

1. **JANUS** — a conceptual principle;
2. **Janus Bridge** — a planned translation between representations;
3. **Janus Directional Coherence Operator** — an experimental forward/backward
   local-flow analysis.

This dossier preserves that separation. It does not allocate another identity
or reinterpret the scientific Operator as the implementation of the principle.

## Proposed reader definition

> **JANUS is the principle that one situation may require complementary
> perspectives whose relation must be preserved rather than collapsed into a
> single view.**

This is a proposal definition for human review. It is broader than the current
controlled Operator definition and narrower than historical universalizing or
physical uses.

## Definition layers

| Layer | Formulation | Authority and scope |
|---|---|---|
| Controlled Operator | Couples opposed or complementary views, directions, or states at a shared interface. | Current authoritative Operator definition in `NX-OP-0016` |
| Architecture principle | One reality may require complementary perspectives; JANUS is not an algorithm. | Accepted Orientation Layer separation |
| Planned Bridge | Translation between representations of one situation, with domain, information loss, provenance, uncertainty, and failure behavior. | Accepted architectural concept; not implemented as JANUS itself |
| Scientific Operator | Compares forward and backward local-flow organization inside reconstructed trajectory geometry. | Semi-formal experimental Research; method-specific |
| Historical archetype | Two-faced passage point, gatekeeper, and polarity-transfer symbol. | Historical symbolic lineage only |
| Historical visual family | Dual-field overlays, quaternionic projections, rotational coupling, and pulse or stair structures. | Historical visual lineage only |

No layer silently supersedes another. The proposed reader definition describes
the conceptual principle, not the Bridge contract or scientific calculation.

## Identity boundary

### What JANUS is in this dossier

- a high-specificity NEXAH conceptual principle;
- a way to preserve complementary perspectives and their relation;
- an editorial and architectural source for more specific realizations;
- a possible broader profile around the controlled Janus Operator.

### What JANUS is not

- a generic synonym for duality, opposition, reversal, or symmetry;
- an automatic inverse relation;
- the Janus Bridge;
- the Directional Coherence calculation;
- proof that every system has two complementary views;
- a physical law, quantum theory, universal geometry, or causal mechanism;
- a license to merge mythic, visual, architectural, and scientific claims.

## Claim-support assessment

### Supported within current repository architecture

- The three JANUS identities are explicitly separated by an accepted ADR.
- `NX-OP-0016` provides a stable controlled Operator definition.
- The Orientation Layer defines the Bridge as planned and the scientific
  Operator as experimental.
- The research corpus contains reproducible scripts, outputs, working
  hypotheses, findings, and explicit limitations.

### Not established by this dossier

- independent replication of JANUS experiments;
- calibrated predictive performance;
- causal precedence of coherence changes;
- universality across dynamical systems;
- equivalence with Koopman, Perron–Frobenius, coherent-set, FTLE, or transition
  path methods;
- a physical interpretation of the broader JANUS principle;
- implementation or validation of the Janus Bridge.

X1 reviews documentary evidence. It does not rerun or independently validate
the complete experimental series.

## Reader explanation test

### Reader Mode

> JANUS helps you hold two complementary perspectives together without
> pretending that they are the same. In NEXAH it informs both a planned bridge
> between representations and an experimental method for comparing forward and
> backward flow—but those are separate applications of the principle.

### Explain Mode

The answer should disclose:

1. the current Operator definition;
2. the accepted three-way separation;
3. which source is architectural and which is experimental;
4. that the historical archetype and visual series are lineage, not authority;
5. that no separate Concept ID or canonical relation exists yet.

## Open questions

1. What minimum condition makes two perspectives complementary rather than
   merely different?
2. Must JANUS always involve a shared interface, or can the relation itself be
   indirect?
3. Is information loss essential to JANUS, or only to some Bridge operations?
4. When does a forward/backward comparison instantiate JANUS rather than only
   bidirectional analysis?
5. Which invariants would a future Janus Bridge need to preserve?
6. Should the broader Concept eventually have an identity separate from
   `NX-OP-0016`, or should a layered profile remain attached to the Operator?
7. Which historical visual formulations remain useful for human explanation?
8. Which Research findings survive independent baselines and domain tests?
