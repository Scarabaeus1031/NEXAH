# JANUS — Visual Evidence Review

**Phase:** X1 · bounded visual evidence pass  
**Reviewed:** 2026-07-16  
**Status:** human review required · non-canonical  
**Scope:** 15 selected public Are.na blocks and four local experimental visuals

## Purpose

This review addresses a known X0 limitation: concepts embedded in book pages,
atlases, posters, and plots were not discoverable from channel metadata alone.
It does not perform bulk image extraction and it does not treat text visible in
an image as validated evidence.

The review distinguishes three questions:

1. **Occurrence:** does the visual explicitly name or formulate JANUS?
2. **Editorial meaning:** what meaning does the visual assign to JANUS?
3. **Claim support:** does another repository artifact support the substantive
   claim within the stated scope?

An Are.na block is cited by channel ID, block ID, and observed public position.
Positions are snapshot evidence and may change.

## Main finding

The pages reveal not one hidden definition but a **family of JANUS usages**:

1. complementary perspective and inclusion/exclusion;
2. local forward/backward directional coherence;
3. transition, handover, crossing, and aperture geometry;
4. navigation or passage through a transition;
5. broad speculative synthesis involving prime, rope, and regime geometries.

The first two have clear documentary roots. The third is a related research
family. The fourth remains experimental. The fifth contains strong claims that
must not be inherited by the Concept dossier without separate validation.

## IEEE boundary

The repository contains two different IEEE lineages that must remain separate:

- **Legacy JANUS-labelled IEEE experiments** include IEEE-300 and IEEE-9241
  plots and scripts under the historical `stability_field_dynamics` branch.
- **Canonical IEEE Geometry V1** validates six descriptive geometry operators
  over frozen IEEE-9 and IEEE-14 benchmark campaigns. It does not name JANUS,
  does not declare a metric for cross-projection comparison, and explicitly
  limits its evidence to benchmark computation rather than operational-grid
  behavior, prediction, causal control, or real-world generalization.

`FIELD_NAVIGATION_VALIDATION/docs/atlas_operator_framework.md` supplies a third,
broader application-level formulation: `J : D_i ↔ D_j` reconstructs gates,
boundaries, crossings, bottlenecks, and admissible transitions between domains.
This is valuable documentary evidence for JANUS as a passage/interface concept,
but it is not the canonical IEEE Geometry V1 method and not a validation of the
broader Concept.

## Penta / Hexagonal human lead

The user identified the Penta/Hexagonal space as a possible missing root. The
review confirms a real experimental lineage, especially in
`log_iv_navigation.md`:

```text
Split → Overlap → Cut → Transfer → Branch
```

The log interprets a threshold cut as a transfer between phase sheets and
describes dual pentagons connected through a transitional hexagonal layer. That
language is clearly adjacent to JANUS themes: two states, a seam, passage, and
branching.

The visual evidence does not yet justify a JANUS relation:

- `v16_4_dual_pentagon.png` is titled as a dual-pentagon split but visually
  presents a near-circular wrapped trajectory with a short second branch;
- `v44_hexagon_loop_3d.png` is a 3D curve whose displayed geometry does not by
  itself establish a hexagonal loop;
- `v56_hexa_topology.png` explicitly shows a six-node topology and aperture-like
  marking, but contains no JANUS identity or forward/backward comparison;
- `penta_im_hepta_master_v4.8.png` is an exploratory symbolic composite with no
  explicit JANUS occurrence or accepted claim boundary.

**Decision:** retain this as `human_lead:penta-hexagonal-transition-space`.
Do not create a Concept, alias, or relation edge. A later review should first
identify the generative scripts, declared quantities, and reproducible relation
between the plotted geometry and the log's pentagon/hexagon terminology.

## Corrections to the JANUS dossier

This pass supports three additions:

1. a Work-level **double-perspective** layer from LIBRARYBOOK;
2. an application-level **domain-transition** layer from the Atlas Operator
   Framework;
3. a documented split between legacy JANUS-labelled IEEE experiments and the
   current canonical IEEE Geometry V1 validation.

It does **not** support merging prime residue geometry, rope geometry, IEEE
benchmarks, or Penta/Hexagonal experiments into JANUS.
